Documented national responses 6

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • The Paris Agreement

    Adopted by Parties to the UNFCCC (in force since 4 November 2016)

    Its aim is Holding the increase in the global average temperature to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels (Paris Agreement (opens in a new tab), Article 2.1(a)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Global warming · SWOT · Weaknesses · Off track on warming · SWOT · Opportunities · A defined path for emissions cuts

  • The COP28 energy goals

    Adopted by Parties to the Paris Agreement, decision 1/CMA.5 (December 2023)

    The goals are tripling renewable capacity and doubling the rate of efficiency improvement by 2030. IRENA’s third tracking report says capacity reached 5.15 TW at the end of 2025 against a target of 11.2 TW (IRENA, 21 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Clean energy at record pace

  • Early Warnings for All

    Adopted by United Nations, led by WMO and UNDRR

    119 countries reported a multi-hazard early warning system in the 2025 status report, against a goal of everyone by the end of 2027 (WMO and UNDRR, 2025 report (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Natural and cosmic hazards · SWOT · Threats · Global warming

  • The Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework

    Adopted by Parties to the Convention on Biological Diversity, decision 15/4 (December 2022)

    It has 23 action-oriented global targets for 2030 (Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (opens in a new tab)), among them 30% of land and sea conserved.

    Related SWOT · Threats · Biodiversity loss · SWOT · Weaknesses · Land and sea heavily altered · SWOT · Strengths · Nature’s stewards

  • The BBNJ Agreement on the high seas

    Adopted by United Nations member states (in force since 17 January 2026)

    It entered into force on 17 January 2026 and its first Conference of the Parties is due in January 2027 (United Nations, BBNJ Agreement (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Ocean warming and acidification · SWOT · Strengths · The ocean buffer

  • The WHO Pandemic Agreement

    Adopted by World Health Assembly (adopted 20 May 2025)

    Its annex on sharing pathogens and benefits is still being negotiated, and the agreement opens for signature only once that annex is adopted (WHO, 19 May 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Pandemics and drug resistance

How this TOWS matrix works

For Earth, each cell will list responses at global scale that a named authoritative body (for example a UN agency, the IPCC or the parties to a treaty) has proposed, adopted or assessed.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and linked to the SWOT items it connects. Hawkeye’s only editorial step is placing each response in a cell. Responses are not ranked. The matrix is completed after all four SWOT quadrants are written.

    Strengths × Threats

    Responses that use strengths to reduce threats

  • WO

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
