Targets and latest values

The 2030 Agenda: all Sustainable Development Goal targets by 2030

Target
World leaders resolved between now and 2030, to end poverty and hunger everywhere (UN General Assembly resolution 70/1 (opens in a new tab), paragraph 3).
Baseline
2015, the year the goals were adopted. The UN measures progress against 2015 baselines (UN, SDG Report 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
Of the 139 SDG targets with trend data, only 36 per cent are on track or making moderate progress. (UN, SDG Report 2026 (opens in a new tab))
Distance
Nearly half — 49 per cent — are advancing too slowly, and 15 per cent have regressed below 2015 baselines. (UN, SDG Report 2026 (opens in a new tab))
Assessment by the tracking agency
The UN says progress remains uneven and insufficient (UN, SDG Report 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Warming well below 2 °C, aiming for 1.5 °C

Target
Holding the increase in the global average temperature to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels and pursuing efforts to limit the temperature increase to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels (Paris Agreement (opens in a new tab), Article 2.1(a)).
Baseline
The 1850–1900 average, which WMO uses as the pre-industrial level (WMO, State of the Global Climate 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
2025: about 1.43 ± 0.13 °C above the 1850–1900 pre-industrial average. 2024: about 1.55 °C above the 1850–1900 average (WMO, State of the Global Climate 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
2025 was 0.07 °C below 1.5 °C and 2024 was 0.05 °C above it (Hawkeye’s arithmetic, single years). The Paris goal refers to long-term warming, which this page does not compute (TBC).
Assessment by the tracking agency
Not checked for this page (TBC).

Triple renewable power capacity by 2030

Target
Tripling renewable energy capacity globally and doubling the global average annual rate of energy efficiency improvements by 2030 (decision 1/CMA.5 (opens in a new tab), paragraph 28(a)). IRENA puts the tripling target at 11.2 TW (IRENA, 21 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
Not given in the sources checked (TBC). One third of 11.2 TW is about 3.7 TW (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Latest
5.15 TW at the end of 2025, after a record 693 gigawatts (GW) of new renewables power added in 2025 (IRENA, 21 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
About 6.05 TW still to add by 2030 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). IRENA says the annual growth rate would have to accelerate to 1 200 GW on average between 2026 and 2030 (IRENA, 21 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Assessment by the tracking agency
installed capacity must more than double today’s levels (IRENA, 21 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Double the yearly rate of energy efficiency improvement by 2030

Target
The same paragraph 28(a) of decision 1/CMA.5 (opens in a new tab): doubling the global average annual rate of energy efficiency improvements by 2030.
Baseline
Not given in the sources checked (TBC).
Latest
energy intensity improved by around 2% in 2025, far below the 4% needed to meet the UAE Consensus energy savings goal (IRENA, 21 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
About 2 percentage points a year short (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
IRENA’s Director-General: energy efficiency is falling behind (IRENA, 21 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Electricity for everyone by 2030

Target
By 2030, ensure universal access to affordable, reliable and modern energy services (UN global indicator framework (opens in a new tab), target 7.1).
Baseline
Not given in this row (TBC).
Latest
92% of people in 2024. In 2024, 655 million people were still without electricity. (Tracking SDG 7, 2026 (opens in a new tab))
Distance
655 million people, or 8% of the world, still to connect by 2030 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
The global rate of access to electricity has stalled at 92 percent. (Tracking SDG 7, 2026 (opens in a new tab))

End hunger by 2030

Target
By 2030, end hunger (UN global indicator framework (opens in a new tab), target 2.1).
Baseline
8.6% of the world’s people in 2022 (SOFI 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
7.8% in 2025, about 645 million people (range 608 to 696 million) (SOFI 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
About 645 million people (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). The fall from 2022 to 2025 was 0.8 percentage points (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
FAO says improvements are insufficient to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals by 2030 (FAO, on SOFI 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

30% of land and of sea conserved by 2030

Target
Ensure and enable that by 2030 at least 30 per cent of terrestrial and inland water areas, and of marine and coastal areas are effectively conserved (Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (opens in a new tab), Target 3).
Baseline
2020. The source gives the rise since then as less than 0.5 percentage points in both realms but not the 2020 values (Protected Planet Report 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
17.6 per cent of land and inland waters and 8.4 per cent of the ocean and coastal areas globally are within documented protected and conserved areas (Protected Planet Report 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
The report says the network needs to grow by a further 12.4 per cent on land and 21.6 per cent in the ocean (Protected Planet Report 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
Assessment by the tracking agency
progress must accelerate (UNEP-WCMC and IUCN (opens in a new tab)). Hawkeye re-opened the official list of Global Protected Planet Reports on 12 November 2026. The 2024 report was still the latest official global assessment listed (Protected Planet, Global Protected Planet Reports (opens in a new tab)).

Early warnings for everyone by the end of 2027

Target
Early Warnings for All aims to ensure that everyone on Earth is protected from hazardous weather, water, or climate events through life-saving early warning systems by the end of 2027 (WMO, Early Warnings for All (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
Not given in the sources checked (TBC).
Latest
119 countries, or 60% of all countries, now reporting the existence of a Multi-Hazard Early Warning System (WMO and UNDRR, 2025 report (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
About 40% of countries, with about 13 months left (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
WMO and UNDRR say critical gaps remain (WMO and UNDRR, 2025 report (opens in a new tab)).

How this GAP analysis works

For Earth, the targets are the globally agreed ones: the 2030 Sustainable Development Goals, the Paris Agreement temperature goal, the energy goals agreed at COP28 and the Kunming-Montreal biodiversity targets.

Each row will quote the target word for word and give the baseline, the latest measured value with its year, and the distance between them. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
