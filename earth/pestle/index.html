Earth PESTLE

Each point is a force at world scale, with its source. Sources were checked on 12 November 2026.

Political 2 points

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • The nuclear order is weakening. SIPRI writes that the strategic nuclear arms control framework has collapsed and that the global non-proliferation regime is under stress (SIPRI Yearbook 2026, summary (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Nuclear war · SWOT · Weaknesses · Weakening cooperation on security

Governance and cooperation

  • A record fall in aid. Official development assistance fell by a record 23.1 per cent in 2025, back to about its 2015 level (UN, SDG Report 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Weakening cooperation on security · SWOT · Weaknesses · Debt crowding out development

Economic 1 point

Economy and finance

  • Interest of about US$1 trillion. Developing countries’ public borrowing costs have risen rapidly, with the interest bill reaching close to $1 trillion in 2025. UNCTAD says 3.7 billion people live in countries that spend more on interest than on education or health (UNCTAD, A World of Debt 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Debt crowding out development · SWOT · Threats · Economic shocks and debt

Social 2 points

Forced displacement

  • 117.8 million people forced from home. As of December 2025, more than 117.8 million people were forcibly displaced worldwide (UN, SDG Report 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Forced displacement

Water and food security

  • Hunger is falling slowly. An estimated 7.8 percent of the global population faced hunger in 2025, about 645 million people (SOFI 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Hunger and food insecurity · GAP · End hunger by 2030

Technological 1 point

Technology and AI

  • Three in four people online. Internet access has surged, from 40 to 74 per cent. Electricity now reaches 92 per cent of the world's population. (UN, SDG Report 2026 (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Health and living standards gains

Legal 2 points

Oceans

  • A treaty for the high seas. The BBNJ Agreement entered into force on 17 January 2026. Its first Conference of the Parties meets from 11 to 22 January 2027 (United Nations, BBNJ Agreement (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · The ocean buffer · SWOT · Opportunities · New global agreements

Pandemics and health

  • The Pandemic Agreement is not yet open for signature. WHO adopted it in May 2025 (WHO, 20 May 2025 (opens in a new tab)). In May 2026 the Assembly gave more time to negotiate its annex on sharing pathogens and benefits. Adoption of the PABS Annex is critical for the opening for signature of the WHO Pandemic Agreement. (WHO, 19 May 2026 (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Pandemics and drug resistance · SWOT · Opportunities · New global agreements

Environmental 1 point

Climate change / global warming

  • Eleven hottest years in a row. The past eleven years, 2015–2025, were the eleven warmest years on record. In 2025, ocean heat content (to a depth of 2,000 metres) reached the highest level since the start of records in 1960 (WMO, State of the Global Climate 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

How this PESTLE analysis works

At Earth level there is no “outside”, so PESTLE covers the forces humanity creates or faces worldwide: geopolitics and cooperation between countries, the world economy, demographic and social change, technology, international law and treaties, and changes in Earth’s natural systems.

Each factor will have 3–5 sourced points. Each point notes whether the source describes the force as working for or against Earth’s systems and people, and links to the SWOT Opportunity or Threat it supports. PESTLE is the evidence layer behind the SWOT.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
