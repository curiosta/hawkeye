About Hawkeye

An evolving personal research project by Manoj Sahukar, started in 2019, with one aim: try and summarize the world.

What is SWOT?

SWOT analysis is a planning framework that looks at a subject through four lenses. Two describe the subject itself and two describe its surroundings:

  • S

    Strengths

    Internal qualities that help.

  • W

    Weaknesses

    Internal qualities that hold back.

  • O

    Opportunities

    External conditions that could help.

  • T

    Threats

    External conditions that could cause harm.

On this site the quadrants are presented in the order the research began: Threats, Opportunities, Weaknesses, Strengths.

The top-down method

Hawkeye applies SWOT at every level, starting from the widest view and zooming in: first the whole Earth — its natural systems as well as its people — then each of the seven continents, then the countries within them, beginning with Asia and India. Each level gives context for the one below it.

Five frameworks

SWOT is the core of each level page. Four related frameworks look at the same place from other angles, using the same themes so items can be linked across them:

  • S

    SWOT

    Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats: what a place has and what it faces.

  • P

    PESTLE

    The large outside forces acting on a place, in six groups: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal and Environmental.

  • G

    GAP

    Where things stand now compared with an officially agreed target.

  • T

    TOWS

    Pairs internal factors (Strengths, Weaknesses) with external ones (Opportunities, Threats) into four kinds of response.

  • S

    SOAR

    Strengths, Opportunities, Aspirations and Results: a forward-looking view built on official goals.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.

Not every framework suits every level. SWOT, PESTLE and GAP are planned for every level. TOWS and SOAR are planned for Earth and for countries, and for a continent only where a regional body has stated its own goals. A framework tab appears on a page only once that framework’s page exists.

Work in progress

This is a living project. Sections marked Analysis in progress haven’t been written yet and will be filled in from the linked source playlists and further research. Nothing in those sections is guessed or filled in for the sake of looking complete.
