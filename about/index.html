About Hawkeye

An evolving personal research project by Manoj Sahukar, started in 2019, with one aim: try and summarize the world.

What is SWOT?

SWOT analysis is a planning framework that looks at a subject through four lenses. Two describe the subject itself and two describe its surroundings:

  • S

    Strengths

    Internal qualities that help.

  • W

    Weaknesses

    Internal qualities that hold back.

  • O

    Opportunities

    External conditions that could help.

  • T

    Threats

    External conditions that could cause harm.

On this site the quadrants are presented in the order the research began: Threats, Opportunities, Weaknesses, Strengths.

The top-down method

Hawkeye applies SWOT at every level, starting from the widest view and zooming in: first the World, then each of the seven continents, then the countries within them, beginning with Asia and India. Each level gives context for the one below it.

Work in progress

This is a living project. Sections marked Analysis in progress haven’t been written yet and will be filled in from the linked source playlists and further research. Nothing in those sections is guessed or filled in for the sake of looking complete.
