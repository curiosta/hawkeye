A Aspirations

No body that covers the whole of Europe states a single vision for it. The Aspirations below are those of the European Union, whose 27 members have 60.1% of Europe’s people. They are quoted in the EU’s own words.

The aims of the Union

Treaty on European Union, Article 3 ·, EU-27, 60.1% of Europe’s population

1. The Union's aim is to promote peace, its values and the well-being of its peoples. 3. The Union shall establish an internal market. It shall work for the sustainable development of Europe based on balanced economic growth and price stability, a highly competitive social market economy, aiming at full employment and social progress, […] (Treaty on European Union, Article 3 (opens in a new tab))

The Strategic Agenda 2024–2029

European Council, adopted 27 June 2024, a political agenda, not a law ·, EU-27, 60.1% of Europe’s population

We will strengthen our competitiveness and become the first climate-neutral continent, making a success of the climate and digital transitions, leaving no one behind. (European Council, Strategic Agenda 2024–2029 (opens in a new tab))

The phrase “first climate-neutral continent” is the EU’s. The EU does not cover the whole continent.

Climate neutrality by 2050

European Climate Law, binding since 29 July 2021, 2040 target in force since 7 April 2026 ·, EU-27, 60.1% of Europe’s population

Union-wide greenhouse gas emissions and removals regulated in Union law shall be balanced within the Union at the latest by 2050, thus reducing emissions to net zero by that date, and the Union shall aim to achieve negative emissions thereafter. (European Climate Law, Article 2(1) (opens in a new tab))

The 2030 and 2040 targets are quoted on the GAP page.

The Competitiveness Compass

European Commission, COM(2025) 30, 29 January 2025, not a law ·, EU-27, 60.1% of Europe’s population

The EU has all the assets to lead in the global economy of tomorrow. (Competitiveness Compass (opens in a new tab))

How this SOAR analysis works

For a continent, the Aspirations are goal statements of the regional and treaty bodies its countries belong to, quoted word for word and labelled with each body’s members.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Official sources, and labelled analysis. Hawkeye reports official and authoritative sources, quoted or attributed, with a link. Documented goals and programmes appear as those bodies stated them.
