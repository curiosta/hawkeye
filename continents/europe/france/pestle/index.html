Political 4 points

Governance and cooperation

  • No absolute majority in the National Assembly. On 20 October 2026 the largest group, Rassemblement National, had 121 of the 577 seats (21%). An absolute majority is 289 seats (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). 574 seats were filled, shared among 11 political groups and 11 non-attached members (Assemblée nationale, Effectif des groupes politiques (opens in a new tab), 17th legislature, elected 30 June–7 July 2024). Seat counts change with by-elections and group moves.

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • Defence founded on nuclear deterrence. La politique de défense de la France est fondée sur le socle de la dissuasion nucléaire (unofficial translation: “France’s defence policy rests on nuclear deterrence”) (Loi n° 2023-703 (opens in a new tab), Art. 2). SIPRI counts 370 French warheads in January 2026, 290 of them in the military stockpile. Of the nine states SIPRI lists as nuclear-armed, France is the only EU member (Hawkeye’s reading of the list; SIPRI Yearbook 2026 summary (opens in a new tab)).
  • A permanent seat on the UN Security Council. The Republic of China, France, the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, and the United States of America shall be permanent members of the Security Council. (UN Charter (opens in a new tab), Art. 23).
  • Spending: ninth in the world, behind Germany in the EU. SIPRI ranks France ninth in military spending in 2025 (US$68.0 billion, 2.03% of GDP) and Germany fourth (US$114 billion) (SIPRI Yearbook 2026 summary (opens in a new tab); SIPRI Military Expenditure Database (opens in a new tab)). The 2026 update of the programming law sets a defence effort of at least 2.5% of GDP in 2030 and an objective of 3.5% by 2035 (Loi n° 2026-791 (opens in a new tab), Art. 1).

Economic 4 points

Economy and finance

  • Deficit 5.1% and debt 115.7% of GDP in 2025. En 2025, le déficit public s’établit à 152,5 Md€ après 169,1 Md€ en 2024, soit 5,1 % du produit intérieur brut, après 5,8 %. (unofficial translation: “In 2025, the public deficit was €152.5 billion after €169.1 billion in 2024, or 5.1% of gross domestic product, after 5.8%.”) La dette publique s’établit à 115,7 % du PIB, après 112,6 % en 2024. (unofficial translation: “Public debt stands at 115.7% of GDP, after 112.6% in 2024.”) (INSEE, Insee Première n° 2106, 29 May 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The March 2026 EU notification gave 115.6% (INSEE, Informations rapides n° 78 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Under an EU excessive deficit procedure since 26 July 2024. The European Commission lists the procedure for France as ongoing (European Commission (opens in a new tab); Council of the EU, ST 12166/24 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Public deficit and debt, and a goods trade deficit · SWOT · Threats · An EU excessive deficit procedure and rising interest costs

Economy and finance

  • GDP of €2,991.1 billion in 2025. Le PIB augmente de 0,8 % en 2025 en volume et atteint 2991,1 milliards d'euros. (unofficial translation: “GDP rose by 0.8% in volume in 2025 and reached €2,991.1 billion.”) (INSEE, Insee Première n° 2105 (opens in a new tab), figure 1). In current US dollars that is US$3,366 billion, the seventh largest in the world in 2025 (World Bank WDI (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · A large economy

Economy and finance

  • Unemployment at 8.3% and rising. Le taux de chômage s’établit à 8,3 % de la population active, supérieur de 0,2 point à son niveau du premier trimestre 2026 et de 0,7 point à celui du deuxième trimestre 2025 (+261 000 chômeurs). Le taux de chômage est à son plus haut niveau depuis le troisième trimestre 2020 qui était affecté par la crise sanitaire, et depuis le troisième trimestre 2019 auparavant. (unofficial translation: “The unemployment rate stands at 8.3% of the labour force, 0.2 points above its level in the first quarter of 2026 and 0.7 points above the second quarter of 2025 (+261,000 unemployed). The unemployment rate is at its highest since the third quarter of 2020, which was affected by the health crisis, and before that since the third quarter of 2019.”) (INSEE, Informations rapides n° 192, 7 August 2026 (opens in a new tab); second quarter of 2026, France including Mayotte).

Social 3 points

Population and demographics

  • As many over-65s as under-20s. Au 1er janvier 2026, en France, les personnes d’au moins 65 ans sont presque aussi nombreuses que celles de moins de 20 ans : les premières représentent 22,2 % des habitants, les secondes 22,5 % (unofficial translation: “On 1 January 2026, in France, people aged 65 or over are almost as numerous as those under 20: the former make up 22.2% of residents, the latter 22.5%”) (INSEE, Insee Première n° 2087 (opens in a new tab)). The median age is 42.3 years (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab), 2025).

Population and demographics

  • More deaths than births in 2025. Ainsi, pour la première fois depuis la fin de la Seconde Guerre mondiale, le solde naturel en France est négatif en 2025 : il s’établit à ‑6 000. (unofficial translation: “So, for the first time since the end of the Second World War, France’s natural balance is negative in 2025, at −6,000.”) L’indicateur conjoncturel de fécondité poursuit sa baisse : après 1,61 en 2024, il s’établit à 1,56 enfant par femme en 2025 ; c’est le niveau le plus faible depuis la fin de la Première Guerre mondiale. (unofficial translation: “The total fertility rate continues to fall: after 1.61 in 2024, it stands at 1.56 children per woman in 2025, the lowest level since the end of the First World War.”) (INSEE, Insee Première n° 2087 (opens in a new tab); provisional). INSEE estimates the population at 69.1 million on 1 January 2026: 66.8 million in metropolitan France and 2.3 million in the overseas departments.

Human development

  • Very high human development. HDI 0.920, rank 26 of 193, and life expectancy 83.3 years (data year 2023) (UNDP HDR 2025 (opens in a new tab)); 83.6 years in 2025 on UN estimates (UN WPP 2024 (opens in a new tab)). INSEE gives 85.9 years for women and 80.3 years for men in 2025 (INSEE (opens in a new tab); provisional).

Technological 3 points

Technology and AI

  • France 2030: 10 objectives and 7 levers. Pour mémoire, le plan fixe 10 objectifs et 7 leviers qui s’articulent autour de trois axes : mieux produire, mieux vivre et mieux comprendre notre monde. (unofficial translation: “As a reminder, the plan sets 10 objectives and 7 levers, organised around three axes: producing better, living better and better understanding our world.”) (Annex to the 2027 draft budget, investissements d’avenir (opens in a new tab), p. 17). The plan was launched on 12 October 2021.
  • €54.3 billion planned, €19.4 billion paid out. At 30 June 2026 the envelopes totalled €54.3 billion, of which €42.2 billion had been committed and €19.4 billion disbursed (Annex to the 2027 draft budget (opens in a new tab), p. 18).

Technology and AI

  • R&D at 2.18% of GDP, against a legal goal of at least 3%. Domestic R&D spending was €64.1 billion in 2024, 2.18% of GDP, unchanged from 2023; business R&D was 1.43% and government R&D 0.75% (MESR-SIES, Note Flash n° 16 (opens in a new tab)). The research programming law sets at least 3% overall and at least 1% for government au cours de la décennie suivante (unofficial translation: “over the following decade”), with no fixed year (Loi n° 2020-1674 (opens in a new tab), Art. 1).

Legal 3 points

Climate change / global warming

  • In the Energy Code: −40% by 2030 and carbon neutrality by 2050. 1° De réduire les émissions de gaz à effet de serre de 40 % entre 1990 et 2030 et d'atteindre la neutralité carbone à l'horizon 2050 en divisant les émissions de gaz à effet de serre par un facteur supérieur à six entre 1990 et 2050. [...] (unofficial translation: “1° To reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 40% between 1990 and 2030 and to reach carbon neutrality by 2050 by dividing greenhouse gas emissions by a factor of more than six between 1990 and 2050. [...]”) (Code de l’énergie, art. L100-4, I (opens in a new tab)). The code sets these as objectives of national energy policy.
  • In the low-carbon strategy: −50% by 2030. The third national low-carbon strategy (SNBC 3), adopted by décret n° 2026-636 of 16 July 2026, aims to Réduire de 50 % nos émissions sur le territoire français d'ici 2030, par rapport à 1990. (unofficial translation: “Reduce our emissions on French territory by 50% by 2030, compared with 1990.”) (Ministère de la Transition écologique, SNBC (opens in a new tab); Décret n° 2026-636 (opens in a new tab)). It sets carbon budgets of 342, 262 and 194 Mt CO2e a year for 2024–28, 2029–33 and 2034–38. The statute still reads −40%; both targets are shown, labelled.

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • No net land take by 2050. Afin d'atteindre l'objectif national d'absence de toute artificialisation nette des sols en 2050, le rythme de l'artificialisation des sols dans les dix années suivant la promulgation de la présente loi doit être tel que, sur cette période, la consommation totale d'espace observée à l'échelle nationale soit inférieure à la moitié de celle observée sur les dix années précédant cette date. (unofficial translation: “To reach the national objective of no net land artificialisation in 2050, the pace of land artificialisation in the ten years after this law is promulgated must be such that, over that period, total land consumption observed nationally is less than half that observed over the ten years before that date.”) (Loi n° 2021-1104 (Climate and Resilience law) (opens in a new tab), art. 191; promulgated 22 August 2021). Progress is on the GAP page.

Environmental 4 points

Energy transition

  • Nuclear 68.1% of electricity in 2025. Nuclear produced 373.0 TWh of 547.5 TWh; its share has ranged between 63% and 77% over the last ten years. Nuclear and renewables together produced a record 521.1 TWh, 95.2% of electricity generated in metropolitan France (RTE, Bilan électrique 2025 (opens in a new tab)). Fossil CO2 per person was 4.0 tonnes in 2024 (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Nuclear and low-carbon electricity

Natural and cosmic hazards

  • Adapting to +4 °C. Le nouveau PNACC a pour socle la trajectoire de réchauffement de référence pour l’adaptation au changement climatique (TRACC) qui prévoit un réchauffement mondial de + 3 °C en 2100 par rapport à l’ère pré-industrielle, soit environ + 4 °C en moyenne sur la France hexagonale. (unofficial translation: “The new PNACC is based on the reference warming trajectory for adaptation to climate change (TRACC), which projects global warming of +3 °C in 2100 compared with the pre-industrial era, that is about +4 °C on average across mainland France.”) (Ministère de la Transition écologique, PNACC-3 (opens in a new tab)). The plan was finalised on 10 March 2025 with 52 measures; an arrêté of 23 January 2026 makes the trajectory (+2 °C in 2030, +2.7 °C in 2050, +4 °C in 2100 for metropolitan France) the regulatory reference (Arrêté du 23 janvier 2026 (opens in a new tab), Art. 1).

Climate change / global warming

  • 367 Mt of greenhouse gases in 2024. Hors puits de carbone, les émissions de GES s’élèvent à 367 Mt CO 2 e en 2024 et ont ainsi diminué de 11,2 Mt CO₂ e, soit de 3,0 % par rapport à l’année précédente (unofficial translation: “Excluding carbon sinks, GHG emissions amounted to 367 Mt CO2e in 2024, a fall of 11.2 Mt CO2e, or 3.0%, on the previous year”) (Citepa, Secten 2026 (opens in a new tab)). That is 32.9% below 1990 (546.9 Mt); Citepa’s first estimate for 2025 is 359 Mt (Citepa, long series (opens in a new tab)).
  • The pace of cuts against the carbon budget. The High Council on Climate writes: Par rapport à la baisse pré-estimée en 2025, pour respecter le 3e budget carbone (2024-2028), le rythme doit s'accéler et au moins doubler, pour atteindre en moyenne plus de 4 %/an en 2026, 2027 et 2028. (unofficial translation: “Compared with the estimated fall in 2025, to meet the third carbon budget (2024–2028) the pace must accelerate and at least double, to reach an average of more than 4% a year in 2026, 2027 and 2028.”) (Haut Conseil pour le climat, Rapport annuel 2026 (opens in a new tab), ch. 3; the misspelling is in the source).

Sources checked 20 October 2026. French quotations are given as published; English translations are unofficial.

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
