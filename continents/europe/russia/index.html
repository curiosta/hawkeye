T

Threats

External · harmful

Military dangers named in nuclear policy

  • The state’s stated view. Russia’s 2024 nuclear deterrence policy names among the main military dangers наличие у потенциального противника и развертывание им систем и средств противоракетной обороны (“a potential adversary’s possession and deployment of missile defence systems and means”) and the build-up of forces with nuclear delivery means near Russia and its allies (Decree 991, 19 November 2024 (opens in a new tab), para. 15(б) and (в)).

Also in PESTLE · Political

Sanctions on oil and technology

  • An oil price cap of US$44.10. European Commission: The new price cap for Russian crude oil is $44,10 per barrel, effective 1 February. The new mechanism ensures that the cap is always 15% lower than the average market price for Urals crude in the previous reference period (22 weeks). (European Commission, 15 January 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • US export controls. A 2024 rule imposes additional export control measures against Russia and Belarus by expanding the scope of items identified under two EAR supplements that are subject to the EAR's Russian and Belarusian industry sector sanctions (Federal Register, 18 June 2024 (opens in a new tab), 89 FR 51644).

Also in PESTLE · Economic

Thawing permafrost and climate extremes

  • The state’s own list. The Climate Doctrine names among the adverse effects of expected climate change рост повторяемости, интенсивности и продолжительности периодов тепла и засух в одних регионах, экстремальных осадков, наводнений и опасного для сельского хозяйства переувлажнения почвы в других (“more frequent, intense and longer heat and drought in some regions, and extreme rainfall, floods and waterlogging harmful to farming in others”) and деградация многолетней мерзлоты и горного оледенения (“degradation of permafrost and mountain glaciers”) (Climate Doctrine (opens in a new tab), para. 47(б) and (г)).

O

Opportunities

External · helpful

Stated partnerships with China and India

  • China. Россия нацелена на дальнейшее упрочение отношений всеобъемлющего партнерства и стратегического взаимодействия с Китайской Народной Республикой (“Russia aims to further strengthen its comprehensive partnership and strategic interaction with the People’s Republic of China”) (Foreign Policy Concept 2023 (opens in a new tab), para. 52).
  • India. Россия будет и далее наращивать особо привилегированное стратегическое партнерство с Республикой Индией (“Russia will continue to build up its particularly privileged strategic partnership with the Republic of India”) (Foreign Policy Concept 2023 (opens in a new tab), para. 53).

Also in PESTLE · Political

Easier Arctic sea access, as the state assesses it

  • Ice conditions. The Climate Doctrine lists among climate change’s favourable effects for Russia: улучшение ледовой обстановки и соответственно условий транспортировки грузов в арктических морях, облегчение доступа к арктическим шельфам и их освоения (“improved ice conditions and so conditions for transporting cargo in the Arctic seas, and easier access to and development of the Arctic shelves”) (Climate Doctrine (opens in a new tab), para. 48(б)).
W

Weaknesses

Internal · harmful

Natural decrease and low fertility

  • 1.46 births per woman. The UN estimates Russia’s fertility rate at 1.45 in 2023 and 1.46 in 2025, with deaths exceeding births by 497,700 in 2023 and by a projected 623,900 in 2025 (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)). The national 2030 target is on the GAP page.
  • An 11.6-year gap between women and men. Life expectancy at birth in 2025 is 67.7 years for men and 79.3 for women (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab); gap: Hawkeye’s arithmetic).

Also in PESTLE · Social · GAP · Life expectancy · GAP · Fertility

A high policy rate

  • 14% key rate. Совет директоров Банка России 23 октября 2026 года принял решение сохранить ключевую ставку на уровне 14,00% годовых. (“On 23 October 2026 the Bank of Russia Board of Directors decided to keep the key rate at 14.00% per annum.”) It added: Текущее ценовое давление сохраняется повышенным. Инфляционные ожидания остаются высокими. (“Current price pressure remains elevated. Inflation expectations remain high.”) (Bank of Russia, 23 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Economic

Low research spending

  • About 1% of GDP on R&D. HSE University: Россия по-прежнему тратит на науку порядка 1% ВВП и занимает лишь 43-е место по данному индикатору. (“Russia still spends about 1% of GDP on science and ranks only 43rd on this indicator.”) It puts R&D intensity at 0.96% in 2023 (HSE ISSEK, 17 September 2024 (opens in a new tab)). The national 2% target is on the GAP page.

Also in PESTLE · Technological · GAP · R&D spending

High fossil CO2

  • 1,781 Mt, 12.3 t a person. Russia emitted 1,781 Mt of fossil CO2 in 2024, 4.6% of the world total and the most in Europe; 12.3 tonnes per person, 2.6 times the world average of 4.7 t (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab); Hawkeye’s arithmetic).

S

Strengths

Internal · helpful

Gas and oil reserves

  • About a fifth of world gas reserves. A press article reproduced in Rosnedra’s media review reads: Россия традиционно находится в мировых лидерах по запасам природного газа – примерно пятая их часть находится в нашей стране. По предварительным данным Роснедр 2024 года, это 63,5 триллиона кубических метров. (unofficial translation: “Russia is traditionally among the world leaders in natural gas reserves – roughly a fifth of them are in our country. According to Rosnedra’s preliminary data for 2024, that is 63.5 trillion cubic metres.”) (Rosnedra media review (opens in a new tab)).
  • Fifth for oil. The same text: По запасам нефти страна находится на пятом месте, в цифрах это – 31,4 миллиарда тонн. (“In oil reserves the country is in fifth place, 31.4 billion tonnes.”) Citing the Ministry of Energy, it puts 2024 output at 685 billion cubic metres of gas and 516 million tonnes of oil (Rosnedra media review (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Economic

The largest nuclear arsenal

  • 5,420 warheads. Russia held 5,420 nuclear warheads in January 2026, 44.5% of the world’s 12,187 (SIPRI Yearbook 2026 (opens in a new tab); share: Hawkeye’s arithmetic).

Also in PESTLE · Political

The world’s largest forest area

  • 815 million hectares. FAO lists the Russian Federation first among the five countries with the most forest, at 815 million ha in 2020 (FAO FRA 2020 Key findings (opens in a new tab)). FAO’s 2025 assessment: More than half (54 percent) of the world’s forests is in only five countries – the Russian Federation, Brazil, Canada, the United States of America and China (in descending order by area). (FAO FRA 2025 Key findings (opens in a new tab)).
  • A large land carbon sink. Russia’s 2026 inventory: В 2024 г. сектор обеспечивал нетто-поглощение ПГ из атмосферы в размере 1116,1 млн. т CO2-экв., компенсируя тем самым 52,2% общего выброса ПГ в других секторах кадастра. (“In 2024 the [land use, land-use change and forestry] sector provided net removals of 1,116.1 million t CO2-eq, offsetting 52.2% of total GHG emissions in the other inventory sectors.”) (Russia’s GHG inventory, 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Environmental · GAP · Balance by 2060

A nuclear icebreaker fleet

  • Eight nuclear icebreakers. Atomflot, part of Rosatom: Сегодня в его состав входят восемь атомных ледоколов: головной универсальный атомный ледокол «Арктика», серийные универсальные ледоколы «Сибирь», «Урал» и «Якутия», а также ледоколы «Ямал», «50 лет Победы», «Таймыр» и «Вайгач». (“Today it comprises eight nuclear icebreakers: the lead universal nuclear icebreaker Arktika, the series universal icebreakers Sibir, Ural and Yakutiya, and the icebreakers Yamal, 50 Let Pobedy, Taymyr and Vaygach.”) (Atomflot, 5 August 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
