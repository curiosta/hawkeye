A Aspirations

Russia’s national development goals are set by Presidential Decree No. 309 of 7 May 2024. The Aspirations below are quoted from it and from the Climate Doctrine. Translations are Hawkeye’s own and unofficial.

Why the goals were set

Presidential Decree No. 309, 7 May 2024

The decree opens: В целях обеспечения устойчивого экономического и социального развития Российской Федерации, укрепления государственного, культурно-ценностного и экономического суверенитета, увеличения численности населения страны и повышения уровня жизни граждан (“In order to ensure sustainable economic and social development of the Russian Federation, strengthen state, cultural-value and economic sovereignty, increase the country’s population and raise living standards”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab)).

Seven national goals

Presidential Decree No. 309, 7 May 2024, item 1

Item 1 of the decree names seven national goals (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab)):

  1. сохранение населения, укрепление здоровья и повышение благополучия людей, поддержка семьи (unofficial translation: “preserving the population, strengthening health and improving people’s well-being, supporting the family”)
  2. реализация потенциала каждого человека, развитие его талантов, воспитание патриотичной и социально ответственной личности (unofficial translation: “realising the potential of every person, developing their talents, raising a patriotic and socially responsible individual”)
  3. комфортная и безопасная среда для жизни (unofficial translation: “a comfortable and safe living environment”)
  4. экологическое благополучие (unofficial translation: “environmental well-being”)
  5. устойчивая и динамичная экономика (unofficial translation: “a sustainable and dynamic economy”)
  6. технологическое лидерство (unofficial translation: “technological leadership”)
  7. цифровая трансформация государственного и муниципального управления, экономики и социальной сферы (unofficial translation: “digital transformation of state and municipal administration, the economy and the social sphere”)

Carbon balance by 2060

Climate Doctrine, Presidential Decree No. 812, 26 October 2023

Ключевой долгосрочной целью климатической политики является достижение с учетом национальных интересов и приоритетов социально-экономического развития не позднее 2060 года баланса между антропогенными выбросами парниковых газов и их поглощением. (“The key long-term goal of climate policy is to achieve, taking account of national interests and socio-economic development priorities, a balance between anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions and their removal by 2060 at the latest.”) (Climate Doctrine (opens in a new tab), para. 21)

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
