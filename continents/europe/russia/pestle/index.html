Russia PESTLE

The large outside forces acting on a place, in six groups: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal and Environmental.

Political 4 points

Governance and cooperation

  • A “state-civilisation”. The Foreign Policy Concept describes особое положение России как самобытного государства-цивилизации, обширной евразийской и евро-тихоокеанской державы (“Russia’s special position as a distinctive state-civilisation, a vast Eurasian and Euro-Pacific power”) (Foreign Policy Concept (opens in a new tab), para. 4).
  • China. Россия нацелена на дальнейшее упрочение отношений всеобъемлющего партнерства и стратегического взаимодействия с Китайской Народной Республикой (“Russia aims to further strengthen its comprehensive partnership and strategic interaction with the People’s Republic of China”) (Foreign Policy Concept 2023 (opens in a new tab), para. 52).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · Stated partnerships with China and India

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • 5,420 warheads. Russia held 5,420 nuclear warheads in January 2026, 44.5% of the world’s 12,187 (SIPRI Yearbook 2026 (opens in a new tab); share: Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
  • The state’s stated view. Russia’s 2024 nuclear deterrence policy names among the main military dangers наличие у потенциального противника и развертывание им систем и средств противоракетной обороны (“a potential adversary’s possession and deployment of missile defence systems and means”) and the build-up of forces with nuclear delivery means near Russia and its allies (Decree 991, 19 November 2024 (opens in a new tab), para. 15(б) and (в)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · The largest nuclear arsenal · SWOT · Threats · Military dangers named in nuclear policy

Economic 5 points

Economy and finance

  • 14% key rate. Совет директоров Банка России 23 октября 2026 года принял решение сохранить ключевую ставку на уровне 14,00% годовых. (“On 23 October 2026 the Bank of Russia Board of Directors decided to keep the key rate at 14.00% per annum.”) It added: Текущее ценовое давление сохраняется повышенным. Инфляционные ожидания остаются высокими. (“Current price pressure remains elevated. Inflation expectations remain high.”) (Bank of Russia, 23 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • An oil price cap of US$44.10. European Commission: The new price cap for Russian crude oil is $44,10 per barrel, effective 1 February. The new mechanism ensures that the cap is always 15% lower than the average market price for Urals crude in the previous reference period (22 weeks). (European Commission, 15 January 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • US export controls. A 2024 rule imposes additional export control measures against Russia and Belarus by expanding the scope of items identified under two EAR supplements that are subject to the EAR's Russian and Belarusian industry sector sanctions (Federal Register, 18 June 2024 (opens in a new tab), 89 FR 51644).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · A high policy rate · SWOT · Threats · Sanctions on oil and technology

Natural resources

  • About a fifth of world gas reserves. A press article reproduced in Rosnedra’s media review reads: Россия традиционно находится в мировых лидерах по запасам природного газа – примерно пятая их часть находится в нашей стране. По предварительным данным Роснедр 2024 года, это 63,5 триллиона кубических метров. (unofficial translation: “Russia is traditionally among the world leaders in natural gas reserves – roughly a fifth of them are in our country. According to Rosnedra’s preliminary data for 2024, that is 63.5 trillion cubic metres.”) (Rosnedra media review (opens in a new tab)).
  • Fifth for oil. The same text: По запасам нефти страна находится на пятом месте, в цифрах это – 31,4 миллиарда тонн. (“In oil reserves the country is in fifth place, 31.4 billion tonnes.”) Citing the Ministry of Energy, it puts 2024 output at 685 billion cubic metres of gas and 516 million tonnes of oil (Rosnedra media review (opens in a new tab)).

Social 3 points

Population and demographics

  • 1.46 births per woman. The UN estimates Russia’s fertility rate at 1.45 in 2023 and 1.46 in 2025, with deaths exceeding births by 497,700 in 2023 and by a projected 623,900 in 2025 (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)). The national 2030 target is on the GAP page.
  • An 11.6-year gap between women and men. Life expectancy at birth in 2025 is 67.7 years for men and 79.3 for women (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab); gap: Hawkeye’s arithmetic).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Natural decrease and low fertility

Human development

  • A poverty target. Decree 309 sets снижение уровня бедности ниже 7 процентов к 2030 году и ниже 5 процентов к 2036 году (“reducing poverty below 7% by 2030 and below 5% by 2036”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab)). The latest Rosstat values are on the GAP page.

Technological 1 point

Technology and AI

  • About 1% of GDP on R&D. HSE University: Россия по-прежнему тратит на науку порядка 1% ВВП и занимает лишь 43-е место по данному индикатору. (“Russia still spends about 1% of GDP on science and ranks only 43rd on this indicator.”) It puts R&D intensity at 0.96% in 2023 (HSE ISSEK, 17 September 2024 (opens in a new tab)). The national 2% target is on the GAP page.

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Low research spending

Legal 3 points

Governance and cooperation

  • National goals in a decree. Presidential Decree No. 309 of 7 May 2024 sets the national development goals to 2030 and 2036. The decree opens: В целях обеспечения устойчивого экономического и социального развития Российской Федерации, укрепления государственного, культурно-ценностного и экономического суверенитета, увеличения численности населения страны и повышения уровня жизни граждан (“In order to ensure sustainable economic and social development of the Russian Federation, strengthen state, cultural-value and economic sovereignty, increase the country’s population and raise living standards”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab)). The goals are listed on the SOAR page.

Climate change / global warming

  • A 2060 goal in the Climate Doctrine. Ключевой долгосрочной целью климатической политики является достижение с учетом национальных интересов и приоритетов социально-экономического развития не позднее 2060 года баланса между антропогенными выбросами парниковых газов и их поглощением. (“The key long-term goal of climate policy is to achieve, taking account of national interests and socio-economic development priorities, a balance between anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions and their removal by 2060 at the latest.”) (Climate Doctrine (opens in a new tab), para. 21)

Governance and cooperation

  • Constitution Article 79. The Constitution of the Russian Federation, as amended after the vote of 1 July 2020, says: Российская Федерация может участвовать в межгосударственных объединениях и передавать им часть своих полномочий в соответствии с международными договорами Российской Федерации, если это не влечет за собой ограничения прав и свобод человека и гражданина и не противоречит основам конституционного строя Российской Федерации. (unofficial translation: “The Russian Federation may participate in interstate associations and transfer to them part of its powers in accordance with international treaties of the Russian Federation, if this does not entail restrictions on the rights and freedoms of the human being and the citizen and does not contradict the foundations of the constitutional system of the Russian Federation.”) It also says: Решения межгосударственных органов, принятые на основании положений международных договоров Российской Федерации в их истолковании, противоречащем Конституции Российской Федерации, не подлежат исполнению в Российской Федерации. (unofficial translation: “Decisions of interstate bodies adopted on the basis of provisions of international treaties of the Russian Federation, in their interpretation contradicting the Constitution of the Russian Federation, are not subject to execution in the Russian Federation.”) (Constitution of the Russian Federation, Article 79 (opens in a new tab)).

Environmental 4 points

Climate change / global warming

  • 1,781 Mt, 12.3 t a person. Russia emitted 1,781 Mt of fossil CO2 in 2024, 4.6% of the world total and the most in Europe; 12.3 tonnes per person, 2.6 times the world average of 4.7 t (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab); Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
  • The state’s own list. The Climate Doctrine names among the adverse effects of expected climate change рост повторяемости, интенсивности и продолжительности периодов тепла и засух в одних регионах, экстремальных осадков, наводнений и опасного для сельского хозяйства переувлажнения почвы в других (“more frequent, intense and longer heat and drought in some regions, and extreme rainfall, floods and waterlogging harmful to farming in others”) and деградация многолетней мерзлоты и горного оледенения (“degradation of permafrost and mountain glaciers”) (Climate Doctrine (opens in a new tab), para. 47(б) and (г)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · High fossil CO2 · SWOT · Threats · Thawing permafrost and climate extremes

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • 832,630 thousand hectares of forest in 2025. FAO lists the Russian Federation first among the ten countries with the most forest, at 832,630 thousand hectares, 20% of the world forest area (FAO FRA 2025 (opens in a new tab), Table 3). The key findings say: More than half (54 percent) of the world’s forests is in only five countries – the Russian Federation, Brazil, Canada, the United States of America and China (in descending order by area). (FAO FRA 2025 Key findings (opens in a new tab)).
  • A large land carbon sink. Russia’s 2026 inventory: В 2024 г. сектор обеспечивал нетто-поглощение ПГ из атмосферы в размере 1116,1 млн. т CO2-экв., компенсируя тем самым 52,2% общего выброса ПГ в других секторах кадастра. (“In 2024 the [land use, land-use change and forestry] sector provided net removals of 1,116.1 million t CO2-eq, offsetting 52.2% of total GHG emissions in the other inventory sectors.”) (Russia’s GHG inventory, 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Official sources, and labelled analysis. Hawkeye reports official and authoritative sources, quoted or attributed, with a link. Documented goals and programmes appear as those bodies stated them. TOWS strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice to any person or firm. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
