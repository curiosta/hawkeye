Targets and latest values

Life expectancy of 78 years by 2030

Target
увеличение ожидаемой продолжительности жизни до 78 лет к 2030 году и до 81 года к 2036 году (“raising life expectancy to 78 years by 2030 and to 81 by 2036”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab), para. 2(б))
Baseline
Not stated in the decree.
Latest
73.5 years in 2025; 73.2 in 2023 (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)). Rosstat’s figure has not been confirmed at source.
Distance
4.5 years short of the 2030 target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 78 − 73.5)
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

A fertility rate of 1.6 by 2030

Target
повышение суммарного коэффициента рождаемости до 1,6 к 2030 году и до 1,8 к 2036 году (“raising the total fertility rate to 1.6 by 2030 and to 1.8 by 2036”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab), para. 2(а))
Baseline
Not stated in the decree.
Latest
1.46 births per woman in 2025; 1.45 in 2023 (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
0.14 births per woman below the 2030 target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 1.6 − 1.46).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Poverty below 7% by 2030

Target
снижение уровня бедности ниже 7 процентов к 2030 году и ниже 5 процентов к 2036 году (“reducing poverty below 7% by 2030 and below 5% by 2036”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
8.3% of the population (12.2 million) in 2023 (Rosstat (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
6.7% (9.7 million) in 2025; 7.1% in 2024 (Rosstat (opens in a new tab)). Rosstat’s tables leave out the Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson regions.
Distance
The 2025 figure is 0.3 percentage points below the 7% level set for 2030 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 7 − 6.7). The 2036 level is 5%.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

A minimum wage of at least RUB 35,000 a month by 2030

Target
рост к 2030 году более чем в два раза по сравнению с суммой, установленной на 2023 год, с достижением его величины не менее чем 35 тыс. рублей в месяц (“more than doubling by 2030 from the amount set for 2023, reaching at least 35,000 roubles a month”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
RUB 22,440 a month from 1 January 2025 (Federal Law 365-FZ (opens in a new tab), Art. 1).
Latest
с 1 января 2026 года в сумме 27 093 рубля в месяц (“from 1 January 2026, 27,093 roubles a month”) (Federal Law 429-FZ (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
RUB 7,907 a month below the RUB 35,000 floor (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 35,000 − 27,093).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Fourth-largest economy at purchasing power parity by 2030

Target
выход не позднее 2030 года на четвертое место в мире по объему валового внутреннего продукта, рассчитанного по паритету покупательной способности (“reaching fourth place in the world by 2030 at the latest in GDP at purchasing power parity”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab), para. 6(а))
Baseline
Not stated in the decree.
Latest
US$6.969 trillion at PPP in 2024, fourth in the world after China, the United States and India; Japan is fifth at US$6.714 trillion (World Bank WDI (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
Fourth in the World Bank’s 2024 data, the rank named in the target.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

R&D spending of at least 2% of GDP by 2030

Target
увеличение к 2030 году внутренних затрат на исследования и разработки не менее чем до 2 процентов валового внутреннего продукта (“increasing domestic R&D spending to at least 2% of GDP by 2030”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab), para. 7(г))
Baseline
Not stated in the decree.
Latest
наукоемкости российской экономики (0,96%) (“R&D intensity of the Russian economy (0.96%)”), data for 2023 (HSE ISSEK, 17 September 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
1.04 percentage points short of the 2% target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 2 − 0.96).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

GHG emissions at no more than 70% of 1990 by 2030

Target
The Climate Doctrine restates the goal set by Decree 666: цель ограничить к 2030 году выбросы парниковых газов до 70 процентов от уровня 1990 года (не более 2162,4 млн. тонн СО2-эквивалента) (“the goal of limiting greenhouse gas emissions by 2030 to 70% of the 1990 level (no more than 2,162.4 million tonnes CO2-equivalent)”) (Climate Doctrine (opens in a new tab), para. 18)
Baseline
The same paragraph gives 2020 emissions of 1482,2 млн. тонн СО2-эквивалента (48 процентов от уровня 1990 года) (“1,482.2 million tonnes CO2-equivalent (48% of the 1990 level)”).
Latest
2024: 2,137.3 Mt CO2-eq excluding land use and forestry, and 1,021.3 Mt including it (Russia’s GHG inventory, 2026 (opens in a new tab), Table Р.2).
Distance
Not computed. The doctrine does not say which inventory basis its 2,162.4 Mt cap uses, and each inventory recalculates the series.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

A balance of emissions and removals by 2060

Target
Ключевой долгосрочной целью климатической политики является достижение с учетом национальных интересов и приоритетов социально-экономического развития не позднее 2060 года баланса между антропогенными выбросами парниковых газов и их поглощением. (“The key long-term goal of climate policy is to achieve, taking account of national interests and socio-economic development priorities, a balance between anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions and their removal by 2060 at the latest.”) (Climate Doctrine (opens in a new tab), para. 21)
Baseline
Not stated in the doctrine.
Latest
Net emissions including land use and forestry were 1,021.3 Mt CO2-eq in 2024, after net removals of 1,116.1 Mt (Russia’s GHG inventory, 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
Not computed. The doctrine sets no figure for the 2060 balance.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

No more than 50% of municipal waste landfilled by 2030

Target
сортировку 100 процентов объема ежегодно образуемых твердых коммунальных отходов, захоронение не более чем 50 процентов таких отходов (“sorting 100% of the municipal solid waste generated each year, and landfilling no more than 50% of it”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab), para. 5(а))
Baseline
Not stated in the decree.
Latest
По данным РЭО на октябрь 2024, доля ТКО, направленных на обработку, составила 54,8%, на утилизацию и обезвреживание отходов — 13,7%, на захоронение ТКО — 83,9%. (“According to REO, as of October 2024 the share of municipal solid waste sent for processing was 54.8%, for recycling and neutralisation 13.7%, and for landfill 83.9%.”) (REO news post (opens in a new tab)). REO’s “processing” is not the same measure as the decree’s “sorting”.
Distance
33.9 percentage points above the target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 50 − 83.9)
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

At least 33 m² of housing per person by 2030

Target
обеспечение граждан жильем общей площадью не менее 33 кв. метров на человека к 2030 году и не менее 38 кв. метров к 2036 году (“providing citizens with housing of at least 33 m² per person by 2030 and at least 38 m² by 2036”) (Decree 309 (opens in a new tab), para. 4(б))
Baseline
28.8 m² per person in 2023 (Rosstat, Housing in Russia 2025 (opens in a new tab), Table 3.2).
Latest
29.4 m² per person in 2024 (Rosstat, Housing in Russia 2025 (opens in a new tab), Table 3.2).
Distance
3.6 m² per person short of the 2030 target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 33 − 29.4)
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Sources checked 26 and 31 October 2026. Russian quotations are given as published; English translations are unofficial.

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
