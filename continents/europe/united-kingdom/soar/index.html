A Aspirations

The United Kingdom has no single codified long-term national vision. Long-term aims sit in law (the Climate Change Act 2008 and the carbon budget orders) and in government programme documents, chiefly the Plan for Change of December 2024, published under the 2024–2026 Starmer government. Andy Burnham, Prime Minister since 20 July 2026, has promised a new 10-year plan, which had not been published on 20 October 2026. The Aspirations below quote those texts, each labelled with its status.

The current position: a promised 10-year plan

Speech: Prime Minister Andy Burnham, 20 July 2026. A promise; no plan published as of 20 October 2026

Later this year, I will bring forward a new plan for Britain, a 10-year plan, laying out a path from where we are now to where I believe we all want Britain to be, wherever we’re coming from, whatever party we support. (GOV.UK, Andy Burnham’s first speech as Prime Minister (opens in a new tab)).

Net zero by 2050 and the carbon budgets

Law: Climate Change Act 2008, s.1 (in force; 100% since 27 June 2019); Carbon Budget Orders 2021 and 2026 (in force)

It is the duty of the Secretary of State to ensure that the net UK carbon account for the year 2050 is at least 100% lower than the 1990 baseline. (Climate Change Act 2008, s.1(1) (opens in a new tab)). The carbon budget orders set limits for the five-year periods on the way:

  1. The carbon budget for the 2033-2037 budgetary period is 965,000,000 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent ([[src:uk-leg-si-2021-750|SI 2021/750]], art. 2)
  2. The carbon budget for the 2038-2042 budgetary period is 535,000,000 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent ([[src:uk-leg-si-2026-695|SI 2026/695]], art. 2)

Plan for Change: five missions (December 2024)

Command Paper: Plan for Change, CP 1210, presented to Parliament on 5 December 2024 by the Prime Minister’s Office (published under the 2024 to 2026 Starmer Labour government)

The Government has five missions to deliver a decade of national renewal: kickstart economic growth, build an NHS fit for the future, safer streets, break down the barriers to opportunity and make Britain a clean energy superpower. These missions reflect the priorities of working people. (Plan for Change, CP 1210 (opens in a new tab), p.11). The milestones:

  1. Through our milestones, we will deliver higher living standards in every part of the United Kingdom, 1.5 million homes built and planning decisions fast-tracked, hospital backlogs ended, police back on the beat, children starting school ready to learn, and secure, home-grown energy. (p.11)
  2. Our milestones for change can only be delivered on the foundations of a stable economy, secure borders and national security. (p.14)

GOV.UK’s missions page is no longer available, and no official statement withdrawing the missions was found; whether the present government keeps, revises or replaces them is not confirmed (TBC).

Plan for Change: growth

Command Paper: Plan for Change, CP 1210, presented to Parliament on 5 December 2024 by the Prime Minister’s Office (published under the 2024 to 2026 Starmer Labour government)

Economic growth is the number one mission of the Government. Through our growth mission, this Government will aim for the highest sustained growth in the G7 – with more people in good jobs, higher living standards and productivity growth in every part of the United Kingdom. (Plan for Change, CP 1210 (opens in a new tab), p.19). Milestone: Building 1.5 million homes in England and fast-tracking planning decisions on at least 150 major economic infrastructure projects – more than were decided in the last 14 years combined. (p.12).

Plan for Change: clean energy

Command Paper: Plan for Change, CP 1210, presented to Parliament on 5 December 2024 by the Prime Minister’s Office (published under the 2024 to 2026 Starmer Labour government)

Milestone: Securing home-grown energy, protecting billpayers and putting us on track to at least 95% clean power by 2030, while accelerating the UK to net zero. (Plan for Change, CP 1210 (opens in a new tab), p.13). Mission: We will achieve this through delivering clean power by 2030 and accelerating to net zero. (p.39).

Plan for Change: NHS waiting times (England)

Command Paper: Plan for Change, CP 1210, presented to Parliament on 5 December 2024 by the Prime Minister’s Office (published under the 2024 to 2026 Starmer Labour government)

Milestone: Ending hospital backlogs to meet the NHS standard of 92% of patients in England waiting no longer than 18 weeks for elective treatment. (Plan for Change, CP 1210 (opens in a new tab), p.12).

Industrial Strategy: a 10-year plan

Policy paper: The UK’s Modern Industrial Strategy, CP 1451, Department for Business and Trade, 23 June 2025 (published under the 2024 to 2026 Starmer Labour government; quarterly updates continued in October 2026)

It is robust, strategic, and unashamedly long-term: a 10-year plan to make the UK the best country to invest in anywhere in the world. Our ambition is for this partnership to significantly increase business investment by 2035. (Industrial Strategy, CP 1451 (opens in a new tab), PDF p.8 and p.20).

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.
