Targets and latest values

Greenhouse gas emissions at least 65% below 1990 by 2030

Target
Die Treibhausgasemissionen werden im Vergleich zum Jahr 1990 schrittweise wie folgt gemindert: 1. bis zum Jahr 2030 um mindestens 65 Prozent, 2. bis zum Jahr 2040 um mindestens 88 Prozent. (unofficial translation: “Greenhouse gas emissions will be reduced step by step compared with 1990 as follows: 1. by at least 65 percent by 2030, 2. by at least 88 percent by 2040.”) (KSG § 3(1) (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
1990 (the law’s base year); the 1990 total is not quoted here.
Latest
2025: …sanken bis 2025 um rund 604 Millionen Tonnen (Mio. t) oder 48,2 %. Für das Jahr 2025 wurden Gesamt-Emissionen in Höhe von 649 Mio. t berichtet. Die Emissionen sinken um 0,1 % gegenüber dem Jahr 2024. (unofficial translation: “…fell by about 604 million tonnes or 48.2% by 2025. Total emissions of 649 million tonnes were reported for 2025. Emissions fall by 0.1% compared with 2024.”) (UBA, updated 2 Jul 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
16.8 percentage points short of the target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 65 − 48.2)

Target

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
