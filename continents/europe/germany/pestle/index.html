Political 4 points

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • The greatest threat, in the government’s words. Das heutige Russland ist auf absehbare Zeit die größte Bedrohung für Frieden und Sicherheit im euroatlantischen Raum. (unofficial translation: “Today’s Russia is, for the foreseeable future, the greatest threat to peace and security in the Euro-Atlantic area.”) (Nationale Sicherheitsstrategie, June 2023 (opens in a new tab), p. 23). Whether this 2023 strategy is still the current one has not been checked (TBC).
  • US$113.6 billion. Germany spent US$113.6 billion on its military in 2025, 3.9% of the world total and second in Europe after Russia (SIPRI Military Expenditure Database (opens in a new tab)).
  • A special fund for the armed forces. Mit Hilfe des Sondervermögens werden im mehrjährigen Durchschnitt von maximal fünf Jahren 2 Prozent des Bruttoinlandsprodukts auf Basis der aktuellen Regierungsprognose für Verteidigungsausgaben nach NATO-Kriterien bereitgestellt. (unofficial translation: “With the help of the special fund, 2 percent of GDP, based on the current government forecast, is provided for defence spending by NATO criteria, as an average over a period of at most five years.”) (BwFinSVermG § 1 (opens in a new tab)). Spending as a share of GDP in 2025 has not been checked at source (TBC).

Governance and cooperation

  • The first national security strategy. Oberste Aufgabe deutscher Sicherheitspolitik ist es sicherzustellen, dass wir in unserem Land auch künftig in Frieden, Freiheit und Sicherheit leben können. (unofficial translation: “The highest task of German security policy is to ensure that we can continue to live in peace, freedom and security in our country.”) (Nationale Sicherheitsstrategie, 2023 (opens in a new tab), p. 14)

Economic 4 points

Economy and finance

  • US$5.1 trillion. GDP was US$5,115.6 billion in 2025, the largest in Europe and third in the world after the United States and China (IMF World Economic Outlook, October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • EUR 231.2 billion. Germany’s towns and districts report a backlog of about EUR 231.2 billion to bring their infrastructure back to an adequate state. Das sind rund 15,5 Mrd. EUR oder 7,2 % mehr als noch im Vorjahr (unofficial translation: “That is about EUR 15.5 billion or 7.2% more than the year before”) (KfW-Kommunalpanel 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · The largest economy in Europe · SWOT · Weaknesses · A backlog of local investment

Natural resources

  • Early-warning stage since July 2025. The Bundesnetzagentur reports that the gas emergency plan stays at its early-warning stage, which has applied since 1 July 2025. Über Pipelines wurden vom 03.11.26-09.11.26 insgesamt 18,1 TWh importiert. (unofficial translation: “18.1 TWh were imported through pipelines from 3 to 9 November 2026.”) (Bundesnetzagentur, 11 November 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • Storage lower than in past years. Die Füllstände der Speicher in Deutschland liegen derzeit bei 58,5 % (144,7 TWh ). Sie sind deutlich niedriger als in den Vergleichszeiträumen vergangener Jahre. (unofficial translation: “Storage in Germany is currently 58.5% full (144.7 TWh). This is clearly lower than in the same periods of past years.”) (Bundesnetzagentur (opens in a new tab))

Social 5 points

Population and demographics

  • A median age of 45.5. Half of Germany’s people were older than 45.5 years in 2025, against 30.9 for the world. That is 8th oldest in Europe (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Fewer births. Die zusammengefasste Geburtenziffer in Deutschland, oft als Geburtenrate bezeichnet, war im Jahr 2025 mit 1,32 Kindern je Frau um 2,7 % niedriger als im Vorjahr (1,35 Kindern je Frau). (unofficial translation: “The total fertility rate in Germany, often called the birth rate, was 1.32 children per woman in 2025, 2.7% lower than the year before (1.35 children per woman).”) (Destatis, 1 July 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • The Opportunity Card. The law lets skilled people from outside the EU come to look for work: Eine Chancenkarte ist eine Aufenthaltserlaubnis zur Suche nach einer Erwerbstätigkeit oder nach Maßnahmen zur Anerkennung ausländischer Berufsqualifikationen. (unofficial translation: “An Opportunity Card is a residence permit to look for work or for steps to have foreign professional qualifications recognised.”) (AufenthG § 20a(1) (opens in a new tab)). The number of cards issued has not been checked (TBC).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · An ageing population · SWOT · Opportunities · Skilled workers from abroad

Human development

  • 5th in the world. Germany’s Human Development Index was 0.959 in 2023, 5th of 193 countries (UNDP Human Development Report 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
  • 81.7 years. Life expectancy at birth in 2025 (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

Technological 5 points

Technology and AI

  • 3.17% of GDP. Die Ausgaben für Forschung und Entwicklung in Deutschland sind im Jahr 2024 um 3,8 % gegenüber dem Vorjahr auf 137,1 Milliarden Euro gestiegen. (unofficial translation: “Spending on research and development in Germany rose by 3.8% in 2024 to EUR 137.1 billion.”) Its share of GDP was 3.17%, the highest since the series began in 1995 (Destatis, 27 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Half the EU average. FTTP availability is limited, covering only a fraction of households, representing half of the EU average coverage. (European Commission, Germany 2025 Digital Decade Country Report (opens in a new tab)). FTTP means fibre to the premises.
  • EUR 32 billion across the EU. The Commission has already approved 18 State aid decisions on first-of-a-kind semiconductor facilities that represent a total public and private investment of over EUR 32 billion (European Commission, European Chips Act (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Record research spending · SWOT · Weaknesses · Slow fibre broadband · SWOT · Opportunities · EU support for chip factories

Energy transition

  • 55.1% in 2025. Während sich der Anteil der erneuerbaren Energien am Bruttostromverbrauch in den letzten 10 Jahren fast verdoppelte und im Jahr 2025 bei 55,1 Prozent lag, verändern sich die Anteile in den Bereichen Wärme (19,2 Prozent) und Verkehr (8,0 Prozent) vergleichsweise langsam. (unofficial translation: “While the share of renewables in gross electricity consumption almost doubled over the last 10 years and stood at 55.1 percent in 2025, the shares in heating (19.2 percent) and transport (8.0 percent) are changing comparatively slowly.”) (UBA, Erneuerbare Energien in Zahlen (opens in a new tab))
  • The 2030 target is 80%. The Renewable Energy Sources Act sets at least 80% of gross electricity consumption by 2030 (EEG 2023, § 1 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · More than half of electricity from renewables

Legal 4 points

Economy and finance

  • The debt brake. Diesem Grundsatz ist entsprochen, wenn die Einnahmen aus Krediten 0,35 vom Hundert im Verhältnis zum nominalen Bruttoinlandsprodukt nicht überschreiten. (unofficial translation: “This principle is met if revenue from loans does not exceed 0.35 percent of nominal gross domestic product.”) (Grundgesetz, Art. 115(2) (opens in a new tab))
  • A EUR 500 billion fund outside the debt brake. Der Bund kann ein Sondervermögen mit eigener Kreditermächtigung für zusätzliche Investitionen in die Infrastruktur und für zusätzliche Investitionen zur Erreichung der Klimaneutralität bis zum Jahr 2045 mit einem Volumen von bis zu 500 Milliarden Euro errichten. (unofficial translation: “The Federation may set up a special fund with its own borrowing authority, of up to 500 billion euros, for additional investment in infrastructure and additional investment to reach climate neutrality by 2045.”) (Grundgesetz, Art. 143h(1) (opens in a new tab)). The same article says the debt-brake rules of Articles 109(3) and 115(2) do not apply to its borrowing.

Climate change / global warming

  • A state objective in the constitution. Der Staat schützt auch in Verantwortung für die künftigen Generationen die natürlichen Lebensgrundlagen und die Tiere im Rahmen der verfassungsmäßigen Ordnung durch die Gesetzgebung und nach Maßgabe von Gesetz und Recht durch die vollziehende Gewalt und die Rechtsprechung. (unofficial translation: “Mindful also of its responsibility towards future generations, the state shall protect the natural foundations of life and animals by legislation and, in accordance with law and justice, by executive and judicial action, all within the framework of the constitutional order.”) (Grundgesetz, Art. 20a (opens in a new tab))
  • Net zero by 2045 in law. Bis zum Jahr 2045 werden die Treibhausgasemissionen so weit gemindert, dass Netto-Treibhausgasneutralität erreicht wird. (unofficial translation: “By 2045, greenhouse gas emissions will be reduced so far that net greenhouse gas neutrality is achieved.”) (KSG § 3(2) (opens in a new tab)).

Environmental 3 points

Climate change / global warming

  • 94,896 Mt since 1850. Germany has emitted 94,896 Mt of fossil CO2 since 1850, 5.1% of the world total and second in Europe after Russia (Hawkeye’s arithmetic, Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
  • 6.8 tonnes a person. 572 Mt in 2024, 6.8 tonnes per person, against a world average of 4.7 tonnes (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · A large share of past emissions

Natural and cosmic hazards

  • The 2018 low water. Die im Rheingebiet transportierte Güterfracht ging im Vergleich zum Jahr 2017 um 11,8 % zurück. (unofficial translation: “Freight carried in the Rhine area fell by 11.8% compared with 2017.”) Barges could in der Regel nur teilbeladen sail, that is, as a rule only part-loaded (BfG, low water of 2018 (opens in a new tab)). This is one documented event. A trend has not been checked at source.

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
