A Aspirations

Germany has no single national vision document. The Aspirations below come from the Basic Law, the Federal Climate Change Act and the 2025 coalition agreement of the current government, each labelled with its status. German quotations are given as published. English translations are unofficial.

The government’s programme

Coalition agreement of CDU, CSU and SPD, 2025, a political agreement, not a law

Deutschland steht vor historischen Herausforderungen. Die Politik der kommenden Jahre wird maßgeblich darüber entscheiden, ob wir auch in Zukunft in einem freien, sicheren, gerechten und wohlhabenden Deutschland leben. (unofficial translation: “Germany faces historic challenges. The policy of the coming years will largely decide whether we continue to live in a free, secure, fair and prosperous Germany.”) (Koalitionsvertrag 2025 (opens in a new tab), Präambel)

An industrial country that becomes climate neutral

Coalition agreement of CDU, CSU and SPD, 2025

Wir wollen Industrieland bleiben und klimaneutral werden. (unofficial translation: “We want to remain an industrial country and become climate neutral.”) (Koalitionsvertrag 2025 (opens in a new tab), section 1.4)

Net zero by 2045

Federal Climate Change Act (KSG), §, 3, binding law

Bis zum Jahr 2045 werden die Treibhausgasemissionen so weit gemindert, dass Netto-Treibhausgasneutralität erreicht wird. (unofficial translation: “By 2045, greenhouse gas emissions will be reduced so far that net greenhouse gas neutrality is achieved.”) (KSG § 3(2) (opens in a new tab)).

The 2030 and 2040 targets are quoted on the GAP page.

Protecting the natural foundations of life

Basic Law, Article 20a, a state objective in the constitution

Der Staat schützt auch in Verantwortung für die künftigen Generationen die natürlichen Lebensgrundlagen und die Tiere im Rahmen der verfassungsmäßigen Ordnung durch die Gesetzgebung und nach Maßgabe von Gesetz und Recht durch die vollziehende Gewalt und die Rechtsprechung. (unofficial translation: “Mindful also of its responsibility towards future generations, the state shall protect the natural foundations of life and animals by legislation and, in accordance with law and justice, by executive and judicial action, all within the framework of the constitutional order.”) (Grundgesetz, Art. 20a (opens in a new tab))

Investment in infrastructure and climate neutrality

Basic Law, Article 143h

Der Bund kann ein Sondervermögen mit eigener Kreditermächtigung für zusätzliche Investitionen in die Infrastruktur und für zusätzliche Investitionen zur Erreichung der Klimaneutralität bis zum Jahr 2045 mit einem Volumen von bis zu 500 Milliarden Euro errichten. (unofficial translation: “The Federation may set up a special fund with its own borrowing authority, of up to 500 billion euros, for additional investment in infrastructure and additional investment to reach climate neutrality by 2045.”) (Grundgesetz, Art. 143h(1) (opens in a new tab))

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.
