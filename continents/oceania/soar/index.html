A Aspirations

The Aspirations below are the Pacific Islands Forum’s own, from its 2050 Strategy. The Forum’s 18 members include Australia and New Zealand and two French territories.

A resilient Pacific region

Vision of the 2050 Strategy, endorsed by Forum Leaders in Suva in 2022

As Pacific Leaders, our vision is for a resilient Pacific Region of peace, harmony, security, social inclusion and prosperity, that ensures all Pacific peoples can lead free, healthy and productive lives. (2050 Strategy (opens in a new tab), p. 6)

Net zero by 2050, with partners’ support

Leaders’ commitment vii of the 2050 Strategy

and with the support of our partners, we commit to ensuring net zero carbon emissions by 2050. (2050 Strategy (opens in a new tab), p. 10)

The commitment depends on partners’ support and does not set a pathway.

Resilience to climate change and disasters

Level of ambition for the Climate Change and Disasters thematic area

All Pacific Peoples remain resilient to the impacts of climate change and disasters and are able to lead safe, secure and prosperous lives. The region continues to play a leadership role in global climate action. (2050 Strategy Implementation Plan 2023–2030 (opens in a new tab), p. 26; also on the Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard (opens in a new tab))

Seven thematic areas

Structure of the 2050 Strategy

The Strategy groups its ambitions under seven thematic areas (2050 Strategy (opens in a new tab), p. 12):

  1. Political Leadership and Regionalism
  2. People-Centered Development
  3. Peace and Security
  4. Resource and Economic Development
  5. Climate Change and Disasters
  6. Ocean and Environment
  7. Technology and Connectivity

How this SOAR analysis works

For a continent, the Aspirations are goal statements of the regional and treaty bodies its countries belong to, quoted word for word and labelled with each body’s members.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
