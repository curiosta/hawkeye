The targets below are the Pacific Islands Forum’s own, from the Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard, which supplement the 2024 Baseline Report to Leaders, delivered to the 53rd Pacific Islands Forum Leaders Meeting hosted by Tonga. (Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard (opens in a new tab)). The dashboard’s own progress ratings could not be read as text, so none is shown. Values are for the Pacific islands (Oceania excluding Australia and New Zealand) unless stated.

Targets and latest values

Child deaths under 25 per 1,000

Target
Reduce deaths to children under 5 years of age to under 25 per 1,000 live births (Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard, People Centred Development, outcome 1.0 (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
50.3 per 1,000 live births (2015) (UN SDG Global Database, SH_DYN_MORT (opens in a new tab))
Latest
36.2 per 1,000 (2024); Oceania as a whole 18.9 (UN SDG Global Database (opens in a new tab))
Distance
11.2 per 1,000 above the target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 25 − 36.2)
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published as text

Premature chronic-disease deaths down by a third

Target
Reduce premature deaths from Non-communicable Diseases (NCDs) by at least one-third (Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard, outcome 1.0 (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
32.9% chance of dying between 30 and 70 from the four main NCDs (2015) (UN SDG Global Database, SH_DTH_NCOM (opens in a new tab))
Latest
30.9% (2021); Australia and New Zealand 8.6% (UN SDG Global Database (opens in a new tab))
Distance
The dashboard does not state a base year. Measured from 2015, a one-third cut would mean about 21.9%; the 2021 value is 9.0 points above that (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published as text

Universal safe drinking water

Target
Universal access to safe drinking water services (Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard, outcome 1.2 (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
56.6% with at least a basic drinking water service (2015) (UN SDG Global Database, SP_ACS_BSRVH2O (opens in a new tab))
Latest
62.6% (2024); rural 53.6% (UN SDG Global Database (opens in a new tab))
Distance
37.4 points short of universal access on the at-least-basic measure (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 100 − 62.6). The safely managed share, which matches “safe” more closely, is not published for this group (TBC).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published as text

87% reading at the expected level

Target
At least 87% of children achieving minimum proficiency in literacy, end primary (Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard, outcome 2.0 (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
About 63% of year six students at or above the expected level in PILNA 2018 (PILNA 2018 regional report)
Latest
53% of year six students met the minimum expected reading level in PILNA 2021 (SPC, PILNA 2021 (opens in a new tab))
Distance
34.0 percentage points short of the target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 87 − 53)
Assessment by the tracking agency
SPC notes that the decrease from 2018 may not be significant (PILNA 2021 (opens in a new tab))

Growth of at least 3.5%

Target
GDP growth rate, at least 2023 Pacific average of 3.5% (Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard, Resource and Economic Development, outcome 1.0 (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
3.5% (2023), as stated in the target
Latest
Forecasts of 3% for 2026 and 2.9% for 2027 for ADB’s Pacific developing members (ADB, 23 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The 2025 outturn is TBC.
Distance
The 2026 forecast is 0.5 points below the target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). These are forecasts, not outturns.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Everyone online

Target
All individuals using the Internet (Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard, Technology and Connectivity, outcome 2.0 (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
12.2% (2015) (UN SDG Global Database, IT_USE_ii99 (opens in a new tab))
Latest
25.6% (2024); Australia and New Zealand 95.7% (UN SDG Global Database (opens in a new tab))
Distance
74.4 percentage points short of the target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 100 − 25.6)
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published as text

Renewable share of energy

Target
No numeric target. The dashboard tracks Renewable energy share in the total final energy consumption under its climate mitigation outcome 1.1 (Blue Pacific 2050 Dashboard (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
36.88% (2015) (UN SDG Global Database, EG_FEC_RNEW (opens in a new tab))
Latest
34.65% (2023); Oceania as a whole 17.63% (UN SDG Global Database (opens in a new tab))
Distance
Not applicable. No numeric target has been set.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Sources checked 19 October 2026. Figures for the Pacific islands use the UN group Oceania excluding Australia and New Zealand. Sources that cover a wider region are labelled.

How this GAP analysis works

For a continent, the targets are those set by the regional or treaty bodies that govern it, plus the global targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
