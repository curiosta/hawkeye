Documented national responses 9

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Medium Term Development Plan IV, 2023–2027

    Adopted by Government of Papua New Guinea (Department of National Planning and Monitoring), launched 7 July 2023

    Twelve Strategic Priority Areas aligned with Vision 2050 (MTDP IV (opens in a new tab), PDF p. 46). Quotes on the PESTLE and SOAR pages.

    Related GAP · A K200 billion economy and one million jobs by 2030

  • Environment Act 2000

    Adopted by National Parliament, Environment Act 2000 (No. 64 of 2000)

    Section 4(c) sets the aim to sustain the potential of natural and physical resources to meet the reasonably foreseeable needs of future generations, and safeguard the life-supporting capacity of air, water, land and eco-systems (Environment Act 2000, as enacted (opens in a new tab)).

  • Monetary policy mandate

    Adopted by National Parliament, Central Banking Act 2000 (consolidated to No 17 of 2021)

    Section 7(a) directs monetary policy at price stability and at promoting employment and growth, especially of the non-mineral and non-petroleum sector (Central Banking Act 2000 (opens in a new tab)). Quote on the PESTLE page.

  • Climate change management law

    Adopted by National Parliament, Climate Change (Management) Act 2015 (Act 19 of 2015), amended by Act 25 of 2021

    A regulatory framework for climate-compatible development and for implementing the UNFCCC and the Paris Agreement (Climate Change (Management) Act 2015, consolidated (opens in a new tab)). Quote on the PESTLE page.

  • National Forest Policy, 1991

    Adopted by Government of Papua New Guinea (PNG Forest Authority, National Forest Plan)

    Based on the Constitution’s fourth National Goal; main objective (i): Management and protection of the nation’s forest resource as a renewable natural assets. (National Forest Plan (opens in a new tab), s 2.0).

    Related SWOT · Strengths · Large forests · SWOT · Strengths · Land held under customary tenure

  • Fisheries management law

    Adopted by National Parliament, Fisheries Management Act 1998 (No. 48 of 1998)

    Section 25(b): conserve the living resources for both present and future generations; (Fisheries Management Act 1998 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · A share of the world’s largest tuna fishery

  • Vision 2050

    Adopted by Government of Papua New Guinea (National Strategic Plan Taskforce), first published November 2009

    ‘We will be a Smart, Wise, Fair, Healthy and Happy Society by 2050.’ (Vision 2050 (opens in a new tab), s 4). More on the SOAR page.

    Related GAP · Top 50 in the Human Development Index by 2050

  • Disaster management

    Adopted by National Parliament, Disaster Management Act 1984 (Chapter 403)

    Section 1: The purpose of this Act is to establish a machinery for forward planning to ensure the efficient, prompt and effective management and control of natural disasters in the country. (Disaster Management Act 1984 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Landslides

  • Enhanced Nationally Determined Contribution

    Adopted by Government of Papua New Guinea (Climate Change and Development Authority), December 2020; conditional on international support

    The contributions outlined in this NDC are all conditional. Targets for forest and land-use emissions and for renewable capacity (Enhanced NDC, 2020 (opens in a new tab)). A draft third NDC was validated in June 2026; Hawkeye has not confirmed that it has been submitted.

    Related GAP · Renewable share of on-grid capacity · GAP · Forest and land-use emissions

Sources checked 18–19 October 2026. Quotations are given as published.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.
