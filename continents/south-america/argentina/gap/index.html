Targets and latest values

Renewable electricity

Target
20% of national electricity consumption from renewable sources by 31 December 2025. …lograr una contribución de las fuentes renovables de energía hasta alcanzar el veinte por ciento (20%) del consumo de energía eléctrica nacional, al 31 de diciembre de 2025. (unofficial translation: “…to achieve a contribution from renewable energy sources reaching twenty per cent (20%) of national electricity consumption by 31 December 2025.”) (Ley 27.191 (opens in a new tab), Art. 5)
Baseline
Not sourced yet
Latest
18.9% of demand in 2025 (26,663 GWh of renewable generation) (CAMMESA, Resumen Anual 2025 (opens in a new tab)): “Renovables MEM/ Dem MEM 16.3% 18.9%” (2024, 2025).
Distance
1.1 percentage points below the target at its deadline (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 20 − 18.9)
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Greenhouse gas emissions

Target
Net emissions of no more than 375 MtCO2e in 2030 and in 2035. La NDC 3.0 de la República Argentina consiste en una meta de emisiones netas para 2030 y 2035 que no supera las 375 MtCO2e. (unofficial translation: “Argentina’s NDC 3.0 consists of a net emissions target for 2030 and 2035 that does not exceed 375 MtCO2e.”) (third NDC, announced 3 November 2025 (opens in a new tab); Hawkeye does not show whether it has been lodged with the UNFCCC.)
Baseline
Not sourced yet
Latest
401 MtCO2e total net emissions in 2022 (Booklet INGEI 2024 (opens in a new tab)). The same booklet gives 378 MtCO2e for 2022 when adjusted for comparison with the earlier 349 MtCO2e target: “El valor para 2022 es de 378 MtCO2e.” (unofficial translation: “The value for 2022 is 378 MtCO2e.”)
Distance
Not computed. The sources do not state that the inventory total and the new target are on the same accounting basis.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Fiscal balance

Target
A balanced or surplus result for the national budget at the close of 2026. Establécese que el Presupuesto General de la Administración Nacional, al cierre del Ejercicio Fiscal 2026, deberá presentar una ejecución con resultado financiero equilibrado o superavitario. (unofficial translation: “The General Budget of the National Administration, at the close of Fiscal Year 2026, must show a balanced or surplus financial result.”) (Ley 27.798 (opens in a new tab), Art. 1)
Baseline
Deficit of 4.6% of GDP in 2023 (Síntesis del Proyecto de Presupuesto 2025 (opens in a new tab))
Latest
Surplus of about 0.2% of GDP in 2025, after 0.3% in 2024, for the wider national public sector, cash basis (Ministerio de Economía, 16 January 2026 (opens in a new tab); 17 January 2025 (opens in a new tab))
Distance
Not computed. The target is for fiscal year 2026 and for the National Administration budget; the latest results are for earlier years and for the wider national public sector.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Native forests

Target
Zoning of native forests into conservation categories. Las categorías de conservación de los bosques nativos son las siguientes: - Categoría I (rojo): sectores de muy alto valor de conservación que no deben transformarse. (unofficial translation: “The conservation categories for native forests are as follows: Category I (red): areas of very high conservation value that must not be transformed.”) (Ley 26.331 (opens in a new tab), Art. 9; categories II yellow and III green allow sustainable use and partial or total transformation within the law)
Baseline
728,469 ha lost in 2007, four historical regions (monitoring report 2024 (opens in a new tab), Tabla 2)
Latest
165,061 ha lost in 2024, all regions; 157,559 ha in the four historical regions (monitoring report 2024 (opens in a new tab), Tabla 2)
Distance
No numeric target. The law zones forests; it sets no national figure for allowed loss.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Poverty (SDG 1.2)

Target
“1.2 By 2030, reduce at least by half the proportion of men, women and children of all ages living in poverty in all its dimensions according to national definitions” (UN SDG global indicator framework (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
Not sourced yet
Latest
32.3% of people below the national poverty line in the first half of 2026, 31 urban areas; 52.9% in the first half of 2024 (INDEC (opens in a new tab))
Distance
Not computed. No baseline year has been confirmed at source, so the halving point is not known.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Drinking water (SDG 6.1)

Target
“6.1 By 2030, achieve universal and equitable access to safe and affordable drinking water for all” (UN SDG global indicator framework (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
Not sourced yet
Latest
85.4% of occupied private dwellings use water from the public network for drinking and cooking; 92.3% have piped water inside the dwelling (2022) (INDEC, Censo 2022 (opens in a new tab), Cuadro 1)
Distance
Not computed. The census measures connection and piping, which is not the same as the target’s “safe and affordable” wording.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

Target

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
