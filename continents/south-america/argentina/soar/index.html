A Aspirations

Argentina has no single statutory long-term national vision document. The Aspirations below are three official, dated documents, each labelled with its legal status.

Pacto de Mayo (May Pact)

Political pact, not a law

Signed on 9 July 2024 at the Casa Histórica in San Miguel de Tucumán by President Javier Milei, the governors of 17 provinces and the head of government of the City of Buenos Aires (Casa Rosada, 9 July 2024 (opens in a new tab)). Decreto 617/2024 created the Consejo de Mayo to implement it (Boletín Oficial (opens in a new tab)). Its ten principles, as published:

  1. La inviolabilidad de la propiedad privada. (unofficial translation: “The inviolability of private property.”)
  2. El equilibrio fiscal innegociable. (unofficial translation: “Non-negotiable fiscal balance.”)
  3. La reducción del gasto público a niveles históricos, en torno al 25% del Producto Bruto Interno. (unofficial translation: “Reducing public spending to historical levels, around 25% of Gross Domestic Product.”)
  4. Una educación inicial, primaria y secundaria útil y moderna, con alfabetización plena y sin abandono escolar. (unofficial translation: “Useful and modern pre-school, primary and secondary education, with full literacy and no school dropout.”)
  5. Una reforma tributaria que reduzca la presión impositiva, simplifique la vida de los argentinos y promueva el comercio. (unofficial translation: “A tax reform that lowers the tax burden, simplifies Argentines’ lives and promotes trade.”)
  6. La rediscusión de la coparticipación federal de impuestos para terminar para poner fin al modelo extorsivo actual que padecen las provincias. (unofficial translation: “Reopening the federal sharing of tax revenue to end the current extortionate model the provinces suffer.”)
  7. El compromiso de las provincias argentinas de avanzar en la explotación de los recursos naturales del país. (unofficial translation: “The commitment of the Argentine provinces to move forward with developing the country’s natural resources.”)
  8. Una reforma laboral moderna que promueva el trabajo formal. (unofficial translation: “A modern labour reform that promotes formal work.”)
  9. Una reforma previsional que le dé sostenibilidad al sistema y respete a quienes aportaron. (unofficial translation: “A pension reform that makes the system sustainable and respects those who paid in.”)
  10. La apertura al comercio internacional, de manera que la Argentina vuelva a ser protagonista del mercado global. (unofficial translation: “Opening up to international trade, so that Argentina is once again a leading player in the global market.”)

Principle 6 is quoted as published, including the repeated “para terminar para poner fin” [sic].

2026 budget law: fiscal balance

Law (Ley 27.798, sanctioned 26 December 2025, published 2 January 2026)

(Ley 27.798 (opens in a new tab), Art. 1): Establécese que el Presupuesto General de la Administración Nacional, al cierre del Ejercicio Fiscal 2026, deberá presentar una ejecución con resultado financiero equilibrado o superavitario. (unofficial translation: “The General Budget of the National Administration, at the close of Fiscal Year 2026, must show a balanced or surplus financial result.”) The message sending the 2026 bill to Congress, dated 15 September 2025, said (Mensaje de Presupuesto 2026 (opens in a new tab)): El equilibrio fiscal se mantendrá por tercer año consecutivo, tal como se asumiera este compromiso inclaudicable del gobierno desde el primer día de gestión (unofficial translation: “Fiscal balance will be maintained for the third year in a row, as this unwavering commitment of the government was assumed from its first day in office”).

Third Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC 3.0)

Executive climate commitment, announced 3 November 2025

(argentina.gob.ar, 3 November 2025 (opens in a new tab)): La NDC 3.0 de la República Argentina consiste en una meta de emisiones netas para 2030 y 2035 que no supera las 375 MtCO2e. Ambas alcanzan todos los gases y las categorías de fuentes de emisión y sumidero reportadas en el Primer Informe Bienal de Transparencia (IBT1), que incluyó el inventario nacional de gases de efecto invernadero 2022 y fue presentado a la CMNUCC en diciembre de 2024. Este objetivo implica un desacople de la trayectoria histórica de emisiones respecto a la perspectiva de crecimiento económico y desarrollo nacional. (unofficial translation: “Argentina’s NDC 3.0 consists of a net emissions target for 2030 and 2035 that does not exceed 375 MtCO2e. Both cover all gases and the emission source and sink categories reported in the First Biennial Transparency Report (BTR1), which included the 2022 national greenhouse gas inventory and was submitted to the UNFCCC in December 2024. This objective implies decoupling the historical emissions path from the outlook for economic growth and national development.”)

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
