Documented national responses 11

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Large Investment Incentive Regime (RIGI)

    Adopted by Congress (Ley 27.742, Title VII), published in the Boletín Oficial on 8 July 2024; regulated by Decreto 749/2024 of 22 August 2024

    Incentives, a US$200 million minimum investment and a 30-year stability guarantee for single-project vehicles (Ley 27.742 (opens in a new tab), Arts. 164, 173, 178; Decreto 749/2024 (opens in a new tab)). Quotes on the SWOT page.

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Incentive regime for large investments (RIGI)

  • Economic deregulation

    Adopted by National Executive, Decree of Necessity and Urgency 70/2023

    (DNU 70/2023 (opens in a new tab), Art. 2): DESREGULACIÓN. El Estado Nacional promoverá y asegurará la vigencia efectiva, en todo el territorio nacional, de un sistema económico basado en decisiones libres, adoptadas en un ámbito de libre concurrencia, con respeto a la propiedad privada y a los principios constitucionales de libre circulación de bienes, servicios y trabajo. (unofficial translation: “DEREGULATION. The national State will promote and ensure, throughout the national territory, the effective operation of an economic system based on free decisions taken in an environment of free competition, respecting private property and the constitutional principles of free movement of goods, services and labour.”)

  • Fiscal anchor

    Adopted by National Executive, message sending the 2025 budget bill to Congress (MEN-2024-51-APN-JGM), 15 September 2024: the government’s stated anchor, not a statute

    (Mensaje de Presupuesto 2025 (opens in a new tab)): La estabilización macroeconómica se sustenta en el ancla fiscal, que consiste en garantizar el equilibrio financiero del Sector Público No Financiero, eliminando la necesidad del financiamiento del déficit vía endeudamiento neto o emisión monetaria. (unofficial translation: “Macroeconomic stabilisation rests on the fiscal anchor, which consists of guaranteeing the financial balance of the Non-Financial Public Sector, removing the need to finance the deficit through net borrowing or money issuance.”) For 2026 the budget law requires a balanced or surplus result (GAP).

    Related SWOT · Strengths · Public finances and slowing inflation · GAP · Fiscal balance

  • Asset regularisation regime

    Adopted by Congress (Ley 27.743, Title II), published in the Boletín Oficial on 8 July 2024

    Title II sets up the Régimen de Regularización de Activos (asset regularisation regime). Its Art. 28 table charges 0% in all three stages on the band “0 a 100.000, inclusive” US dollars, and 5%, 10% or 15% above that, by stage (Ley 27.743 (opens in a new tab), Art. 28).

  • Severance fund option

    Adopted by Congress (Ley 27.742, Art. 96), 8 July 2024

    (Ley 27.742 (opens in a new tab), Art. 96): Mediante convenio colectivo de trabajo, las partes podrán sustituir la indemnización prevista en el artículo 245 de la ley 20.744 por un fondo o sistema de cese laboral conforme los parámetros que disponga el Poder Ejecutivo nacional. (unofficial translation: “Through a collective labour agreement, the parties may replace the severance pay provided for in article 245 of law 20.744 with a severance fund or system under the parameters set by the national Executive.”)

  • Access to air transport markets

    Adopted by National Executive (Decreto 599/2024, Reglamento de Acceso a los Mercados Aerocomerciales), dated 8 July 2024, Boletín Oficial 10 July 2024

    (Decreto 599/2024 (opens in a new tab), Art. 25): DERECHOS DE TRÁFICO. Subsidiariamente, de forma unilateral, la autoridad competente, considerando la solicitud de prestación de servicios de transporte aéreo por parte de transportadores extranjeros podrá otorgar autorizaciones que comprendan derechos de tráfico de cabotaje, bajo condiciones de estricta reciprocidad. (unofficial translation: “TRAFFIC RIGHTS. Subsidiarily and unilaterally, the competent authority, considering requests from foreign carriers to provide air transport services, may grant authorisations that include cabotage traffic rights, under conditions of strict reciprocity.”)

  • Renewable electricity target

    Adopted by Congress (Ley 27.191), 2015

    Set a target of 20% of national electricity consumption from renewables by 31 December 2025 (Ley 27.191 (opens in a new tab), Art. 5). CAMMESA reports 18.9% for 2025 (GAP).

    Related GAP · Renewable electricity

  • Firm long-term LNG export authorisations

    Adopted by Congress (Ley 27.742, Art. 154, adding Art. 3° bis to Ley 24.076), 8 July 2024

    (Ley 27.742 (opens in a new tab), Art. 154): las autorizaciones de exportación de GNL que se otorguen tendrán carácter firme respecto de los volúmenes de GNL autorizados durante un plazo de hasta treinta (30) años, desde la puesta en marcha de la planta de licuefacción (en tierra o flotante) o sus ampliaciones o etapas sucesivas (unofficial translation: “LNG export authorisations granted will be firm for the authorised LNG volumes for a period of up to thirty (30) years from the start-up of the liquefaction plant (onshore or floating) or its expansions or successive stages”)

  • IMF Extended Fund Facility (2025)

    Adopted by Government of Argentina and the IMF Executive Board, approved 11 April 2025

    A 48-month arrangement of SDR 15.267 billion (about US$20 billion); about US$15.8 billion (SDR 11.452 billion) disbursed after the second review on 21 May 2026 (IMF Press Release 25/101 (opens in a new tab); 26/165 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · IMF financing (2025 Extended Fund Facility) · SWOT · Threats · Repayments due to the IMF

  • Second National Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation Plan

    Adopted by Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development (Resolución 146/2023), dated 20 April 2023, Boletín Oficial 24 April 2023

    (Resolución 146/2023 (opens in a new tab), Art. 1): Apruébase el segundo PLAN NACIONAL DE ADAPTACIÓN Y MITIGACIÓN AL CAMBIO CLIMÁTICO como Anexo I (IF-2023-34599085-APN-SCCDSEI#MAD) y sus Fichas de Medidas de Adaptación, Mitigación o Pérdidas y Daños como Anexo II (IF-2023-34599471-APN-SCCDSEI#MAD), los cuales forman parte integrante de la presente. (unofficial translation: “The second NATIONAL CLIMATE CHANGE ADAPTATION AND MITIGATION PLAN is approved as Annex I (IF-2023-34599085-APN-SCCDSEI#MAD), with its Adaptation, Mitigation or Loss and Damage Measure Sheets as Annex II (IF-2023-34599471-APN-SCCDSEI#MAD), both forming an integral part of this resolution.”)

  • Third Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC 3.0)

    Adopted by Subsecretaría de Ambiente, presented to the Mesa Ampliada of the National Cabinet under Ley 27.520 and announced on 3 November 2025

    (argentina.gob.ar, 3 November 2025 (opens in a new tab)): La NDC 3.0 de la República Argentina consiste en una meta de emisiones netas para 2030 y 2035 que no supera las 375 MtCO2e. Ambas alcanzan todos los gases y las categorías de fuentes de emisión y sumidero reportadas en el Primer Informe Bienal de Transparencia (IBT1), que incluyó el inventario nacional de gases de efecto invernadero 2022 y fue presentado a la CMNUCC en diciembre de 2024. (unofficial translation: “Argentina’s NDC 3.0 consists of a net emissions target for 2030 and 2035 that does not exceed 375 MtCO2e. Both cover all gases and the emission source and sink categories reported in the First Biennial Transparency Report (BTR1), which included the 2022 national greenhouse gas inventory and was submitted to the UNFCCC in December 2024.”)

    Related GAP · Greenhouse gas emissions

Sources checked 17–18 October 2026. Spanish quotations are given as published; English translations are unofficial.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.
