T

Threats

External · harmful

Repayments due to the IMF

  • A large balance owed to the Fund. On 30 September 2026 Argentina owed the IMF SDR 41,968.67 million under extended arrangements, equal to 1,316.75% of its IMF quota (IMF, Financial Position in the Fund, 30 September 2026 (opens in a new tab), section IV).
  • Payments rise each year to 2030. Projected payments to the Fund, based on existing use of resources, are SDR 5,790.01 million in 2027, SDR 7,132.68 million in 2028, SDR 7,904.94 million in 2029 and SDR 8,568.11 million in 2030. A further SDR 898.65 million was due between 30 September and the end of 2026 (IMF, 30 September 2026 (opens in a new tab), section VI).
  • Principal and charges. Of the 2027 total, SDR 3,250.00 million is principal and SDR 2,540.01 million is charges and interest (IMF, 30 September 2026 (opens in a new tab), section VI).
  • The 2025 arrangement is mostly drawn. Of the SDR 15,267.00 million Extended Fund Facility approved on 11 April 2025 and running to 10 April 2029, SDR 11,452.00 million had been drawn by 30 September 2026 (IMF, 30 September 2026 (opens in a new tab), section V). The same financing is listed under Opportunities.

Drought shocks to farm exports

  • Exports fell by a quarter in the 2023 drought. Goods exports fell 24.5% in 2023, to US$66,789 million (INDEC, Complejos exportadores 2023 (opens in a new tab), March 2024): Las exportaciones alcanzaron 66.789 millones de dólares, con una caída interanual de 24,5% (unofficial translation: “Exports reached 66,789 million dollars, a 24.5% fall year on year”).
  • Grain and oilseed complexes were hit hardest. Wheat exports fell 69.4%, soy 43.9%, maize 32.7% and sunflower 25.2%. INDEC attributes these falls largely to the drought, through lower quantities shipped (INDEC, 2023 (opens in a new tab)): Se considera relevante destacar que la caída en las ventas de estos complejos obedeció en gran medida al impacto que tuvo la sequía, pues la reducción se encuentra asociada en la mayoría de los productos a una caída en el índice de cantidades despachadas (unofficial translation: “It is worth noting that the fall in these complexes’ sales was largely due to the impact of the drought, since in most products the reduction is associated with a fall in the index of quantities shipped”).
  • Exports have risen again since. In INDEC’s revised series, goods exports were US$88,703 million in 2022, US$66,988 million in 2023, US$79,703 million in 2024 and US$87,111 million in 2025. Over the same years the soybean complex earned US$24,900 million, US$13,948 million, US$19,628 million and US$21,442 million (2023–2025 figures provisional) (INDEC, Complejos exportadores 2025 (opens in a new tab), Cuadro 3).

Exposure to world commodity prices and a few buyers

  • A quarter of exports from one crop complex. The soybean complex earned US$21,442 million in 2025, 24.6% of goods exports (INDEC, Complejos exportadores 2025 (opens in a new tab), 4 March 2026, Cuadro 1): Las exportaciones de bienes alcanzaron USD 87.111 millones en 2025, lo que representó un aumento interanual de 9,3%. (unofficial translation: “Goods exports reached USD 87,111 million in 2025, a 9.3% rise year on year.”)
  • World prices move the totals. In 2025 the price index for soy by-products fell 20.6% while the quantities shipped rose 2.1%, so their export value fell 18.9% (INDEC, 2025 (opens in a new tab), Cuadro 2).
  • Meal, oil and beans. Soy meal and pellets made up 39.9% of the soybean complex’s exports (US$8,566 million), soy oil 33.7% (US$7,220 million) and soybeans 22.9% (US$4,916 million) (INDEC, 2025 (opens in a new tab), section 2.1): Los tres principales países concentraron el 45,4% de las exportaciones del complejo. (unofficial translation: “The three main countries accounted for 45.4% of the complex’s exports.”)
  • Three buyers take over a third of exports. Brazil, China and the United States together took 35.6% of goods exports in 2025 (Hawkeye’s sum of the three shares). Trade with Mercosur accounted for 18.9% of exports and 29.0% of imports (INDEC, Intercambio comercial argentino, December 2025 (opens in a new tab), section 2): Brasil fue el principal destino de las exportaciones de bienes con 14,7% del total; le siguieron China y Estados Unidos, con 11,3% y 9,6% de los despachos de bienes argentinos al exterior, respectivamente. (unofficial translation: “Brazil was the main destination of goods exports with 14.7% of the total; China and the United States followed, with 11.3% and 9.6% of Argentine goods shipments abroad, respectively.”)
O

Opportunities

External · helpful

Incentive regime for large investments (RIGI)

  • Created in July 2024. Ley 27.742, published on 8 July 2024, created the Régimen de Incentivo para Grandes Inversiones (RIGI) (Boletín Oficial, Ley 27.742 (opens in a new tab), Art. 164): Créase el Régimen de Incentivo para Grandes Inversiones (RIGI) por el que se establecen, para vehículos titulares de un único proyecto que cumplan con los requisitos previstos en el presente, ciertos incentivos, certidumbre, seguridad jurídica y un sistema eficiente de protección de derechos adquiridos a su amparo. (unofficial translation: “The Large Investment Incentive Regime (RIGI) is created, establishing, for vehicles holding a single project that meet the requirements set out here, certain incentives, certainty, legal security and an efficient system for protecting the rights acquired under it.”)
  • A minimum of US$200 million per project. (Ley 27.742 (opens in a new tab), Art. 173): el monto mínimo de inversión en activos computables será de al menos doscientos millones de dólares estadounidenses (USD 200.000.000) (unofficial translation: “the minimum investment in eligible assets will be at least two hundred million US dollars (USD 200,000,000)”).
  • Thirty years of stability. (Ley 27.742 (opens in a new tab), Art. 178): gozará de la garantía de estabilidad prevista en el capítulo VI del presente título por un plazo de treinta (30) años contados desde la fecha de adhesión (unofficial translation: “will enjoy the stability guarantee provided for in chapter VI of this title for a period of thirty (30) years from the date of joining”).
  • Regulated in August 2024. The regime’s rules were set by Decreto 749/2024, dated 22 August 2024 (InfoLEG, Decreto 749/2024 (opens in a new tab)).

RIGI is a domestic law. It is listed under Opportunities for the large investment projects it is designed to attract, which depend on investors’ decisions.

Lithium resources

  • About 19% of world lithium resources. USGS puts Argentina’s measured and indicated lithium resources at 28 million tons, out of a world total of about 150 million tons; the share is Hawkeye’s arithmetic (USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026, Lithium (opens in a new tab), World Resources): “measured and indicated lithium resources have increased substantially worldwide and total about 150 million tons.”
  • Second only to the United States. USGS gives the United States 30 million tons. Among the other countries it lists, Argentina’s figure is the largest (USGS, 2026 (opens in a new tab)): “Resources are distributed as follows: Argentina, 28 million tons; Bolivia, 23 million tons; Chile, 13 million tons; …”
  • A regional concentration. With Bolivia (23 million tons) and Chile (13 million tons), the three neighbours hold 64 million tons, about 43% of the world total (Hawkeye’s arithmetic from the same USGS list).

IMF financing (2025 Extended Fund Facility)

  • A US$20 billion arrangement. On 11 April 2025 the IMF Executive Board approved a 48-month Extended Fund Facility for Argentina of SDR 15.267 billion (IMF Press Release 25/101 (opens in a new tab); amount and dates also in IMF, Financial Position in the Fund, 30 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)): “The IMF Executive Board approved a 48-month Extended Fund Facility (EFF) arrangement for Argentina totaling US$20 billion (or 479 percent of quota), with an immediate disbursement of US$12 billion…”
  • About US$15.8 billion disbursed. The second review, completed on 21 May 2026, unlocked about US$1 billion (SDR 0.8 billion), “bringing total disbursements under the arrangement to about US$15.8 billion (SDR 11.452 billion)” (IMF Press Release 26/165 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Most targets met. The IMF reported at the second review: “While the end‑December quantitative target for net international reserves (NIR) accumulation was missed, most key performance criteria and indicative targets were met” (IMF, 21 May 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Also a debt. Amounts drawn are repaid to the IMF on a set schedule; see Repayments due to the IMF under Threats.

Weaknesses

Internal · harmful

Poverty and indigence

  • About a third of people below the poverty line. In the first half of 2026, 24.4% of households and 32.3% of people in the 31 urban areas INDEC surveys were below the poverty line (INDEC, poverty and indigence, first half of 2026 (opens in a new tab)): El porcentaje de hogares por debajo de la línea de pobreza (LP) alcanzó el 24,4%. Dentro de estos hogares reside el 32,3% de las personas. (unofficial translation: “The percentage of households below the poverty line (LP) reached 24.4%. 32.3% of people live in these households.”)
  • Indigence. In the same period 5.6% of households and 7.5% of people were below the indigence line (INDEC, first half of 2026 (opens in a new tab)): En este conjunto, hay 5,6% de hogares por debajo de la línea de indigencia (LI), que incluyen al 7,5% de las personas. (unofficial translation: “Within this group, 5.6% of households are below the indigence line (LI); they include 7.5% of people.”)
  • Lower than in early 2024. In the first half of 2024, 52.9% of people and 42.5% of households were below the poverty line, and 18.1% of people and 13.6% of households below the indigence line (INDEC, first half of 2026 (opens in a new tab), Cuadro 1).
  • Up on the second half of 2025. (INDEC, first half of 2026 (opens in a new tab)): Con respecto al segundo semestre de 2025, la incidencia de la pobreza registró un aumento en los hogares y en las personas de 3,4 y 4,1 puntos porcentuales (p.p.), respectivamente. (unofficial translation: “Compared with the second half of 2025, the incidence of poverty rose by 3.4 and 4.1 percentage points (p.p.) for households and people, respectively.”)

Loss of native forest

  • 165,061 hectares lost in 2024. Official monitoring recorded 165,061 ha of native forest loss in 2024 across all regions, 157,559 ha of it in the four historically monitored regions (Yungas, Parque Chaqueño, Selva Paranaense and Espinal) (Informe de monitoreo de la superficie de bosque nativo 2024 (opens in a new tab), Tabla 2).
  • Three provinces lost the most. Santiago del Estero lost 44,423 ha, Chaco 37,788 ha and Formosa 16,635 ha (monitoring report 2024 (opens in a new tab)): Durante el año 2024 las provincias que más pérdida de bosque registraron fueron Santiago del Estero seguida por Chaco y Formosa. (unofficial translation: “In 2024 the provinces that recorded the most forest loss were Santiago del Estero, followed by Chaco and Formosa.”)
  • Lower than in 2007. The same table records 728,469 ha lost in 2007 in the four historical regions (monitoring report 2024 (opens in a new tab), Tabla 2).
  • A zoning law, not a loss target. Ley 26.331 sets minimum standards for protecting native forests and sorts them into three conservation categories, red, yellow and green; it sets no national figure for allowed loss (InfoLEG, Ley 26.331 (opens in a new tab), Art. 9): Categoría I (rojo): sectores de muy alto valor de conservación que no deben transformarse. (unofficial translation: “Category I (red): areas of very high conservation value that must not be transformed.”)

Strengths

Internal · helpful

Public finances and slowing inflation

  • First annual surplus since 2010. The national public sector closed 2024 with a financial surplus of 0.3% of GDP, the first annual one since 2010 according to the government release (Ministerio de Economía, 17 January 2025 (opens in a new tab)): Esto redundó en un superávit financiero para el año 2024 de $ 1.764.786 millones (0,3% del PBI) y uno primario de $ 10.405.810 millones (1,8% del PBI). (unofficial translation: “This resulted in a financial surplus for 2024 of $1,764,786 million (0.3% of GDP) and a primary surplus of $10,405,810 million (1.8% of GDP).”)
  • A second year in surplus. In 2025 the financial surplus was about 0.2% of GDP and the primary surplus about 1.4% (Ministerio de Economía, 16 January 2026 (opens in a new tab)): Por segundo año consecutivo, luego de más de una década de déficit financiero, en el año 2025, el Sector Público Nacional (SPN) acumuló superávit financiero. (unofficial translation: “For the second year in a row, after more than a decade of financial deficits, the National Public Sector (SPN) recorded a financial surplus in 2025.”)
  • From a deficit of 4.6% of GDP in 2023. The summary of the 2025 budget bill gives 2023 as the starting point (Síntesis del Proyecto de Presupuesto 2025 (opens in a new tab)): …si se considera que el punto de partida era un déficit de 4,6% del PBI en 2023. (unofficial translation: “…considering that the starting point was a deficit of 4.6% of GDP in 2023.”)
  • Monthly inflation of 1.9%. Consumer prices rose 1.9% in September 2026 and 23.6% over the first nine months of the year (INDEC, consumer price index, September 2026 (opens in a new tab), 13 October 2026): El nivel general del Índice de precios al consumidor registró un alza mensual de 1,9% en septiembre de 2026 y acumuló en el año una variación de 23,6%. (unofficial translation: “The general level of the consumer price index rose 1.9% in the month of September 2026 and has risen 23.6% so far this year.”)
  • Still high over twelve months. Prices were 33.4% higher in September 2026 than a year earlier (INDEC summary table in the same report (opens in a new tab)).

Satellite communications (ARSAT)

  • Two telecommunications satellites. ARSAT offers capacity on its ARSAT-1 and ARSAT-2 satellites, covering the whole American continent in the C and Ku bands (ARSAT, Conectividad satelital (opens in a new tab)): A través de los satélites ARSAT-1 y ARSAT-2, ofrecemos el servicio de espacio sobre nuestros satélites, llegando a todo el continente americano, en Banda C o Banda Ku. (unofficial translation: “Through the ARSAT-1 and ARSAT-2 satellites we offer space on our satellites, reaching the whole American continent, in the C band or Ku band.”)
  • Nationwide two-way messaging. ARSAT-1 also carries a two-way messaging service covering all of Argentina (ARSAT (opens in a new tab)): A través de nuestro satélite geoestacionario ARSAT-1, ofrecemos nuestro servicio de mensajes bidireccionales para aplicaciones fijas o en movimiento, […] y con cobertura en todo el territorio argentino. (unofficial translation: “Through our geostationary satellite ARSAT-1 we offer our two-way messaging service for fixed or mobile applications, […] with coverage across all of Argentina.”)

Two points only: these are the satellite facts confirmed at source so far. ARSAT quotes are shown without the extra spaces the web page puts before commas.
