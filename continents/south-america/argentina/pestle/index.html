Political 3 points

Governance and cooperation

  • A one-year emergency, 2024–25. Ley 27.742 declared a one-year public emergency in administrative, economic, financial and energy matters; the law was published on 8 July 2024, so that term has run out (Ley 27.742 (opens in a new tab), Art. 1): Declárase la emergencia pública en materia administrativa, económica, financiera y energética por el plazo de un (1) año. (unofficial translation: “A public emergency is declared in administrative, economic, financial and energy matters for a period of one (1) year.”)
  • Emergency by decree, extended for health. DNU 70/2023 declared a wider public emergency until 31 December 2025; InfoLEG notes that Decreto 942/2025 extended the national health emergency in that article to 31 December 2026 (InfoLEG, DNU 70/2023 (opens in a new tab), Art. 1): Declárase la emergencia pública en materia económica, financiera, fiscal, administrativa, previsional, tarifaria, sanitaria y social hasta el 31 de diciembre de 2025. (unofficial translation: “A public emergency is declared in economic, financial, fiscal, administrative, pension, utility-tariff, health and social matters until 31 December 2025.”)
  • A council to carry out the Pacto de Mayo. Decreto 617/2024 set up the Consejo de Mayo to draft the laws and acts needed for the ten-principle Pacto de Mayo signed on 9 July 2024 (Boletín Oficial, Decreto 617/2024 (opens in a new tab), Art. 1): Créase el “Consejo de Mayo” como un órgano colegiado consultivo de debate y elaboración de proyectos de leyes y demás actos administrativos necesarios para la implementación de los lineamientos acordados en el “Pacto de Mayo”. (unofficial translation: “The ‘May Council’ is created as a collegiate advisory body for debating and drafting the bills and other administrative acts needed to implement the guidelines agreed in the ‘May Pact’.”) The principles are quoted on the SOAR page.

Economic 3 points

Economy and finance

  • Fiscal surpluses in 2024 and 2025. The national public sector recorded a financial surplus of 0.3% of GDP in 2024 and about 0.2% in 2025 (Ministerio de Economía, 17 January 2025 (opens in a new tab); 16 January 2026 (opens in a new tab)). Quotes on the SWOT page.
  • Inflation. Consumer prices rose 1.9% in September 2026, 23.6% in the first nine months and 33.4% over twelve months (INDEC, September 2026 (opens in a new tab), 13 October 2026).
  • IMF programme. A 48-month, SDR 15.267 billion (about US$20 billion) Extended Fund Facility was approved on 11 April 2025; about US$15.8 billion had been disbursed after the second review on 21 May 2026 (IMF Press Release 25/101 (opens in a new tab); 26/165 (opens in a new tab)).

Social 3 points

Human development

  • Poverty. 32.3% of people and 24.4% of households in the 31 surveyed urban areas were below the poverty line in the first half of 2026, against 52.9% and 42.5% in the first half of 2024 (INDEC, first half of 2026 (opens in a new tab), Cuadro 1).
  • Indigence. 7.5% of people and 5.6% of households were below the indigence line in the first half of 2026, against 18.1% and 13.6% in the first half of 2024 (INDEC, first half of 2026 (opens in a new tab), Cuadro 1).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Poverty and indigence

Population and demographics

  • Population and life expectancy. The UN estimates Argentina’s population at 45.85 million on 1 July 2025, with life expectancy at birth of 77.7 years (women 80.2, men 75.1) (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab), medium variant).

Technological 2 points

Technology and AI

  • Satellites. ARSAT offers capacity on the ARSAT-1 and ARSAT-2 satellites across the American continent in the C and Ku bands, and a two-way messaging service on ARSAT-1 covering all of Argentina (ARSAT, Conectividad satelital (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Satellite communications (ARSAT)

Natural resources

  • Lithium. USGS puts Argentina’s measured and indicated lithium resources at 28 million tons of about 150 million worldwide (about 19%, Hawkeye’s arithmetic); only the United States, at 30 million tons, has a larger figure (USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Legal 3 points

Economy and finance

  • Large-investment regime. Ley 27.742 created the RIGI (Art. 164), with a minimum investment of US$200 million (Art. 173) and a 30-year stability guarantee (Art. 178) (Ley 27.742 (opens in a new tab)). Quotes on the SWOT page.
  • Severance fund option. (Ley 27.742 (opens in a new tab), Art. 96): Mediante convenio colectivo de trabajo, las partes podrán sustituir la indemnización prevista en el artículo 245 de la ley 20.744 por un fondo o sistema de cese laboral conforme los parámetros que disponga el Poder Ejecutivo nacional. (unofficial translation: “Through a collective labour agreement, the parties may replace the severance pay provided for in article 245 of law 20.744 with a severance fund or system under the parameters set by the national Executive.”)
  • Deregulation by decree. (DNU 70/2023 (opens in a new tab), Art. 2): DESREGULACIÓN. El Estado Nacional promoverá y asegurará la vigencia efectiva, en todo el territorio nacional, de un sistema económico basado en decisiones libres, adoptadas en un ámbito de libre concurrencia, con respeto a la propiedad privada y a los principios constitucionales de libre circulación de bienes, servicios y trabajo. (unofficial translation: “DEREGULATION. The national State will promote and ensure, throughout the national territory, the effective operation of an economic system based on free decisions taken in an environment of free competition, respecting private property and the constitutional principles of free movement of goods, services and labour.”)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · Incentive regime for large investments (RIGI)

Environmental 3 points

Climate change / global warming

  • Climate target. Argentina’s third NDC, announced on 3 November 2025, caps net emissions at 375 MtCO2e in both 2030 and 2035 (argentina.gob.ar, 3 November 2025 (opens in a new tab)); see the GAP row.
  • Why the new number is higher than the old one. The release explains that the 375 MtCO2e target covers more territory and emission categories than the earlier 349 MtCO2e target for 2030, and so is stricter (same release (opens in a new tab)): Por esta razón 375MtCO2e —2030-2035— es menor que el 349MtCO2e del 2025-2030. (unofficial translation: “For this reason 375MtCO2e (2030–2035) is lower than the 349MtCO2e of 2025–2030.”)

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • Native forests. Ley 26.331 sets minimum standards for protecting native forests (InfoLEG, Ley 26.331 (opens in a new tab)); official monitoring recorded 165,061 ha of native forest loss in 2024 (monitoring report 2024 (opens in a new tab), Tabla 2).

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
