A Aspirations

South America has no single continent-wide body. The Aspirations below are goal statements of three regional bodies and one trade agreement, each labelled with its members. None covers all twelve countries.

MERCOSUR: a common market

Treaty, signed 26 March 1991 by Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay and Uruguay

Los Estados Partes deciden constituir un Mercado Común, que deberá estar conformado al 31 de diciembre de 1994, el que se denominará "Mercado Común del Sur" (MERCOSUR). (unofficial translation: “The States Parties decide to establish a Common Market, which is to be in place by 31 December 1994 and will be called the ‘Southern Common Market’ (MERCOSUR).”) (Tratado de Asunción (opens in a new tab), Art. 1)

The four founding States Parties signed the treaty. Current membership was not checked for this page.

EU–MERCOSUR agreement

Interim trade agreement, provisionally applied from 1 May 2026; not yet fully in force

The agreement became binding under international law upon the start of its provisional application on 1 May 2026, following the completion of all the required internal procedures by the parties. (European Commission, DG Trade (opens in a new tab))

Full entry into force needs the European Parliament’s consent; the wider Partnership Agreement needs ratification by all EU Member States.

Andean Community: balanced development

Founding agreement (codified text); members Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador and Peru

El presente Acuerdo tiene por objetivos promover el desarrollo equilibrado y armónico de los Países Miembros en condiciones de equidad, mediante la integración y la cooperación económica y social; acelerar su crecimiento y la generación de ocupación; facilitar su participación en el proceso de integración regional, con miras a la formación gradual de un mercado común latinoamericano. (unofficial translation: “This Agreement aims to promote the balanced and harmonious development of the Member Countries on equitable terms, through integration and economic and social cooperation; to speed up their growth and job creation; and to help them take part in regional integration, with a view to gradually forming a Latin American common market.”) (Acuerdo de Cartagena (opens in a new tab), Art. 1)

Amazon states: a common agenda and 2030 targets

Declaration of the leaders of the eight Amazon Cooperation Treaty states, 9 August 2023

The leaders agreed to avanzar en una nueva agenda común de cooperación en la Amazonía que sea implementada bajo el objetivo del desarrollo sostenible, de la conservación y uso sostenible de la biodiversidad, de los bosques y del agua (unofficial translation: “advance a new common cooperation agenda for the Amazon, carried out with the aim of sustainable development and the conservation and sustainable use of biodiversity, forests and water”) (Declaración de Belém (opens in a new tab), paragraph 1). The preamble stresses la urgencia de acordar metas comunes al 2030 para el combate a la deforestación (“the urgency of agreeing common 2030 targets to fight deforestation”).

The declaration calls zero deforestation an “ideal” and does not set a numeric target; see the GAP row.

How this SOAR analysis works

For a continent, the Aspirations are goal statements of the regional and treaty bodies its countries belong to, quoted word for word and labelled with each body’s members.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.
