Political 1 point

Governance and cooperation

  • President re-elected on 25 October 2026. Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva foi reeleito neste domingo (25) com 51,79% dos votos válidos no segundo turno, was re-elected with 51.79% of valid votes in the second round (Hawkeye’s translation), against 48.21% for Flávio Bolsonaro. It is his fourth term (Agência Câmara, 25 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Economic 2 points

Economy and finance

  • Selic rate held at 13.75%. O Copom decidiu manter a taxa Selic em 13,75% a.a. The monetary policy committee decided to keep the Selic rate at 13.75% a year (Hawkeye’s translation) on 4 November 2026. Its inflation forecast for 2026 is 5.6% (Banco Central do Brasil, 4 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Inflation above the target range

Economy and finance

  • Growth of 2.3% in 2025, unemployment 5.2%. GDP grew 2.3% in 2025 and reached R$12.7 trillion (IBGE, 3 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)). No trimestre de julho a setembro de 2026, a taxa de desocupação no Brasil foi estimada em 5,2%. From July to September 2026, unemployment was 5.2% (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ministry of Finance (opens in a new tab)).

Social 1 point

Human development

  • Extreme poverty 3.5% in 2024. Extreme poverty fell from 4.4% in 2023 to 3.5% in 2024, using the line of US$2.15 a day. Segundo o IBGE, a extrema pobreza seria quase três vezes maior sem programas sociais como o Bolsa Família. IBGE says extreme poverty would be almost three times higher without social programmes such as Bolsa Família (Hawkeye’s translation) (MDS, reporting IBGE (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Poverty and inequality

Technological 1 point

Energy transition

  • Small-scale generation 7.0% of electricity. A micro e minigeração distribuída (MMGD) atingiu 7,0% na geração total de eletricidade no Brasil, em 2025. Small and very small generators, mostly rooftop solar, made 7.0% of all electricity in 2025 (Hawkeye’s translation) (EPE (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · Fast growth in solar power

Legal 2 points

Economy and finance

  • Congress approved the EU trade agreement. Após a publicação do Decreto Legislativo nº 14, em 17 de março de 2026, o Brasil notificou oficialmente a Comissão Europeia em 18 de março de 2026. After Legislative Decree No. 14 was published on 17 March 2026, Brazil formally notified the European Commission on 18 March 2026 (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ministry of Foreign Affairs (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · A trade deal with the European Union

Governance and cooperation

  • A four-year plan set in law. Law No. 14,802 of 10 January 2024 sets the federal Pluriannual Plan for 2024 to 2027. Its first annex sets the vision, values, axes and national targets (Plano Plurianual 2024–2027 (opens in a new tab)).

Environmental 2 points

Climate change / global warming

  • Emissions 41.6% below 2005. Em comparação com 2005, ano de referência das metas da Contribuição Nacionalmente Determinada (NDC), a redução das emissões foi de 41,6%. Compared with 2005, the base year of the national climate pledge, emissions were 41.6% lower in 2024 (Hawkeye’s translation) (MCTI, 11 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page GAP · Net emissions 59 to 67% below 2005 by 2035

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • Amazon clearing at a record low. 3,335 km² of the Amazon was cleared from August 2025 to July 2026, the lowest since 1988, and 5,516.69 km² of the Cerrado (INPE, 16 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Official sources, and labelled analysis. Hawkeye reports official and authoritative sources, quoted or attributed, with a link. Documented goals and programmes appear as those bodies stated them. TOWS strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice to any person or firm. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
