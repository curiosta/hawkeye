Targets and latest values

Net emissions 59 to 67% below 2005 by 2035

Target
A nova NDC estabelece a meta de reduzir as emissões líquidas de gases de efeito estufa do País entre 59% e 67% até 2035, em comparação aos níveis de 2005, o que equivale a alcançar entre 850 milhões e 1,05 bilhão de toneladas de CO₂ equivalente em termos absolutos. The new pledge sets a cut in net greenhouse gas emissions of 59 to 67% by 2035 compared with 2005, which means 850 million to 1.05 billion tonnes of CO2-equivalent (Hawkeye’s translation) (Secom, 13 November 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
2005, the reference year of the pledge (MCTI (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
1.51 billion tonnes in 2024, 41.6% below 2005 (MCTI, 11 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
17.4 to 25.4 percentage points to go, or about 0.46 to 0.66 billion tonnes (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in this source. The Government aims for the top of the range, 67%.

Net emissions of 1.32 billion tonnes in 2025

Target
The first national climate pledge estabelece um limite de 1,32 Gt CO₂ eq para 2025, sets a limit of 1.32 billion tonnes of CO2-equivalent for 2025 (Hawkeye’s translation) (MCTI, 11 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
2.07 billion tonnes in 2022 (MCTI (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
1.51 billion tonnes in 2024 (MCTI, 11 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The 2025 figure is not out yet.
Distance
0.19 billion tonnes above the 2025 limit in 2024 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published yet. The inventory for 2025 is due in a later report.

Zero deforestation in the Amazon by 2030

Target
O principal objetivo da 5ª fase do PPCDAm é estabelecer bases sólidas para alcançar o desmatamento zero até 2030. The main aim of the plan’s fifth phase is to lay solid foundations for zero deforestation by 2030 (Hawkeye’s translation). Here zero deforestation refere-se à eliminação do desmatamento ilegal e à compensação da supressão legal de vegetação nativa, means ending illegal clearing and making up for legal clearing (Hawkeye’s translation) (PPCDAm, 5th phase (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
5,731 km² cleared from August 2024 to July 2025, the latest final figure (INPE (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
3,335 km² from August 2025 to July 2026, an estimate (INPE, 16 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
3,335 km² of clearing a year still to end or make up for. INPE’s figure counts all clearing, legal and illegal, so the exact distance to the target is not published (TBC).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in this source.

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
