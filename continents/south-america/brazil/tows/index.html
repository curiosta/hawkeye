Documented national responses 4

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • The Amazon deforestation plan

    Adopted by Ministry of Environment and Climate Change, PPCDAm phase 5 (2023 to 2027)

    The plan aims to reduzir a perda da vegetação nativa na Amazônia e alcançar o desmatamento zero até 2030, cut the loss of native vegetation in the Amazon and reach zero deforestation by 2030 (Hawkeye’s translation) (PPCDAm, 5th phase (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Clearing in the Cerrado · SWOT · Strengths · Lowest Amazon deforestation on record

  • Bolsa Família cash transfers

    Adopted by Ministry of Development and Social Assistance

    Segundo o IBGE, a extrema pobreza seria quase três vezes maior sem programas sociais como o Bolsa Família. IBGE says extreme poverty would be almost three times higher without programmes such as Bolsa Família (Hawkeye’s translation) (MDS, reporting IBGE (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Poverty and inequality

  • Putting the EU trade agreement into effect

    Adopted by Ministries of Foreign Affairs, Development and Trade, and Agriculture (March 2026)

    The Government reafirma seu compromisso com a plena implementação do Acordo Provisório de Comércio, restates its commitment to carry out the interim trade agreement in full (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ministry of Foreign Affairs (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · A trade deal with the European Union

  • A tight interest rate to bring inflation down

    Adopted by Banco Central do Brasil, Copom (4 November 2026)

    The committee will keep policy tight para manter a restrição adequada de forma a assegurar a convergência da inflação à meta, to keep enough restraint to bring inflation back to target (Hawkeye’s translation) (Banco Central do Brasil (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Inflation above the target range

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
