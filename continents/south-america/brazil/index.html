T

Threats

External · harmful

Floods in Rio Grande do Sul

  • 183 deaths in the 2024 floods. The state Civil Defence counted 183 confirmed deaths, 28 people missing, 806 injured and 2,398,255 people affected in 478 municipalities (Rio Grande do Sul Civil Defence, 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

Inflation above the target range

  • Inflation above the upper limit. On 4 November 2026 the central bank’s monetary policy committee said that a inflação cheia e a média das medidas subjacentes aceleraram, situando-se acima do limite superior do intervalo de tolerância da meta. Headline inflation and the average core measures sped up and are above the upper limit of the target range (Hawkeye’s translation). It kept the Selic rate at 13.75% (Banco Central do Brasil, 4 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Economic

Clearing in the Cerrado

  • 5,517 km² of Cerrado cleared in a year. No Cerrado, o desmatamento foi de 5.516,69 km², redução de 23,8% em relação aos 7.235,27 km² do período anterior. In the Cerrado savanna, clearing was 5,516.69 km², 23.8% less than the 7,235.27 km² of the year before (Hawkeye’s translation). This is more than the Amazon figure for the same year. Maranhão, Tocantins, Piauí e Bahia, que formam o Matopiba, responderam por 74,3% do total. Four states, known as Matopiba, made up 74.3% of the total (Hawkeye’s translation) (INPE, 16 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

O

Opportunities

External · helpful

A trade deal with the European Union

  • The Mercosur–EU interim trade agreement. The Government announced that o Acordo Provisório de Comércio entre o MERCOSUL e a União Europeia entrará em vigor em 1º de maio de 2026. The interim trade agreement between Mercosur and the European Union enters into force on 1 May 2026 (Hawkeye’s translation). It says that A redução de tarifas, a eliminação de barreiras e o aumento da previsibilidade regulatória criarão condições mais favoráveis para exportações, investimentos e integração às cadeias globais de valor. Lower tariffs, fewer barriers and more predictable rules will help exports, investment and links to global supply chains (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ministry of Foreign Affairs (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Legal

Fast growth in solar power

  • Solar capacity up by a third in a year. A geração solar fotovoltaica atingiu 88,1 TWh (geração centralizada e MMGD) crescendo 24,7% e a sua capacidade instalada alcançou 64.793 MW, expansão de 33,7% em relação ao ano anterior. Solar generation reached 88.1 TWh, up 24.7%, and capacity reached 64,793 MW, up 33.7% on the year before (Hawkeye’s translation). Wind generation reached 116.5 TWh (EPE, National Energy Balance 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

W

Weaknesses

Internal · harmful

High public debt and low investment

  • Gross government debt of 82.9% of GDP. General government gross debt was 82.93% of GDP in September 2026, up from 81.95% in June (Banco Central do Brasil (opens in a new tab)).
  • Investment of 16.8% of GDP. A taxa de investimento de 2025 foi de 16,8% do PIB, contra 16,9% em 2024. The investment rate in 2025 was 16.8% of GDP, against 16.9% in 2024 (Hawkeye’s translation) (IBGE (opens in a new tab)).

Poverty and inequality

  • 23.1% of people in poverty in 2024. No período, a população em situação de pobreza recuou de 27,3% para 23,1%, uma redução de 8,6 milhões de pessoas. Between 2023 and 2024 the share of people in poverty fell from 27.3% to 23.1%, 8.6 million fewer people (Hawkeye’s translation). The line is US$6.85 a day per person (MDS, reporting IBGE (opens in a new tab)).
  • Income inequality stays high. The Gini index of income per person was 0.504 in 2024. It would be 0.542 without social benefits: passando de 0,504 para 0,542 (MDS, reporting IBGE (opens in a new tab)). A Gini index of 0 means everyone has the same income, and 1 means one person has it all.

Also in PESTLE · Social

Farming is the largest source of emissions

  • Farming 38.4% of emissions in 2024. Em 2024, a Agropecuária respondeu por 38,4% das emissões, seguida por Energia (28,3%), LULUCF (18,9%), Processos Industriais e Uso de Produtos (7,2%) e Resíduos (7,2%). In 2024, farming made up 38.4% of emissions, then energy (28.3%), land use and forests (18.9%), industry (7.2%) and waste (7.2%) (Hawkeye’s translation). Methane from the digestion of cattle and other ruminants was 62% of farm emissions (MCTI, 11 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
S

Strengths

Internal · helpful

Mostly renewable electricity

  • 86.8% of electricity from renewable sources. A participação de renováveis na matriz elétrica ficou em 86,8% em 2025. Renewables made up 86.8% of the electricity mix in 2025 (Hawkeye’s translation). Wind and solar together made 26.4% of all electricity (EPE, National Energy Balance 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Lowest Amazon deforestation on record

  • 3,335 km² cleared in the Amazon. Na Amazônia, o desmatamento foi de 3.335 km² entre agosto de 2025 e julho de 2026, redução de 41,8% em relação ao período anterior, quando foram registrados 5.731 km². É a menor taxa da série histórica, iniciada em 1988. In the Amazon, 3,335 km² was cleared from August 2025 to July 2026, 41.8% less than the 5,731 km² of the year before. It is the lowest rate since the series began in 1988 (Hawkeye’s translation). The 2026 figure is an estimate (INPE, 16 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Emissions down 27% in two years. O Brasil registrou queda de 27% nas emissões líquidas de gases de efeito estufa de 2022 a 2024, passando de 2,07 para 1,51 gigatoneladas de dióxido de carbono equivalente (Gt CO₂ eq). Net greenhouse gas emissions fell 27% from 2022 to 2024, from 2.07 to 1.51 billion tonnes of CO2-equivalent (Hawkeye’s translation), mainly because of less deforestation (MCTI, 11 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Record oil output

  • 3.8 million barrels a day in 2025. A produção nacional de petróleo em 2025 foi de 3,8 milhões de barris/dia, 12% acima da de 2024. National oil output in 2025 was 3.8 million barrels a day, 12% more than in 2024 (Hawkeye’s translation). A produção de petróleo do pré-sal teve média de 3 milhões de barris/dia no ano, cerca de 80% da produção brasileira. Deep offshore “pre-salt” fields gave about 80% of it (Hawkeye’s translation) (ANP, 30 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Record harvests

  • Farming grew 11.7% in 2025. IBGE reports that várias culturas registraram crescimento de produção em 2025, com destaque para o milho (23,6%) e a soja (14,6%), que alcançaram produções recordes na série histórica. Many crops grew in 2025, above all maize (23.6%) and soya (14.6%), which reached record harvests (Hawkeye’s translation) (IBGE, 3 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Off the UN Hunger Map

  • Below 2.5% undernourished. The UN Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) announced in July 2025 that Brazil had left its Hunger Map. O resultado reflete a média trienal 2022/2023/2024, que colocou o país abaixo do patamar de 2,5% da população em risco de subnutrição ou de falta de acesso à alimentação suficiente. The result reflects the average for 2022 to 2024, which put the country below 2.5% of people at risk of undernourishment (Hawkeye’s translation) (MDS, 23 December 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
