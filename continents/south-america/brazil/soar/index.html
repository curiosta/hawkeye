A Aspirations

Brazil sets its medium-term goals in a four-year plan passed as a law, and its climate goals in pledges under the Paris Agreement. The Aspirations below quote those texts in Portuguese, with Hawkeye’s translation, each labelled with its status. A long-term strategy, Estratégia Brasil 2050, has been prepared but no act adopting it has been found, so it is not shown.

A democratic, just, developed and sustainable country

Vision for 2027 in the Pluriannual Plan 2024–2027, Law No. 14,802 of 10 January 2024, Annex I, section 1.1

Um país democrático, justo, desenvolvido e ambientalmente sustentável, onde todas as pessoas vivam com qualidade, dignidade e respeito às diversidades. A democratic, just, developed and environmentally sustainable country, where all people live with quality, dignity and respect for diversity (Hawkeye’s translation) (Plano Plurianual 2024–2027 (opens in a new tab)).

Net emissions 59 to 67% below 2005 by 2035

Brazil’s pledge under the Paris Agreement, delivered to the UN on 13 November 2024

The pledge aims for the top of its range: envidar todos os esforços para lograr uma ambiciosa redução de emissões líquidas de 67%, to make every effort to cut net emissions by 67% (Hawkeye’s translation) (Secom, 13 November 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

Climate neutrality by 2050

Stated by the Government when it delivered the 2035 pledge, 13 November 2024

With the new pledge, Brazil will be aligned with the objetivo de neutralidade climática até 2050, the goal of climate neutrality by 2050 (Hawkeye’s translation) (Secom (opens in a new tab)).

Zero deforestation in the Amazon by 2030

PPCDAm phase 5 (2023 to 2027), a federal plan

The plan proposes um novo compromisso para reduzir a perda da vegetação nativa na Amazônia e alcançar o desmatamento zero até 2030, a new commitment to cut native vegetation loss in the Amazon and reach zero deforestation by 2030 (Hawkeye’s translation) (PPCDAm, 5th phase (opens in a new tab)).

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
