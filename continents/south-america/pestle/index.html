Political 2 points

Governance and cooperation

  • A democracy clause for MERCOSUR, Bolivia and Chile. La plena vigencia de las instituciones democráticas es condición esencial para el desarrollo de los procesos de integración entre los Estados Partes del presente Protocolo. (unofficial translation: “The full validity of democratic institutions is an essential condition for the development of integration processes among the States Parties to this Protocol.”) (Protocolo de Ushuaia (opens in a new tab), Art. 1, signed 24 July 1998)
  • The Andean Community’s aims. Asimismo, son objetivos de este Acuerdo propender a disminuir la vulnerabilidad externa y mejorar la posición de los Países Miembros en el contexto económico internacional; fortalecer la solidaridad subregional y reducir las diferencias de desarrollo existentes entre los Países Miembros. (unofficial translation: “The Agreement also aims to reduce external vulnerability and improve the Member Countries’ position in the international economy, to strengthen subregional solidarity and to reduce development gaps between Member Countries.”) Its members are Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador and Peru (Acuerdo de Cartagena (opens in a new tab), Art. 1)

Economic 4 points

Economy and finance

  • Growth of 2.5%. South America is expected to grow 2.5% in both 2026 and 2027. Latin America and the Caribbean as a whole: 2.2% in 2026 and 2.5% in 2027 (ECLAC, 20 August 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • Informal work. Informality affects 47% of employed people in Latin America and the Caribbean (ECLAC, Social Panorama 2025 (opens in a new tab)); quotes on the SWOT page
  • World conditions. ECLAC expects world growth of 2.9% in 2026, the lowest rate since 2022 (ECLAC, 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • A new trade agreement. The EU–MERCOSUR interim trade agreement has been provisionally applied since 1 May 2026 (European Commission (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · A slower world economy · SWOT · Weaknesses · Low investment and informal work · SWOT · Opportunities · The EU–MERCOSUR trade agreement

Social 3 points

Human development

  • Income concentration. The richest 10% in Latin America receive 34.2% of total income and the poorest 10% receive 1.7% (ECLAC, 2025 (opens in a new tab))
  • Poverty. 25.5% of Latin Americans lived in income poverty in 2024 and 9.8% in extreme poverty (ECLAC, 2025 (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Concentrated income and falling social spending

Forced displacement

  • Displacement from Venezuela. 7,640,872 people from Venezuela were abroad at the end of 2025, 6,548,354 of them in other South American countries (UNHCR (opens in a new tab); Hawkeye’s sum)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Venezuelan displacement

Technological 2 points

Energy transition

  • Renewables. Nearly 69% of the region’s energy mix in 2024; solar and wind capacity and generation up 30% on 2023 (WMO, 2025 (opens in a new tab), Latin America and the Caribbean)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · A high share of renewable energy · SWOT · Opportunities · Fast growth in solar and wind

Technology and AI

  • Amazon monitoring. The eight Amazon states decided in 2023 to institutionalise the Amazon Regional Observatory as a permanent monitoring instrument (Declaración de Belém (opens in a new tab), paragraph 8)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · A shared Amazon observatory

Legal 2 points

Governance and cooperation

  • Escazú in force. Tras alcanzar los requisitos establecidos en su artículo 22, el Acuerdo de Escazú entró en vigor el 22 de abril de 2021. (unofficial translation: “Having met the requirements of its article 22, the Escazú Agreement entered into force on 22 April 2021.”) It is open to the 33 countries of Latin America and the Caribbean (CEPAL (opens in a new tab))
  • EU–MERCOSUR binding since 1 May 2026. The agreement became binding under international law upon the start of its provisional application on 1 May 2026, following the completion of all the required internal procedures by the parties. (European Commission (opens in a new tab)). Full entry into force still needs the European Parliament’s consent.

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · The Escazú Agreement on environmental rights · SWOT · Opportunities · The EU–MERCOSUR trade agreement

Environmental 2 points

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • Amazon point of no return. The 2023 Belém Declaration commits the eight Amazon states to la acción urgente para evitar el punto de no retorno en la Amazonía (“urgent action to prevent the point of no return in the Amazon”) (Declaración de Belém (opens in a new tab), paragraph 1)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · The Amazon’s point of no return

Climate change / global warming

  • Glaciers. With the loss of the Humboldt glacier, the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela became the second country in the world to lose all of its glaciers. (WMO, 2025 (opens in a new tab))

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a continent, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups. Where a continent has no permanent population, government or national economy, as with Antarctica, the groups follow what its governing treaties and official bodies report on.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
