Hawkeye analysis

Strategy cells on this page are Hawkeye’s own analysis. Each recommendation pairs a verified strength or weakness with a verified opportunity or threat, and compares it with a documented case from another place. The precedent facts are sourced. The pairing, the transfer and the recommendation are Hawkeye’s work, not official policy and not advice to any person or firm.

The documented responses listed lower on this page remain official programmes or instruments, each confirmed at their official source. Several of those responses already work in the same area as a recommendation. A recommendation is a pairing and a lesson from elsewhere. It is not a claim that Colombia has no policy in that area. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.

  • WO Responses that use opportunities to reduce weaknesses

    • Use new wind and solar capacity to bring net emissions back toward the 1990 level

      Pairs SWOT · Weaknesses · Net emissions above the 1990 level · SWOT · Opportunities · More wind and solar power

      Use new wind and solar capacity to cut net greenhouse-gas emissions, which remain above the 1990 level. Official Colombian programmes already include the climate action law and the energy transition law. This recommendation pairs that emissions weakness with the live increase in non-conventional renewable capacity.

      Analogous case. The European Environment Agency, in its 9 November 2026 press release on the Trends and Projections report, states: In 2025, net EU greenhouse gas emissions under the scope of the European Climate Law dropped by 39% below 1990 levels, and This drop also came while gross domestic product and populations grew by 74% and 8% respectively over the same period. The figures for 2025 are preliminary. The same release records that the pace of cuts slowed in 2025, and that year-on-year emissions rose in domestic transport and buildings (EEA, 9 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. Colombia’s net emissions in 2021 were 280.1 million tonnes of CO2 equivalent, 25.8% above 1990, and the live SWOT also records 2,522.72 MW from non-conventional renewable sources (live SWOT). The EU cut net emissions 39% below 1990 while GDP grew. The 2025 figures are preliminary. The analogy is limited because 2,522.72 MW of capacity does not by itself match an EU-wide policy mix, and because Colombia’s 2021 figure is still well above 1990. Deforestation also rose again in 2025, which can add emissions. Transport and buildings in the EU still saw emissions rise in 2025.

Documented national responses 5

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • The climate action law

    Adopted by Law 2169 of 2021

    The law aims establecer metas y medidas mínimas para alcanzar la carbono neutralidad, la resiliencia climática y el desarrollo bajo en carbono en el país en el corto, mediano y largo plazo, to set minimum goals and measures for carbon neutrality, climate resilience and low-carbon development in the short, medium and long term (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ley 2169 de 2021 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Net emissions above the 1990 level

  • Zero net deforestation by 2030

    Adopted by Law 2169 of 2021, article 5

    Reducir la deforestación neta de bosque natural a 0 hectáreas/ año a 2030. Cut net loss of natural forest to zero hectares a year by 2030 (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ley 2169 de 2021 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Deforestation rose again in 2025

  • Voluntary substitution of coca crops

    Adopted by Decree-Law 896 of 2017 (PNIS)

    The programme aims promover la sustitución voluntaria de cultivos de uso ilícito, to promote the voluntary replacement of illicit crops (Hawkeye’s translation), to help farming families out of poverty (Decreto-Ley 896 de 2017 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Coca crops still growing

  • “Total peace” as state policy

    Adopted by Law 2272 of 2022

    The law aims definir la política de paz como una política de Estado, to make peace policy a policy of the state (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ley 2272 de 2022 (opens in a new tab)).

  • The energy transition law

    Adopted by Law 2099 of 2021

    The law modernises energy rules to promote fuentes no convencionales de energía, non-conventional energy sources (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ley 2099 de 2021 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · More wind and solar power

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every documented national response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Those responses are not ranked. Strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice.
