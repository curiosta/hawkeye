Colombia GAP

Where things stand now compared with an officially agreed target.

Each row sets a target from an official Colombian text against the latest official figure. Most targets come from the tables of first-level indicators in the National Development Plan 2022–2026, which ends in 2026. Distances are Hawkeye’s arithmetic. DANE’s 2025 poverty figures use an updated method, so they are not fully comparable with the plan’s 2021 baselines.

Targets and latest values

Deforestation of no more than 140,000 hectares a year

Target
140,000 hectares a year by the end of the plan period (National Development Plan 2022–2026, first-level indicators (opens in a new tab), page 167).
Baseline
174,103 hectares in 2021 (National Development Plan 2022–2026, first-level indicators (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
119,483 hectares in 2025, up 5.2% on 2024 (IDEAM, deforestation 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
Target met: 20,517 hectares below the ceiling in 2025 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
The Ministry of Environment says the plan’s goal was beaten in each of the four years (Ministry of Environment, 31 July 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Extreme poverty down to 9.60%

Target
9.60% of people by the end of the plan period (National Development Plan 2022–2026, first-level indicators (opens in a new tab), page 145).
Baseline
12.2% in 2021 (National Development Plan 2022–2026, first-level indicators (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
9.6% in 2025 (DANE, 12 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
At the target in 2025 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic), on DANE’s updated method.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in these sources.

2,297.08 MW of non-conventional renewable capacity

Target
2,297.08 MW in commercial operation by the end of the plan period (National Development Plan 2022–2026, first-level indicators (opens in a new tab), page 167).
Baseline
297.08 MW (National Development Plan 2022–2026, first-level indicators (opens in a new tab)).

Distance

Emissions of no more than 169.44 million tonnes in 2030

Target
Law 2169 of 2021 sets a cut of 51% against the reference scenario, lo que representa un máximo de emisiones país de 169.44 millones de tCO2eq en 2030, which means a national maximum of 169.44 million tonnes of CO2-equivalent in 2030 (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ley 2169 de 2021 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
222.7 million tonnes of net emissions in 1990 (NDC 3.0, declarative version (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
280.1 million tonnes of net emissions in 2021, the latest inventory year (NDC 3.0 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
About 110.7 million tonnes above the 2030 ceiling in 2021 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). Data for 2022 onwards is not yet in this inventory.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in these sources.

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
