A Aspirations

Colombia sets its goals in a National Development Plan passed as a law for each four-year presidential term, and its climate goals in law and in pledges under the Paris Agreement. The 2022–2026 plan is still the law in force while the 2026–2030 plan is drafted. The Aspirations below quote those texts in Spanish, with Hawkeye’s translation, each labelled with its status.

A world leader in protecting life

National Development Plan 2022–2026, Law 2294 of 2023, article 1

The plan tiene como objetivo sentar las bases para que el país se convierta en un líder de la protección de la vida, aims to lay the foundations for the country to become a leader in protecting life (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ley 2294 de 2023 (opens in a new tab)). A new plan for 2026 to 2030 is due in Congress on 7 February 2027.

Carbon neutrality by 2050

In law: Law 2169 of 2021, article 5

Alcanzar la carbono neutralidad a 2050. Reach carbon neutrality by 2050 (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ley 2169 de 2021 (opens in a new tab)).

No more than 155 to 161 million tonnes in 2035

NDC 3.0, approved by the Government in December 2025

Colombia decided that para 2035 emitirá como máximo entre 155 y 161 millones de toneladas de CO2, it will emit at most 155 to 161 million tonnes of CO2 in 2035 (Hawkeye’s translation). On forests, el país se compromete a bajar la tasa anual a un rango de 37.500 a 49.999 hectáreas, the country commits to bring yearly deforestation down to 37,500 to 49,999 hectares (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ministry of Environment, 11 December 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.
