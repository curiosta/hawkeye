T

Threats

External · harmful

Inflation and public finances

  • Inflation of 6.3%, twice the target. La inflación anual en septiembre repuntó levemente y se ubicó en 6,3%. Annual inflation rose slightly to 6.3% in September 2026 (Hawkeye’s translation). Expectations stay alejadas de la meta del 3% en todos los horizontes, far from the 3% target at every horizon (Hawkeye’s translation) (Banco de la República, 30 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • El Niño is a risk. El Fenómeno de El Niño constituye un riesgo al alza importante para las sendas de alimentos y regulados. The El Niño weather pattern is an important upward risk for food and regulated prices (Hawkeye’s translation) (Banco de la República (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Economic

Coca crops still growing

  • 261,000 hectares of coca. The UN Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) and the Government found 261,000 hectares of coca at 31 December 2024, 3.5% more than the 253,000 hectares of 2023 (UNODC and SIMCI, coca survey 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
O

Opportunities

External · helpful

More wind and solar power

  • 2,522.72 MW of new renewable capacity. Se dispone de 2.522,72 MW provenientes de fuentes no convencionales de energía renovable (FNCER). There are 2,522.72 MW from non-conventional renewable sources (Hawkeye’s translation), such as wind and solar, up from a baseline of 297.08 MW (Ministry of Mines and Energy, SINERGIA follow-up (opens in a new tab), National Development Plan 2022–2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Technological · GAP · Wind and solar capacity, 2026

A new national plan for 2026 to 2030

  • The plan bill is due on 7 February 2027. El proyecto de Ley definitivo se radicará formalmente ante el Congreso de la República el 7 de febrero de 2027. The final bill will be filed in Congress on 7 February 2027 (Hawkeye’s translation). The Bases were due to go to the National Planning Council on 15 November 2026 (DNP, 17 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

W

Weaknesses

Internal · harmful

Deforestation rose again in 2025

  • 119,483 hectares of forest lost. Para el año 2025 los resultados de monitoreo de la superficie deforestada reportan para Colombia una superficie de pérdida de bosque de 119.483 hectáreas. In 2025, monitoring showed 119,483 hectares of forest lost in Colombia (Hawkeye’s translation). Así mismo, se identifica una tendencia de aumento del 5,2% de la deforestación en el país, an increase of 5.2% on 2024 (Hawkeye’s translation) (IDEAM, deforestation 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Environmental · GAP · Deforestation, 2026

Net emissions above the 1990 level

  • Up 25.8% from 1990 to 2021. En 2021, las emisiones netas fueron de 280.101,98 kt CO₂eq, frente a 222.664,72 kt CO₂eq en 1990, lo que representa un incremento del 25,80 %. In 2021, net emissions were 280.1 million tonnes of CO2-equivalent, against 222.7 million in 1990, an increase of 25.8% (Hawkeye’s translation) (NDC 3.0, declarative version (opens in a new tab)). 2021 is the latest year in this inventory.

Also in GAP · Emissions, 2030

Strained public finances

  • The central bank flags public finances. La evolución de las finanzas públicas es un factor de primer orden en el desempeño macroeconómico futuro y en los esfuerzos de la política monetaria por conducir la inflación a su meta. Public finances are a first-order factor for the economy and for bringing inflation to target (Hawkeye’s translation). The bank pointed to an economic rescue bill sent to Congress on 29 October 2026 (Banco de la República, 30 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
S

Strengths

Internal · helpful

Poverty at its lowest in the series

  • Monetary poverty 28.0% in 2025. En 2025, el 28,0% de los colombianos estaba en condición de pobreza monetaria, down 3.8 points from 31.8% in 2024. Extreme poverty was 9.6%, down from 11.7% (DANE, 12 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Multidimensional poverty 9.9%. En 2025, el porcentaje de personas en situación de pobreza multidimensional en Colombia fue 9,9% en el total nacional. It was 22.4% in rural areas (DANE, 14 April 2026 (opens in a new tab)). This index counts gaps in schooling, health, work and housing.

Also in PESTLE · Social · GAP · Monetary poverty, 2026 · GAP · Multidimensional poverty, 2026

Deforestation below the 2021 level

  • 31% below 2021. En 2025, Colombia mantuvo una reducción del 31% frente a la línea base de 2021, superando ampliamente la meta del 20% establecida en el Plan Nacional de Desarrollo. In 2025, deforestation stayed 31% below the 2021 baseline, well beyond the plan’s 20% goal (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ministry of Environment, 31 July 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
