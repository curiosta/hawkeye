Political 2 points

Governance and cooperation

  • A new President from 7 August 2026. The National Electoral Council declared Abelardo De La Espriella elected for 2026 to 2030. He won 12.960.166 votos in the second round, against 12,708,312 for Iván Cepeda Castro, a margin of 251,854 votes (Canal Capital, 24 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Governance and cooperation

  • The plan for 2026 to 2030 is being drafted. The new plan is called “Patria Milagro”. Its bill is due in Congress on 7 February 2027 (DNP, 17 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)). Until then, the 2022–2026 plan in Law 2294 of 2023 applies (Ley 2294 de 2023 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · A new national plan for 2026 to 2030

Economic 1 point

Economy and finance

  • Policy rate 12.25%. On 30 October 2026, four of the seven board members voted to keep the rate at 12.25% and three voted for a rise of 0.25 points. The bank says its policy mantiene una política monetaria restrictiva, consistente con la perspectiva de una senda de inflación decreciente en 2027, stays restrictive, in line with falling inflation in 2027 (Hawkeye’s translation) (Banco de la República, 30 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Inflation and public finances

Social 1 point

Human development

  • Extreme poverty 9.6%. En 2025, el porcentaje de personas en situación de pobreza monetaria extrema, en el total nacional fue del 9,6%. In 2025, 9.6% of people were in extreme poverty (Hawkeye’s translation) (DANE, 12 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Poverty at its lowest in the series · GAP · Monetary poverty down to 35.50%

Technological 1 point

Energy transition

  • A law for the energy transition. Law 2099 of 2021 aims at la dinamización del mercado energético a través de la utilización, desarrollo y promoción de fuentes no convencionales de energía, a more dynamic energy market through the use, development and promotion of non-conventional energy sources (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ley 2099 de 2021 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · More wind and solar power

Legal 2 points

Human development

  • Pension reform delayed to April 2027. The Constitutional Court ruled that the upheld parts of Law 2381 of 2024 entrarán en vigencia a partir del 1 de abril de 2027, enter into force from 1 April 2027 (Hawkeye’s translation). It sent nine provisions back to the House of Representatives (Constitutional Court, C-264 of 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Climate change / global warming

  • Carbon neutrality by 2050 in law. Law 2169 of 2021 sets the goal to Alcanzar la carbono neutralidad a 2050, reach carbon neutrality by 2050 (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ley 2169 de 2021 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page GAP · Emissions of no more than 169.44 million tonnes in 2030

Environmental 1 point

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • Forest loss of 119,483 hectares in 2025. Deforestation rose 5.2% in 2025, after 113,608 hectares in 2024 (IDEAM (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Deforestation rose again in 2025 · GAP · Deforestation of no more than 140,000 hectares a year

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
