Canada PESTLE

Political 2 points

Governance and cooperation

  • 69.0% turnout in April 2025. Official turnout for the 45th general election (GE), held on April 28, 2025, was 69.0%. This is 6.4 percentage points higher than the previous GE in 2021 (62.6%) and is the highest turnout since the 1993 election (Elections Canada (opens in a new tab)).

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • NATO’s 2% target in 2025-26. In June 2025, the Prime Minister announced that Canada will achieve NATO’s 2% target this year – half a decade ahead of schedule. The investment for 2025-26 will be over $9 billion. (Prime Minister of Canada, 9 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)). NATO is the North Atlantic Treaty Organization.

On the SWOT page GAP · Core defence spending of 3.5% of GDP by 2035

Economic 2 points

Economy and finance

  • Policy interest rate 2.50%. On 28 October 2026, the Bank of Canada raised its target for the overnight rate by 0.25 percentage points to 2.50% (Bank of Canada, policy interest rate (opens in a new tab)). Statistics Canada lists consumer price inflation at 3.1% for September 2026 and unemployment at 6.7% for October 2026 (Statistics Canada, key indicators (opens in a new tab)).

Economy and finance

  • A deficit of 2.5% of GDP in 2025-26. Budget 2025 projects the projected deficit as a share of the economy declines across the horizon, from 2.5 per cent in 2025-26 to 1.5 per cent by 2029-30. (Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab)). These are projections.

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Tariffs from the largest trading partner

Social 1 point

Population and demographics

  • 41,798,407 people on 1 July 2026. Statistics Canada’s preliminary estimate grew 0.2% in the second quarter of 2026. It counts 2,779,774 non-permanent residents, down 0.7% in the quarter, and 99,148 immigrants arriving in the quarter, down 4.2% from a year earlier (Statistics Canada, 23 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Technological 1 point

Technology and AI

  • Artificial intelligence and quantum. Budget 2025 describes Canada as a leading developer of artificial intelligence and quantum with a highly educated workforce (Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab)). This is the Government’s own description. No measured indicator has been confirmed at source for this page.

Legal 2 points

Climate change / global warming

  • Net zero by 2050 in law. Section 6 of the Canadian Net-Zero Emissions Accountability Act reads: The national greenhouse gas emissions target for 2050 is net-zero emissions. The Act defines the milestone years as any of the years 2030, 2035, 2040 and 2045 (Canadian Net-Zero Emissions Accountability Act (opens in a new tab)).

Economy and finance

  • Consumer carbon price ended on 1 April 2025. The Government of Canada has made regulations that cease the application of the federal fuel charge, effective April 1, 2025. It states that A price on pollution for large emitters will continue to be a pillar of Canada's plan (Department of Finance Canada (opens in a new tab)).

Environmental 2 points

Climate change / global warming

  • 685 Mt of greenhouse gases in 2024. Canada's total GHG emissions in 2024 were 685 megatonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent (Mt CO 2 eq), a 0.3% decrease from 687 Mt CO 2 eq in 2023. From 2005 to 2024, Canada's GHG emissions decreased by 10.3% (-78 Mt CO 2 eq) (ECCC (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Oil and gas: the largest source of emissions

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • 14.0% of land and 15.5% of ocean conserved. At the end of 2025, Canada had conserved 14.0% of its land and fresh water and 15.5% of its marine territory (ECCC, conserved areas (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page GAP · 30% of land conserved by 2030

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
