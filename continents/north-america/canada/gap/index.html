Targets and latest values

Greenhouse gases 40 to 45% below 2005 by 2030

Target
Canada committed to reduce its GHG emissions by 40‑45% below 2005 levels by 2030 (ECCC (opens in a new tab)). Under the Net-Zero Act, the 2030 target is Canada’s Paris Agreement pledge (Act, section 7 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
763 Mt CO2 equivalent in 2005 (ECCC (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
685 Mt in 2024, 10.3% below 2005 (ECCC (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
A 40% cut means about 458 Mt. The 2024 level is about 227 Mt above that, and about 265 Mt above a 45% cut (Hawkeye’s arithmetic on rounded figures).
Assessment by the tracking agency
The 2025 progress report states: Canada’s emissions target is 40% to 45% below 2005 levels by 2030, with an interim objective of 20% below 2005 levels by 2026. Based on the most recent inventory of GHG emissions, and including the calculated LULUCF accounting contribution, Canada’s emissions were 655 Mt in 2023, or 14% below 2005 levels. Under the 2025 “With Measures” scenario, including the LULUCF accounting contribution, the report states: In 2026, Canada will reduce emissions by 16% below 2005 levels and In 2030, Canada will reduce emission by 21% below 2005 levels (wording as published). Under the 2025 “With Additional Measures” scenario it states: In 2026, Canada will reduce emissions by 16% below 2005 levels and In 2030, Canada will reduce emission by 28% below 2005 levels (wording as published). It then says: These data are used as Canada’s official assessment of progress towards our economy-wide emissions reduction targets. (ECCC, 2025 ERP Progress Report (opens in a new tab), section 3.2).

30% of land conserved by 2030

Target
Canada has set a target to conserve 25% of its lands and 25% of its oceans by 2025, and 30% of each by 2030. (ECCC (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
12.7% at the end of 2020 (ECCC (opens in a new tab), data table).
Latest
14.0% at the end of 2025, including 12.9% in protected areas (ECCC (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
16.0 percentage points short of 30% (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). The 25% goal for 2025 was not met (11.0 points short).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in this source.

30% of ocean conserved by 2030

Target
The same target: 25% of oceans by 2025 and 30% by 2030 (ECCC (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
12.5% at the end of 2020 (ECCC (opens in a new tab), data table).
Latest
15.5% at the end of 2025, including 11.4% in protected areas (ECCC (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
14.5 percentage points short of 30% (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). The 25% goal for 2025 was not met (9.5 points short).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in this source.

Core defence spending of 3.5% of GDP by 2035

Target
Canada is already beginning work toward a target of 3.5% of Gross Domestic Product (GDP) by 2035 (Prime Minister of Canada, 25 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)). This is NATO’s new pledge, agreed at the June 2025 summit.
Baseline
1.01% of GDP in 2014 (NATO, Defence Expenditure of NATO Countries 2014-2026 (opens in a new tab), Table 3, core defence, share of real GDP).
Latest
2.03% in 2025 and 2.13% in 2026. NATO marks both as estimates (NATO (opens in a new tab), Table 3).
Distance
1.37 percentage points below 3.5% in 2026 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). The earlier 2% target was met in 2025, by NATO’s estimate.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in this source.

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
