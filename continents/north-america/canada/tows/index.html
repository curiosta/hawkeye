Documented national responses 6

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Build Canada Homes

    Adopted by Government of Canada, Budget 2025 (4 November 2025)

    With investments of 13 billion dollars over 5 years, our flagship home building initiative, Build Canada Homes, will build at a speed and scale not seen in generations. We will double the pace of housing construction over the next decade (Budget 2025 speech (opens in a new tab)).

  • Rebuilding the Canadian Armed Forces

    Adopted by Prime Minister of Canada, 9 June 2025

    With this increase, Canada will achieve NATO’s 2% target this year. The plan includes Developing new drones and sensors to monitor the seafloor and the Arctic. (Prime Minister of Canada, 9 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related GAP · Core defence spending of 3.5% of GDP by 2035

  • Defence spending in Budget 2025

    Adopted by Government of Canada, Budget 2025 (4 November 2025)

    With $30 billion over five years, Budget 2025 represents the largest defence investment in decades. We will meet our NATO 2% commitment this year, modernize NORAD, reinforce our Arctic defences (Budget 2025 speech (opens in a new tab)). NORAD is the North American Aerospace Defense Command, run jointly with the United States.

  • Carbon pricing for large emitters

    Adopted by Department of Finance Canada, March 2025

    A price on pollution for large emitters will continue to be a pillar of Canada's plan to build a strong economy and greener future. (Department of Finance Canada (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Oil and gas: the largest source of emissions

  • Clean Electricity Regulations and the vehicle standard

    Adopted by Environment and Climate Change Canada, 12 February 2025

    The Clean Electricity Regulations will ensure a net-zero electricity system by 2050, and the Electric Vehicle Availability Standard will require 100% zero-emission vehicle sales by 2035. (ECCC, 12 February 2025 (opens in a new tab)). Later changes to the vehicle standard have not been confirmed at source for this page (TBC).

    Related SWOT · Strengths · Mostly clean electricity

  • Major Projects Office and trade corridors

    Adopted by Government of Canada, Budget 2025 (4 November 2025)

    Through the Major Projects Office, we are also helping to develop transformative energy and trade corridors. (Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · New export routes for energy

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
