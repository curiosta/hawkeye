Targets and latest values

Net emissions of 364–404 MtCO2e by 2035 (Paris pledge)

Target
México se compromete a alcanzar emisiones netas entre 364 y 404 MtCO2e en 2035 de manera no condicionada, y entre 332 y 363 MtCO2e de forma condicionada a la movilización de financiamiento, la transferencia de tecnología y el fortalecimiento de capacidades por medio de la cooperación internacional. (unofficial translation: “Mexico commits to reach net emissions of between 364 and 404 MtCO2e in 2035 unconditionally, and between 332 and 363 MtCO2e conditional on the mobilisation of finance, technology transfer and capacity building through international cooperation.”) (NDC 3.0 (opens in a new tab), mitigation component; submitted on 17 November 2025, UNFCCC (opens in a new tab)). The earlier pledge for 2030: México aumenta su meta de reducción de gases de efecto invernadero de 22% a 35% en 2030, con respecto a su línea base, con recursos nacionales que aportarán al menos un 30% y 5% con cooperación y financiamiento internacional previsto para energías limpias. (unofficial translation: “Mexico raises its greenhouse gas reduction target from 22% to 35% in 2030, relative to its baseline, with national resources providing at least 30% and 5% with international cooperation and finance foreseen for clean energy.”) (NDC update 2022 (opens in a new tab)). A commitment under the Paris Agreement; not a law.
Baseline
The 2030 pledge is measured against a business-as-usual path of 991 MtCO2e in 2030 (NDC 2022 (opens in a new tab)). The national plan’s indicator 4.3.1 uses 599 MtCO2e for 2024, a projected value, with a 2030 goal of 533 (PND 2025-2030 (opens in a new tab), p. 81).
Latest
El valor absoluto de las emisiones netas del INEGyCEI es del 2022 de 569 MtCO2e. (unofficial translation: “The absolute value of net emissions in the national inventory (INEGyCEI) for 2022 is 569 MtCO2e.”) (Plan Nacional de Desarrollo 2025-2030, DOF 15 April 2025 (opens in a new tab), p. 81)
Distance
Not computed against the 2035 range, which is for a year not yet reached. The 2022 value is 36 MtCO2e above the plan’s 2030 goal of 533 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 569 − 533).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

35% clean electricity by 2024 (target repealed in 2025)

Target
The energy ministry cites the Energy Transition Law: la SENER fijará como meta una participación mínima de energías limpias en la generación de energía eléctrica del 25 por ciento para el año 2018, del 30 por ciento para 2021 y del 35 por ciento para 2024. (unofficial translation: “SENER shall set as a goal a minimum share of clean energy in electricity generation of 25 per cent for 2018, 30 per cent for 2021 and 35 per cent for 2024.”) (SENER, DOF 23 January 2024 (opens in a new tab), section 4.1). That law was repealed by the Law on Energy Planning and Transition of 18 March 2025: Se abroga la Ley de Transición Energética publicada en el Diario Oficial de la Federación el 24 de diciembre de 2015, y las demás disposiciones que se opongan al presente ordenamiento. (unofficial translation: “The Energy Transition Law published in the Official Gazette on 24 December 2015 is repealed, together with any other provisions that conflict with this law.”) The new law sets no number: La Secretaría de Energía fijará como meta una participación mínima de energías limpias en la generación de energía eléctrica, la cual debe corresponder a las metas de largo plazo establecidas en la Ley General de Cambio Climático. (unofficial translation: “The Ministry of Energy shall set as a goal a minimum share of clean energy in electricity generation, which must match the long-term goals set in the General Law on Climate Change.”) (LPTE (opens in a new tab), transitional articles 2 and 6)
Baseline
20.3% of total electricity generation in 2016 (SENER, Estrategia de Transición (opens in a new tab))
Latest
31.2% in 2022 (SENER (opens in a new tab)). A later report gives Durante el periodo de enero – junio de 2023, las energías limpias alcanzaron una participación del 26.5% dentro de la generación total neta de energía (unofficial translation: “During January to June 2023, clean energy reached a 26.5% share of total net energy generation”) (SENER, RAEL (opens in a new tab)); it measures net generation over half a year, so it is not comparable with the 2022 figure. Full-year 2023 and 2024 shares: TBC.
Distance
In 2022 the share was 3.8 percentage points below the 35% set for 2024 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 35 − 31.2). The target year has passed and the target has been repealed.
Assessment by the tracking agency
The energy ministry lists causes que impidieron el cumplimiento de los porcentajes planeados en la meta (unofficial translation: “that prevented the planned percentages of the goal from being met”) (SENER, DOF 23 January 2024 (opens in a new tab))

Reduce poverty and inequality (Plan México objective 13)

Target
Disminuir la pobreza y desigualdad. (unofficial translation: “Reduce poverty and inequality.”) (Plan Nacional de Desarrollo 2025-2030, DOF 15 April 2025 (opens in a new tab), Objetivos del Plan México hacia 2030, objective 13, p. 101). No number is given.
Baseline
41.9% of the population (51.9 million) in 2018; 36.3% (46.8 million) in 2022 (CONEVAL, 10 August 2023 (opens in a new tab))
Latest
29.6% (38.5 million people) in 2024: En 2024, en México 38.5 millones de personas se encontraban en situación de pobreza multidimensional; 7.0 millones estaban en situación de pobreza extrema (unofficial translation: “In 2024, 38.5 million people in Mexico were in multidimensional poverty; 7.0 million were in extreme poverty”) (INEGI, 13 August 2025 (opens in a new tab))
Distance
Not computed: the objective has no number. The share fell 6.8 percentage points between 2022 and 2024 (INEGI).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published

30% of land and sea under conservation by 2030

Target
National plan indicator 4.5.1, the share of land and sea bajo algún mecanismo de conservación (unofficial translation: “under some conservation mechanism”): 30% of land and 30% of sea by 2030 (Plan Nacional de Desarrollo 2025-2030, DOF 15 April 2025 (opens in a new tab), p. 83)
Baseline
12.39% of land and 23.78% of sea in 2024, counting all conservation mechanisms (Plan Nacional de Desarrollo 2025-2030, DOF 15 April 2025 (opens in a new tab), p. 83)
Latest
Federal protected areas alone: 11.76% of land and 23.78% of sea (CONANP (opens in a new tab)). No later value on the plan’s wider measure has been published (TBC).
Distance
Against the 2024 baseline: 17.61 percentage points for land and 6.22 for sea below 30% (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 30 − 12.39; 30 − 23.78).

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
