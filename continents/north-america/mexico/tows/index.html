Documented national responses 10

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • National Development Plan 2025–2030 and Plan México

    Adopted by Federal Executive; approved by the Cámara de Diputados on 10 April 2025; DOF 15 April 2025 (Edición Vespertina)

    En este marco, destaca la conformación del Plan México, una iniciativa que contempla la colaboración entre el Gobierno de México y el sector privado, cuyo objetivo es fomentar un desarrollo económico equitativo y sustentable a largo plazo basado en el aprovechamiento de nuestro mercado interno para nuestra propia industria, reduciendo con ello las importaciones innecesarias. (unofficial translation: “Within this framework, Plan México stands out: an initiative of collaboration between the Government of Mexico and the private sector whose aim is to promote equitable and sustainable long-term economic development based on using our domestic market for our own industry, thereby reducing unnecessary imports.”) (Plan Nacional de Desarrollo 2025-2030, DOF 15 April 2025 (opens in a new tab), Presentación). Its vision and 13 objectives are on the SOAR page.

    Related GAP · Reduce poverty and inequality (Plan México objective 13) · GAP · Half of public purchases made in Mexico (objective 6) · GAP · 1.5 million more manufacturing jobs (objective 3)

  • Interoceanic Corridor of the Isthmus of Tehuantepec

    Adopted by Presidential decree, DOF 14 June 2019, in force the next day; placed under the Navy (SEMAR) by the amending decree of DOF 14 March 2023

    Article 1 sets up a public body cuyo objeto es instrumentar una plataforma logística que integre la prestación de servicios de administración portuaria que realizan las entidades competentes en los Puertos de Coatzacoalcos, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave y de Salina Cruz, Oaxaca y su interconexión mediante transporte ferroviario (unofficial translation: “whose purpose is to implement a logistics platform integrating the port administration services provided by the competent bodies in the Ports of Coatzacoalcos, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave, and Salina Cruz, Oaxaca, and their connection by rail”) (DOF, 14 June 2019 (opens in a new tab); amended DOF 14 March 2023 (opens in a new tab)).

  • Sonora Sustainable Energy Plan

    Adopted by Government of the State of Sonora (CODESO), presented as a joint federal–state project; listed in the national plan as commitment 90; not a DOF instrument

    El Gobierno de Sonora desarrolla una política industrial denominada Plan Sonora de Energías Sostenibles, cuyo eje es diseñar un ecosistema industrial sostenible promoviendo la descarbonización en la actividad industrial del Estado. (unofficial translation: “The Government of Sonora is developing an industrial policy called the Sonora Sustainable Energy Plan, whose core is to design a sustainable industrial ecosystem by promoting decarbonisation of the state’s industry.”) The solar plant at Puerto Peñasco generará en total 1 GW, y 192 MG en baterías (unofficial translation: “will generate 1 GW in total, and 192 MG in batteries”) (Plan Sonora, April 2024 (opens in a new tab), p. 5). The plan’s launch date has not been confirmed (TBC).

  • National Guard under the defence ministry

    Adopted by Constitutional reform by the Congress of the Union and a majority of state legislatures; DOF 30 September 2024 (Edición Vespertina); in force 1 October 2024

    Article 21, new tenth paragraph: La Federación contará con la Guardia Nacional, fuerza de seguridad pública, profesional, de carácter permanente e integrada por personal militar con formación policial, dependiente de la secretaría del ramo de defensa nacional, para ejecutar la Estrategia Nacional de Seguridad Pública en el ámbito de su competencia. (unofficial translation: “The Federation shall have the National Guard, a professional, permanent public security force made up of military personnel with police training, under the ministry of national defence, to carry out the National Public Security Strategy within its competence.”) (DOF, 30 September 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · A high homicide rate · SWOT · Threats · Firearms trafficked from the United States

  • National Water Plan 2024–2030

    Adopted by Government of Mexico, National Water Commission (CONAGUA), presented 2 December 2024; a federal plan, not a DOF legal instrument

    El objetivo principal del Plan Nacional Hídrico 2024-2030 es garantizar el derecho humano al agua en cantidad y calidad suficiente, asegurar la sostenibilidad de nuestros recursos y fomentar un manejo adecuado y responsable del agua en todos sus usos. (unofficial translation: “The main aim of the National Water Plan 2024-2030 is to guarantee the human right to water in sufficient quantity and quality, ensure the sustainability of our resources and promote proper and responsible management of water in all its uses.”) (CONAGUA (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Drought and water stress · SWOT · Threats · Shared river water owed under the 1944 treaty · GAP · 17 strategic water projects and irrigation upgrades by 2030

  • PEMEX Strategic Plan 2025–2035

    Adopted by Ministry of Energy (SENER), Ministry of Finance (SHCP) and PEMEX, presented 5 August 2025

    The plan’s second axis: el segundo eje es financiero, centrado en los mecanismos que dan sostenibilidad presupuestal, suavizan la carga de pasivos y liberan recursos para destinarlos a fines productivos. (unofficial translation: “the second axis is financial, centred on mechanisms that give budgetary sustainability, ease the burden of liabilities and free resources for productive uses.”) (PEMEX, Plan Estratégico 2025-2035 (opens in a new tab), p. 1). PEMEX adds: El objetivo central es dotar a la empresa de una posición financiera sólida que le permita financiar su gasto operativo, cumplir con sus obligaciones financieras y mantener su estabilidad en el mediano y largo plazo. (unofficial translation: “The central aim is to give the company a solid financial position that lets it fund its operating spending, meet its financial obligations and remain stable in the medium and long term.”) (PEMEX, Boletín 26/2025 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · A heavily indebted state oil company

  • Constitutional welfare rights (Article 4)

    Adopted by Constitutional reform of Article 4, DOF 8 May 2020 (declared by the Comisión Permanente of Congress); reformed again, DOF 2 December 2024

    Current text: Las personas adultas mayores de sesenta y cinco años tienen derecho a recibir por parte del Estado una pensión no contributiva en los términos que fije la ley. (unofficial translation: “Older adults over sixty-five years of age have the right to receive a non-contributory pension from the State under the terms set by law.”) (Constitution, consolidated text, last reform 7 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The 2020 reform first added the non-contributory pension, at 68 years (DOF, 8 May 2020 (opens in a new tab)). Article 4 also guarantees a pension to people with a permanent disability under 65; see the PESTLE page.

    Related SWOT · Strengths · Falling poverty

  • Minimum wages for 2026

    Adopted by Council of Representatives of the National Minimum Wage Commission (CONASAMI), adopted unanimously 3 December 2025; DOF 9 December 2025; in force 1 January 2026

    El propósito del MIR es hacer posible que se recupere el poder adquisitivo de las personas trabajadoras asalariadas que perciben un salario mínimo. (unofficial translation: “The purpose of the MIR [independent recovery amount] is to make it possible for salaried workers who earn a minimum wage to recover purchasing power.”) The general minimum wage rises 13.0% to MXN 315.04 a day, and 5.0% to MXN 440.87 in the northern border free zone (DOF, 9 December 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

  • Judicial reform

    Adopted by Constitutional reform by the Congress of the Union (Article 135); DOF 15 September 2024; in force 16 September 2024

    Article 96, first paragraph: Las Ministras y Ministros de la Suprema Corte de Justicia de la Nación, Magistradas y Magistrados de la Sala Superior y las salas regionales del Tribunal Electoral del Poder Judicial de la Federación, Magistradas y Magistrados del Tribunal de Disciplina Judicial, Magistradas y Magistrados de Circuito y Juezas y Jueces de Distrito, serán elegidos de manera libre, directa y secreta por la ciudadanía el día que se realicen las elecciones federales ordinarias del año que corresponda conforme al siguiente procedimiento: (unofficial translation: “The Justices of the Supreme Court of Justice of the Nation, the Magistrates of the Superior Chamber and regional chambers of the Electoral Tribunal of the Federal Judiciary, the Magistrates of the Judicial Discipline Tribunal, Circuit Magistrates and District Judges shall be elected freely, directly and by secret ballot by the citizens on the day of the ordinary federal elections of the corresponding year, under the following procedure:”) (DOF, 15 September 2024 (opens in a new tab)). A transitional article set the first vote for el primer domingo de junio del año 2025 (unofficial translation: “the first Sunday of June 2025”).

  • Preparing the USMCA (T-MEC) joint review

    Adopted by Ministry of Economy, notice signed 16 September 2025; DOF 17 September 2025; 60-day comment period

    El Gobierno de México, a través de la Secretaría de Economía, invita a las partes interesadas a presentar información, comentarios y recomendaciones respecto al funcionamiento del Tratado entre los Estados Unidos Mexicanos, los Estados Unidos de América y Canadá (Tratado), como parte del proceso de consultas que conduce la Secretaría de Economía en preparación para la revisión conjunta prevista en el artículo 34.7 del Tratado. (unofficial translation: “The Government of Mexico, through the Ministry of Economy, invites interested parties to submit information, comments and recommendations on the operation of the Agreement between the United Mexican States, the United States of America and Canada (the Agreement), as part of the consultation the Ministry of Economy is conducting to prepare for the joint review provided for in Article 34.7 of the Agreement.”) (DOF, 17 September 2025 (opens in a new tab)). The review clause is quoted in the SWOT Threats (USMCA, Article 34.7(2) (opens in a new tab)).

How this TOWS matrix works
