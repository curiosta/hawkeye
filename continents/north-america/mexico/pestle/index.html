Mexico PESTLE

The large outside forces acting on a place, in six groups: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal and Environmental.

Political 3 points

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • Violence. INEGI recorded 33,241 homicides in 2024, a preliminary rate of 25.6 per 100,000 people: La tasa de homicidios fue de 25.6 por cada 100 mil habitantes: 5.6 en mujeres y 46.0 en hombres. (unofficial translation: “The homicide rate was 25.6 per 100,000 inhabitants: 5.6 among women and 46.0 among men.”) (INEGI, 1 August 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Defence spending. SIPRI puts military spending at US$13.6 billion in 2025, 0.47% of the world total (SIPRI Military Expenditure Database (opens in a new tab)). The National Guard’s move under the defence ministry is under Legal below.

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · A high homicide rate · SWOT · Threats · Firearms trafficked from the United States

Governance and cooperation

  • Elected judges. A constitutional reform published on 15 September 2024 provides that federal judges, including the justices of the Supreme Court, serán elegidos de manera libre, directa y secreta por la ciudadanía (unofficial translation: “shall be elected freely, directly and by secret ballot by the citizens”) (DOF, 15 September 2024 (opens in a new tab), Article 96). It came into force on 16 September 2024; the first vote was set for the first Sunday of June 2025.

Economic 3 points

Economy and finance

  • Exports. Goods exports were US$617.7 billion in 2024 and US$664.8 billion in 2025, 96.8% of them non-oil; 83.70% of non-oil exports went to the United States (INEGI, 27 January 2026 (opens in a new tab); preliminary; shares are Hawkeye’s arithmetic except the US share).
  • Informal work. 54.4% of workers were in informal jobs in March 2025 (54.3% in March 2024) (INEGI, ENOE (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · A large export base and record formal jobs · SWOT · Weaknesses · Most workers in informal jobs · SWOT · Threats · Heavy reliance on one export market

Natural resources

  • The state oil company’s debt. PEMEX’s financial debt was US$85.2 billion at 31 December 2025, 12.7% lower than a year earlier (PEMEX, 27 February 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · A heavily indebted state oil company

Social 3 points

Population and demographics

  • A young population. 131,946,900 people in 2025, with a median age of 29.6 years and life expectancy at birth of 75.4 years (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab), medium variant).

Human development

  • Poverty falling. 29.6% of people (38.5 million) were in multidimensional poverty in 2024, down from 41.9% (51.9 million) in 2018 (INEGI, 13 August 2025 (opens in a new tab)). The 2022 figure was 36.3% (46.8 million) (CONEVAL, 10 August 2023 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Less access to health care by 2022. El porcentaje de personas con carencia por acceso a los servicios de salud pasó de 16.2% a 39.1% entre 2018 y 2022, lo cual representa un cambio de 20.1 a 50.4 millones de personas en esta situación, respectivamente. (unofficial translation: “The percentage of people lacking access to health services went from 16.2% to 39.1% between 2018 and 2022, a change from 20.1 to 50.4 million people in this situation, respectively.”) (CONEVAL, 10 August 2023 (opens in a new tab), p. 2). The 2024 figure has not been confirmed at source for this page (TBC).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Falling poverty

Technological 3 points

Energy transition

  • Clean electricity. The energy ministry reports that la participación de las energías limpias en la generación total de energía eléctrica pasó de 20.3% a 31.2% entre 2016 y 2022 (unofficial translation: “the share of clean energy in total electricity generation went from 20.3% to 31.2% between 2016 and 2022”) (SENER, Estrategia de Transición, DOF 23 January 2024 (opens in a new tab)). The law that set the 35%-by-2024 target was repealed on 18 March 2025; see the GAP page.

Technology and AI

  • Research spending. 0.25% of GDP in 2024 (World Bank, World Development Indicators (opens in a new tab)).
  • A target for higher-value industry. One of the Plan México objectives for 2030 is to Crecer en 15% el contenido en cadenas globales de valor en los sectores automotriz, aeroespacial, electrónico, semiconductores, farmacéutico, químico, entre otros. (unofficial translation: “Grow by 15% the content in global value chains in the automotive, aerospace, electronics, semiconductor, pharmaceutical and chemical sectors, among others.”) (Plan Nacional de Desarrollo 2025-2030, DOF 15 April 2025 (opens in a new tab), p. 101). It means growth by 15%, not by 15 percentage points.

Legal 4 points

Human development

  • A constitutional right to a pension. Article 4 of the Constitution, as reformed on 2 December 2024: Las personas adultas mayores de sesenta y cinco años tienen derecho a recibir por parte del Estado una pensión no contributiva en los términos que fije la ley. (unofficial translation: “Older adults over sixty-five years of age have the right to receive a non-contributory pension from the State under the terms set by law.”) (Constitution, consolidated text, last reform 7 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The 2020 text set the age at 68.
  • Disability pensions. La Federación y las entidades federativas garantizarán la entrega de una pensión no contributiva a las personas con discapacidad permanente menores de sesenta y cinco años, en los términos que fije la ley. (unofficial translation: “The Federation and the states shall guarantee a non-contributory pension to people with a permanent disability under sixty-five years of age, under the terms set by law.”) (Constitution, Article 4 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Falling poverty

Climate change / global warming

  • A general climate law. The General Law on Climate Change (2012) establece disposiciones para enfrentar los efectos adversos del cambio climático (unofficial translation: “sets out provisions to address the adverse effects of climate change”) (Article 1) and aims to Regular las emisiones de gases y compuestos de efecto invernadero para que México contribuya a lograr la estabilización de sus concentraciones en la atmósfera a un nivel que impida interferencias antropógenas peligrosas en el sistema climático (unofficial translation: “Regulate emissions of greenhouse gases and compounds so that Mexico contributes to stabilising their concentrations in the atmosphere at a level that prevents dangerous anthropogenic interference with the climate system”) (Article 2, section II) (LGCC, last reform 1 April 2024 (opens in a new tab)). Mexico’s climate pledges are on the GAP page.

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • The National Guard under the defence ministry. A constitutional reform published on 30 September 2024 makes the National Guard a fuerza de seguridad pública, profesional, de carácter permanente e integrada por personal militar con formación policial, dependiente de la secretaría del ramo de defensa nacional (unofficial translation: “professional, permanent public security force made up of military personnel with police training, under the ministry of national defence”) (DOF, 30 September 2024 (opens in a new tab), Article 21). It came into force on 1 October 2024.

Environmental 4 points

Climate change / global warming

  • Emissions. 461 Mt of fossil CO2 in 2024, 1.2% of the world total and 3.5 tonnes per person, against a world average of 4.73 t; 22,016 Mt in total since 1850 (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab); per person: Hawkeye’s arithmetic with UN WPP 2024 population).

Water and food security

  • Drought. 38.1% of the country was in moderate to exceptional drought at the end of 2024 (CONAGUA, 31 December 2024 (opens in a new tab)) and 12.2% in moderate to extreme drought on 30 September 2026 (CONAGUA, 30 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Water stress. Freshwater withdrawals were 44.9% of available resources in 2022 (World Bank (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Drought and water stress · SWOT · Threats · Shared river water owed under the 1944 treaty

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • Protected areas. 232 federal protected areas cover 11.76% of the land and 23.78% of the marine area (CONANP (opens in a new tab)).

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
