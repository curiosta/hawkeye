A Aspirations

Mexico’s National Development Plan 2025–2030 includes a long-term chapter, Plan México. The Aspirations below are the chapter’s vision sentence, the plan’s seven axes, Plan México’s 13 objectives for 2030 and Mexico’s 2035 climate pledge, each labelled with its status.

Plan México: the vision

National development plan (PND 2025–2030), approved by the Cámara de Diputados; DOF 15 April 2025

El Plan México es una apuesta por el futuro del país. No se trata solo de aumentar la producción o mejorar las exportaciones, sino de transformar la estructura productiva de manera que el crecimiento sea equitativo, sostenible y con soberanía. (unofficial translation: “Plan México is a bet on the country’s future. It is not only about raising production or improving exports, but about transforming the productive structure so that growth is equitable, sustainable and sovereign.”) (Plan Nacional de Desarrollo 2025-2030, DOF 15 April 2025 (opens in a new tab), p. 100)

Seven axes of the plan

National development plan (PND 2025–2030)

Este PND se articula sobre cuatro ejes generales y tres ejes transversales que estructuran la política pública en su conjunto. (unofficial translation: “This PND is built on four general axes and three cross-cutting axes that structure public policy as a whole.”) (Plan Nacional de Desarrollo 2025-2030, DOF 15 April 2025 (opens in a new tab), p. 13). The first four below are the general axes and the last three the cross-cutting ones.

  1. Gobernanza con justicia y participación ciudadana. (unofficial translation: “Governance with justice and citizen participation.”)
  2. Desarrollo con bienestar y humanismo. (unofficial translation: “Development with well-being and humanism.”)
  3. Economía moral y trabajo. (unofficial translation: “Moral economy and work.”)
  4. Desarrollo sustentable. (unofficial translation: “Sustainable development.”)
  5. Igualdad sustantiva y derechos de las mujeres. (unofficial translation: “Substantive equality and women’s rights.”)
  6. Innovación pública para el desarrollo tecnológico nacional. (unofficial translation: “Public innovation for national technological development.”)
  7. Derechos de los pueblos y comunidades indígenas y afromexicanas. (unofficial translation: “Rights of Indigenous and Afro-Mexican peoples and communities.”)

Plan México objectives for 2030

National development plan (PND 2025–2030), “Objetivos del Plan México hacia 2030”; not a law

The plan lists 13 objectives towards 2030 (Plan Nacional de Desarrollo 2025-2030, DOF 15 April 2025 (opens in a new tab), p. 101):

  1. Ser la economía número 10 del mundo fortaleciendo el mercado interno y la participación internacional. (unofficial translation: “Be the world’s 10th largest economy by strengthening the domestic market and international participation.”)
  2. Elevar la proporción de inversión respecto del PIB: mantener la proporción de inversión respecto del PIB arriba de 25% a partir de 2026 y arriba de 28% en 2030. (unofficial translation: “Raise investment as a share of GDP: keep it above 25% from 2026 and above 28% in 2030.”)
  3. Crear 1.5 millones de empleos adicionales en manufactura especializada y en sectores estratégicos. (unofficial translation: “Create 1.5 million additional jobs in specialised manufacturing and strategic sectors.”)
  4. Que el 50% de la proveeduría y el consumo nacional sean productos hechos en México en sectores estratégicos. (unofficial translation: “Have 50% of supply and national consumption be products made in Mexico in strategic sectors.”)
  5. Crecer en 15% el contenido en cadenas globales de valor en los sectores automotriz, aeroespacial, electrónico, semiconductores, farmacéutico, químico, entre otros. (unofficial translation: “Grow by 15% the content in global value chains in the automotive, aerospace, electronics, semiconductor, pharmaceutical and chemical sectors, among others.”)
  6. El 50% de las compras públicas serán de producción nacional. Las compras públicas serán una herramienta de desarrollo. (unofficial translation: “50% of public purchases will be of national production. Public purchasing will be a tool for development.”)
  7. Vacunas hechas en México: impulsar el desarrollo completo de procesos de fabricación, farmacéutica y envasado local con énfasis en biotecnología. (unofficial translation: “Vaccines made in Mexico: promote the full development of local manufacturing, pharmaceutical and packaging processes, with an emphasis on biotechnology.”)
  8. Reducir de 2.6 a 1 año el tiempo para concretar una inversión: 50% menos trámites y requisitos en una ventanilla única digital de inversiones. (unofficial translation: “Reduce from 2.6 to 1 year the time needed to complete an investment: 50% fewer procedures and requirements in a single digital investment window.”)
  9. Formar 150 mil profesionistas técnicos anuales con formación continua alineada a sectores estratégicos, y 100% de educación dual en media superior técnica. (unofficial translation: “Train 150,000 technical professionals a year with continuing education aligned to strategic sectors, and 100% dual education in technical upper-secondary schooling.”)
  10. Sostenibilidad ambiental: promover inversiones con prácticas ambientales, sociales y de gobernanza, reúso de aguas, inversión en energía limpia con respaldo, sistemas de manejo de residuos sólidos y acciones de impacto comunitario. (unofficial translation: “Environmental sustainability: promote investment with environmental, social and governance practices, water reuse, investment in clean energy with backup, solid waste management systems and community-impact actions.”)
  11. El 30% de las pequeñas y medianas empresas contarán con acceso a financiamiento. (unofficial translation: “30% of small and medium-sized enterprises will have access to finance.”)
  12. Ser uno de los 5 países más visitados a nivel mundial. (unofficial translation: “Be one of the 5 most visited countries in the world.”)
  13. Disminuir la pobreza y desigualdad. (unofficial translation: “Reduce poverty and inequality.”)

The plan also lists ten strategies for Plan México; they are not repeated here.

The 2035 climate pledge

Commitment under the Paris Agreement (NDC 3.0), submitted 17 November 2025; not a law

México se compromete a alcanzar emisiones netas entre 364 y 404 MtCO2e en 2035 de manera no condicionada, y entre 332 y 363 MtCO2e de forma condicionada a la movilización de financiamiento, la transferencia de tecnología y el fortalecimiento de capacidades por medio de la cooperación internacional. (unofficial translation: “Mexico commits to reach net emissions of between 364 and 404 MtCO2e in 2035 unconditionally, and between 332 and 363 MtCO2e conditional on the mobilisation of finance, technology transfer and capacity building through international cooperation.”) (NDC 3.0 (opens in a new tab))

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
