35% renewable power capacity by 2025 (ASEAN)

Target
APAEC Phase II: aspirational targets of 23% share of RE in Total Primary Energy Supply and 35% share of RE in ASEAN installed power capacity by 2025 (APAEC Phase II (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
2018: 26.4% of ASEAN installed power capacity came from renewable energy. AEO8 also records 33.6% in 2022, slightly down from 33.7% in 2021. With the significant increase in solar PV, hydro, and wind, the RE share of ASEAN installed capacity grew from 26.4% in 2018 to 33.6% in 2022. However, this value slightly decreased from the 2021 level of 33.7%, following the additional capacity from coal. (8th ASEAN Energy Outlook, ACE 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
33.5% of installed power capacity in 2023 (ASEAN Centre for Energy, 2025 (opens in a new tab)); ten ASEAN members at the time of the data
Distance
1.5 percentage points below the target (Hawkeye’s arithmetic: 35 − 33.5)
Assessment by the tracking agency
The 2025 outcome has not been reported yet (TBC). The successor plan, APAEC 2026–2030, aims at 45% RE Share in Installed Power Capacity by 2030 (ASEAN Centre for Energy, 2025 (opens in a new tab))

How this GAP analysis works

For a continent, the targets are those set by the regional or treaty bodies that govern it, plus the global targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
