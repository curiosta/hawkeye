Documented regional responses 8

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP)

    Adopted by 15 parties (13 in Asia); signed 15 November 2020, in force 1 January 2022

    A free-trade and investment partnership (RCEP Agreement (opens in a new tab), Art. 1.3). Objective quoted on the SWOT page.

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · The RCEP free-trade partnership

  • Bangkok Treaty (South-East Asia Nuclear Weapon-Free Zone)

    Adopted by Ten ASEAN states parties; signed 15 December 1995, in force 27 March 1997

    Parties renounce nuclear weapons inside and outside the zone (Bangkok Treaty (opens in a new tab), Art. 3(1)). Text quoted on the SWOT page.

    Related SWOT · Strengths · A South-East Asian nuclear-weapon-free zone

  • ASEAN Agreement on Disaster Management and Emergency Response (AADMER)

    Adopted by ASEAN members; signed 26 July 2005, in force 24 December 2009

    The objective of this Agreement is to provide effective mechanisms to achieve substantial reduction of disaster losses in lives and in the social, economic and environmental assets of the Parties, and to jointly respond to disaster emergencies through concerted national efforts and intensified regional and international co-operation. (AADMER (opens in a new tab), Art. 2)

    Related SWOT · Threats · Floods, heat and drought in 2025

  • Charter of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation

    Adopted by 10 members, 8 in Asia; signed 7 June 2002, in force 19 September 2003

    The main SCO goals and tasks shall be: strengthening mutual trust, friendship and goodneighborliness between the member States (SCO Charter (opens in a new tab), Art. 1)

    Related SWOT · Threats · Rising military spending and five nuclear-armed states

  • Charter of the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC)

    Adopted by 8 South Asian members; Dhaka, 8 December 1985

    The objectives of the ASSOCIATION shall be: a) to promote the welfare of the peoples of SOUTH ASIA and to improve their quality of life; (SAARC Secretariat, SAARC Charter (opens in a new tab), Art. I)

  • Charter of the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC)

    Adopted by 6 Gulf states; 25 May 1981

    The basic objectives of the Cooperation Council are: 1. To effect coordination, integration and inter-connection between Member States in all fields in order to achieve unity between them. (GCC Charter (opens in a new tab), Art. 4)

  • Intergovernmental Agreement on the Asian Highway Network

    Adopted by Adopted in Bangkok, 18 November 2003; in force 4 July 2005

    Its preamble begins: Conscious of the need to promote and develop international road transport in Asia and with neighbouring regions, (UNTS Vol. 2323, No. 41607 (opens in a new tab))

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Mobile networks and road links

  • ASEAN Plan of Action for Energy Cooperation (APAEC) Phase II

    Adopted by ASEAN energy ministers, 19 November 2020; 2021–2025, completed; succeeded by APAEC 2026–2030

    Sub-theme: Accelerating Energy Transition and Strengthening Energy Resilience through Greater Innovation and Cooperation (APAEC Phase II (opens in a new tab)). Its 2025 targets are on the GAP page.

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Renewable power in South-East Asia

Sources checked 26 October 2026. Several sources cover only one grouping (ASEAN) or a wider region (ESCAP’s Asia-Pacific); each is labelled.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a continent, the responses are documented instruments of the regional and treaty bodies its countries belong to, each named with its members and the date it was adopted.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
