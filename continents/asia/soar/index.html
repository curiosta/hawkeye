A Aspirations

No body speaks for the whole of Asia. The Aspirations below are those of four regional bodies, in their own words; their members and population shares are in the groupings table on the SWOT page.

A prosperous ASEAN by 2045

ASEAN Community Vision 2045, adopted at the 46th ASEAN Summit, 26 May 2025 · 11 members, 14.5% of Asia’s population

Leaders met to reaffirm our commitment to building a resilient, innovative, dynamic, people-centred ASEAN, through the adoption of the ASEAN Community Vision 2045. (para 1) and set out: We envision a prosperous ASEAN as a single market with highly-skilled and inclusive workforce, productivity and innovation-driven growth, and incorporating sustainability across and along the value chain. (para 5) (ASEAN Community Vision 2045 (opens in a new tab))

Adopted by the ten members before Timor-Leste joined in October 2025.

Unity among the Gulf states

GCC Charter, Art. 4, 25 May 1981 · 6 members, 1.3% of Asia’s population

The basic objectives of the Cooperation Council are: 1. To effect coordination, integration and inter-connection between Member States in all fields in order to achieve unity between them. (GCC Charter (opens in a new tab))

GCC members’ combined GDP was US$2,419.1 billion in 2025 (IMF WEO, Oct 2026 (opens in a new tab); Hawkeye’s sum). Official intra-GCC merchandise exports were USD 132.1 billion in 2024 (GCC-STAT, Economic Performance Outlook 2024 (opens in a new tab)). That figure does not itself measure the Charter’s unity aim.

Welfare and growth in South Asia

SAARC Charter, Art. I, 8 December 1985 · 8 members, 41.2% of Asia’s population

The objectives of the ASSOCIATION shall be: a) to promote the welfare of the peoples of SOUTH ASIA and to improve their quality of life; b) to accelerate economic growth, social progress and cultural development in the region and to provide all individuals the opportunity to live in dignity and to realise their full potentials (SAARC Charter (opens in a new tab))

The official Charter text is on the SAARC Secretariat’s Charter page (SAARC Secretariat, SAARC Charter (opens in a new tab)).

Mutual trust and regional security

SCO Charter, Art. 1, 7 June 2002 · 10 members, 8 in Asia, 68.3% of Asia’s population

The main SCO goals and tasks shall be: strengthening mutual trust, friendship and goodneighborliness between the member States; development of multifaceted cooperation in the maintenance and strengthening of peace, security and stability in the region (SCO Charter (opens in a new tab))

The share counts the eight Asian members only; Russia and Belarus are counted in Europe.

How this SOAR analysis works

For a continent, the Aspirations are goal statements of the regional and treaty bodies its countries belong to, quoted word for word and labelled with each body’s members.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
