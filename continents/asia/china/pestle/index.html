China PESTLE

The large outside forces acting on a place, in six groups: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal and Environmental.

Political 2 points

Governance and cooperation

  • The 15th Five-Year Plan, approved on 12 March 2026. 十四届全国人大四次会议3月12日表决通过了关于国民经济和社会发展第十五个五年规划纲要的决议，决定批准这个规划纲要。 The fourth session of the 14th National People’s Congress voted on 12 March to approve the plan outline (Hawkeye’s translation) (gov.cn, 13 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The plan covers 2026 to 2030.

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • Military spending of US$336 billion. SIPRI estimates that China spent US$336 billion on its military in 2025, 12% of the world total, second after the United States (SIPRI Yearbook 2026, Summary (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · A growing nuclear arsenal

Economic 2 points

Economy and finance

  • Low inflation and lending rates. In October 2026, China’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) increased by 0.7% year on year. (NBS, 11 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)). In 2025, The consumer prices in 2025 maintained the same level as that of the previous year. (NBS 2025 Statistical Communiqué (opens in a new tab)). On 20 October 2026, the one-year Loan Prime Rate was 3.0% and the five-year rate was 3.5% (Loan Prime Rate, 20 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The Loan Prime Rate is the benchmark rate for bank loans.

Economy and finance

  • Unemployment 5.2% in 2025. The urban surveyed unemployment rate in 2025 averaged 5.2 percent. There were 301.15 million migrant workers (NBS 2025 Statistical Communiqué (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page GAP · Urban unemployment below 5.5%

Social 1 point

Population and demographics

  • Plans for a “fertility-friendly society”. Chapter 37 of the plan aims to 有效降低家庭生育养育教育成本，努力稳定新出生人口规模. Cut the cost to families of having, raising and educating children, and work to keep the number of births stable (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)). Births were 7.92 million in 2025, with a birth rate of 5.63 per thousand (NBS 2025 Statistical Communiqué (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · A shrinking and ageing population

Technological 1 point

Technology and AI

  • A target for research spending. The plan aims for 全社会研发经费投入年均增长7%以上. Total research and development spending grows by more than 7% a year (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab), chapter 3). Spending grew 8.1% in 2025 (NBS 2025 Statistical Communiqué (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · High and rising research spending

Legal 2 points

Governance and cooperation

  • Binding and expected targets. The plan has two kinds of indicators. For binding ones, 对约束性指标和公共服务、生态环保、安全保障等任务，强化责任分解落实. Responsibility for binding indicators, and for public services, the environment and security, is divided among officials and enforced (Hawkeye’s translation). Expected indicators are pursued through policy settings (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab), chapter 62).

Climate change / global warming

  • A national carbon market. 截至2025年底，全国碳排放权交易市场配额累计成交量8.65亿吨，累计成交额576.63亿元. By the end of 2025, 865 million tonnes of allowances had been traded on the national carbon market, worth 57.663 billion yuan in total (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ministry of Ecology and Environment (opens in a new tab)).

Environmental 2 points

Pandemics and health

  • Cleaner air, but not yet clean. 地级及以上城市环境空气细颗粒物（PM 2.5 ）平均浓度为28.0微克/立方米，比2024年下降4.4%。 Fine particle (PM2.5) pollution in cities averaged 28.0 micrograms per cubic metre, 4.4% lower than in 2024 (Hawkeye’s translation) (Ministry of Ecology and Environment (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page GAP · City PM2.5 below 27 micrograms by 2030

Water and food security

  • 91.4% of river and lake sections in good condition. Of the 3,641 sections under the national monitoring program for surface water, 91.4 percent were of fairly clean water quality (Grade I to III) (NBS 2025 Statistical Communiqué (opens in a new tab)).

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
