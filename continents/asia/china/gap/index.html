Targets and latest values

City PM2.5 below 27 micrograms by 2030

Target
地级及以上城市细颗粒物（PM 2.5 ）浓度降至27微克/立方米以下. City fine particle pollution falls below 27 micrograms per cubic metre (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab), chapter 3).
Baseline
28.0 micrograms per cubic metre in 2025, the year before the plan began (Ministry of Ecology and Environment (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
28.0 in 2025, 4.4% lower than in 2024 (MEE (opens in a new tab)). No figure for 2026 yet.
Distance
More than 1.0 microgram per cubic metre to go (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). The fall in 2025 alone was about 1.3 micrograms.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published yet for the new plan period.

Forest cover of 25.8% by 2030

Target
森林覆盖率达到25.8%. Forest cover reaches 25.8% (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab), chapter 3).
Baseline
25.09% in 2025 (Ministry of Ecology and Environment (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
25.09% in 2025 (MEE (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
0.71 percentage points to go (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published yet for the new plan period.

3,600 GW of wind and solar power by 2035

Target
风电和太阳能发电总装机容量达到2020年的6倍以上、力争达到36亿千瓦. Wind and solar capacity reaches more than six times the 2020 level, aiming for 3,600 GW (Hawkeye’s translation) (Address to the UN Climate Summit, 24 September 2025 (opens in a new tab)). This is part of China’s 2035 pledge under the Paris Agreement.
Baseline
End of 2020: The installed grid-connected wind power generation capacity was 281.53 million kilowatts, up by 34.6 percent and the installed grid-connected solar power generation capacity was 253.43 million kilowatts, up by 24.1 percent. (NBS Statistical Communiqué 2020, 28 February 2021 (opens in a new tab)). Together this is 534.96 million kilowatts, or 534.96 GW (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). Six times this 2020 level is 3,209.76 GW. The 2035 pledge aims for more than six times the 2020 level, and for 3,600 GW.
Latest
1,841.7 GW at the end of 2025: 640.0 GW of wind and 1,201.7 GW of solar (NBS 2025 Statistical Communiqué (opens in a new tab), Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Distance
About 1,758 GW to go, roughly 176 GW a year from 2026 to 2035 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). Capacity grew by about 430 GW in 2025, using the growth rates the Bureau reports.
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in this source.

Life expectancy of 80 years by 2030

Target
人均预期寿命提高到80岁. Life expectancy rises to 80 years (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab), chapter 3).
Baseline
The plan says that in the 14th plan period (2021 to 2025), 人均预期寿命提升至79岁以上, life expectancy rose to over 79 years (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab), chapter 1).
Latest
2025: 2025年，我国人口健康状况不断改善，平均预期寿命达到79.25岁，比上年提高0.25岁。 (unofficial translation: “In 2025, the health of China’s population improved, and average life expectancy reached 79.25 years, 0.25 years higher than the year before.”) (NBS, 2025 statistical report on society and people’s well-being, 30 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The UN estimate for 2025 is 78.4 years (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)), using a different method.
Distance
On China’s official measure, 79.25 years in 2025 is 0.75 years short of 80 years (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published yet for the new plan period.

Urban unemployment below 5.5%

Target
城镇调查失业率低于5.5%. The urban surveyed unemployment rate is below 5.5% (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab), chapter 3).
Baseline
5.2% on average in 2025 (NBS 2025 Statistical Communiqué (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
5.2% on average in 2025 and 5.1% at the end of the year (NBS 2025 Statistical Communiqué (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
Within the target by 0.3 percentage points (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in this source.

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
