A Aspirations

China sets its goals in five-year plans approved by the National People’s Congress, and in pledges made by the President at the United Nations. The Aspirations below quote those texts in Chinese, with Hawkeye’s translation, each labelled with its status.

A moderately developed country by 2035

15th Five-Year Plan Outline, chapter 3, approved by the National People’s Congress on 12 March 2026

到2035年人均国内生产总值比2020年翻一番、达到中等发达国家水平. By 2035, GDP per person doubles from 2020 and reaches the level of a moderately developed country (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

Carbon peak before 2030, carbon neutrality before 2060

Announced by the President at the UN General Assembly, 22 September 2020

二氧化碳排放力争于2030年前达到峰值，努力争取2060年前实现碳中和。 China aims for CO2 emissions to peak before 2030, and will strive for carbon neutrality before 2060 (Hawkeye’s translation) (Address to the UN General Assembly, 22 September 2020 (opens in a new tab)). The 15th plan aims for the peak to be 如期实现, reached on time (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

Net greenhouse gases 7 to 10% below the peak by 2035

China’s 2035 pledge under the Paris Agreement, announced at the UN Climate Summit, 24 September 2025

到2035年，中国全经济范围温室气体净排放量比峰值下降7%-10%，力争做得更好。非化石能源消费占能源消费总量的比重达到30%以上. By 2035, net greenhouse gas emissions across the whole economy fall 7 to 10% below their peak, with efforts to do better. Non-fossil energy reaches over 30% of all energy use (Hawkeye’s translation) (Address to the UN Climate Summit, 24 September 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

“Beautiful China”: cleaner air and less carbon per unit of GDP

15th Five-Year Plan Outline, chapter 3 (targets for 2030)

碳达峰目标如期实现，单位国内生产总值二氧化碳排放降低17%. The carbon peak is reached on time, and CO2 per unit of GDP falls by 17% (Hawkeye’s translation). The same goal sets city PM2.5 below 27 micrograms and forest cover at 25.8% (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

Self-reliance in science and technology

15th Five-Year Plan Outline, chapter 3

科技自立自强水平大幅提高。 China’s self-reliance in science and technology rises greatly (Hawkeye’s translation), with research spending growing by more than 7% a year (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Official sources, and labelled analysis. Hawkeye reports official and authoritative sources, quoted or attributed, with a link. Documented goals and programmes appear as those bodies stated them. TOWS strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice to any person or firm.
