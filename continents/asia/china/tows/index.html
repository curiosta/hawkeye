Documented national responses 6

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Controls on total carbon emissions and on coal and oil use

    Adopted by 15th Five-Year Plan Outline, chapter 47 (March 2026)

    推动煤炭和石油消费达峰，单位GDP能耗下降10%左右. Bring coal and oil use to a peak, and cut energy use per unit of GDP by about 10% (Hawkeye’s translation). The plan also aims to 扩大全国碳排放权交易市场覆盖范围, widening the national carbon market (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Coal still supplies half of all energy

  • Support for having and raising children

    Adopted by 15th Five-Year Plan Outline, chapter 37 (March 2026)

    发挥育儿补贴和婴幼儿照护、子女教育个人所得税专项附加扣除政策作用. Use childcare subsidies and income tax deductions for infant care and children’s education (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · A shrinking and ageing population

  • Care for older people and a later retirement age

    Adopted by 15th Five-Year Plan Outline, chapter 40 (March 2026)

    稳妥实施渐进式延迟法定退休年龄. Carefully carry out a gradual rise in the statutory retirement age (Hawkeye’s translation). The plan also aims to 推行长期护理保险, rolling out long-term care insurance (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · A shrinking and ageing population

  • A new model for property

    Adopted by 15th Five-Year Plan Outline, chapter 44 (March 2026)

    加快构建房地产发展新模式，健全多主体供给、多渠道保障、租购并举的住房制度. Speed up a new model for property, with housing supplied by many providers and both renting and buying supported (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Property slump and weak demand

  • Curbing hidden local government debt

    Adopted by 15th Five-Year Plan Outline, chapter 52 (March 2026)

    坚决遏制新增地方政府隐性债务，优化中央和地方政府债务结构，加快推动地方政府融资平台改革转型. Firmly stop new hidden local government debt, improve the mix of central and local debt, and speed up reform of local government financing vehicles (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Property slump and weak demand

  • A special drive to boost consumption

    Adopted by 15th Five-Year Plan Outline, chapter 15 (March 2026)

    深入实施提振消费专项行动，增强居民消费能力，改善消费意愿. Carry out the special action to boost consumption, so that households can and want to spend more (Hawkeye’s translation) (15th Five-Year Plan Outline (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Room to grow spending at home

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
