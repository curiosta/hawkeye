Each row sets a target from an official Japanese text against the latest official figure. The 2030 and 2040 power-mix figures are outlooks in the Strategic Energy Plans, not statutory targets. Japan’s fiscal year runs from April to March. Distances are Hawkeye’s arithmetic where target and latest use the same measure; where a figure has not been confirmed at source, the cell says TBC. Japanese quotations are given as published, with unofficial translations.

Targets and latest values

Greenhouse gas emissions 46% below FY2013 by FY2030

Target
In April 2021, Japan declared that it would aim to reduce its greenhouse gas emissions by 46 percent in FY 2030 from its FY 2013 levels, as an ambitious target which is aligned with the 2050 goal (Japan’s NDC (opens in a new tab)). The same NDC sets 60% for FY2035 and 73% for FY2040.
Baseline
FY2013: 1,394 million tonnes CO2 equivalent (NIES / MOE (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
FY2024: 2013年度の排出量（13億9,400万トン）と比べて、24.9%（3億4,710万トン）減少。 (unofficial translation: “Down 24.9% (347.1 million tonnes) compared with FY2013 emissions (1,394 million tonnes).”) Emissions were 1,046 million tonnes; counting removals, net emissions were 28.7% below FY2013 (NIES / MOE (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
21.1 percentage points on gross emissions (46 − 24.9), or 17.3 points counting removals (46 − 28.7) (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).

TBC

Renewables at 36–38% of power generation in FY2030 (outlook)

Target
The FY2030 power mix in the Sixth Strategic Energy Plan puts renewables at 36～38％ of generation, with a note on aiming for 38％以上の高み (unofficial translation: “a height of 38% or more”) (ANRE, Sixth Strategic Energy Plan outline (opens in a new tab)). For FY2040 the Seventh Plan’s outlook is 電源構成は、再エネが4～5割程度、原子力が2割程度、火力が3～4割程度です。 (unofficial translation: “The power mix is around 40–50% renewables, around 20% nuclear and around 30–40% thermal.”) (ANRE (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
FY2023: 22.9% (Hawkeye’s arithmetic from the FY2024 release: 23.1% after a 0.2-point rise).
Latest
FY2024: 発電電力量の構成は、再生可能エネルギー(水力を含む)が0.2%pt増の23.1%、原子力が0.9%pt増の9.4%、火力(バイオマスを除く)が1.1%pt減の67.5%。 (unofficial translation: “In the composition of generated power, renewables (including hydro) rose 0.2 points to 23.1%, nuclear rose 0.9 points to 9.4% and thermal (excluding biomass) fell 1.1 points to 67.5%.”) (METI (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
12.9 to 14.9 percentage points below the FY2030 range (36 − 23.1; 38 − 23.1) (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
TBC

Nuclear at 20–22% of power generation in FY2030 (outlook)

Target
The same FY2030 outlook puts nuclear at 20～22％ of generation (ANRE, Sixth Strategic Energy Plan outline (opens in a new tab)); the FY2040 outlook is around 20% (ANRE (opens in a new tab); quoted in the row above).
Baseline
FY2023: 8.5% (Hawkeye’s arithmetic from the FY2024 release: 9.4% after a 0.9-point rise).
Latest
FY2024: 9.4% of generation (METI (opens in a new tab); quoted in the row above).
Distance
10.6 to 12.6 percentage points below the FY2030 range (20 − 9.4; 22 − 9.4) (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
TBC

Security-related spending at 2% of GDP in FY2027

Target
2027年度において、防衛力の抜本的強化とそれを補完する取組をあわせ、そのための予算水準が現在の国内総生産（ＧＤＰ）の２％に達するよう、所要の措置を講ずる。 (unofficial translation: “In FY2027, the necessary measures will be taken so that the budget level for the fundamental reinforcement of defence capabilities, together with the efforts that complement it, reaches 2% of current gross domestic product (GDP).”) (National Security Strategy, 16 Dec 2022 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
FY2023 initial budget: about ¥7.6 trillion (about 1.4% of FY2022 GDP) (House of Councillors (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
FY2025: 2025年度の補正予算の成立により、2025年度の当初予算と補正予算をあわせて、上記の予算水準（安全保障関連経費）は、11兆円程度（2022年度のＧＤＰの２％）に到達した (unofficial translation: “With the passage of the FY2025 supplementary budget, the FY2025 initial and supplementary budgets together brought the budget level (security-related expenditure) to about ¥11 trillion (2% of FY2022 GDP)”). The FY2025 initial budget alone was about ¥9.9 trillion (about 1.8%) (House of Councillors, 3 Mar 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The FY2026 total had not been published when the paper was written.
Distance
Reached in FY2025, two years before FY2027, on the government’s measure (initial plus supplementary budget, FY2022 GDP as the denominator).
Assessment by the tracking agency
TBC

Domestic semiconductor sales above ¥15 trillion in 2030

Target
2030年に、国内で半導体を生産する企業の合計売上高（半導体関連）として、15兆円超（※2020年現在５兆円）を実現し、我が国の半導体の安定的な供給を確保する。 (unofficial translation: “In 2030, achieve combined (semiconductor-related) sales of more than ¥15 trillion by companies producing semiconductors in Japan (¥5 trillion as of 2020), securing a stable supply of semiconductors for Japan.”) (METI, Semiconductor and Digital Industry Strategy (opens in a new tab), p. 3).
Baseline
2020: ¥5 trillion (same source).
Latest
2024: ¥9.4 trillion of 国内生産売上高 (unofficial translation: “domestic production sales”). The same chart gives 2020 as ¥7.4 trillion on this series. The body text rounds the latest figure as 足下9兆円 (unofficial translation: “about ¥9 trillion at present”). METI states that this series was エキスパートインタビュー、個社情報及び「SIA 2025 FACTBOOK」等に基づいて、一定の仮定に基づいて経済産業省にて作成。 (unofficial translation: “prepared by the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry on the basis of expert interviews, company information and the ‘SIA 2025 FACTBOOK’ and other sources, under certain assumptions.”) (METI, 18 March 2026 meeting material (opens in a new tab), p. 27). This 2020 figure (¥7.4 trillion) is not the ¥5 trillion figure in the 2023 strategy text quoted as the target.
Distance
On this 2026 METI series, ¥5.6 trillion short of ¥15 trillion (15 − 9.4, Hawkeye’s arithmetic). The 2023 strategy target is “more than ¥15 trillion”, so the remaining gap is at least that amount on this series.
Assessment by the tracking agency
METI: 政策支援による製造能力の本格稼働は順調に進めば2025年以降（ラピダス、TSMC、マイクロン等）に大きく進展することを踏まえれば、2030年の国内生産売上高15兆円達成が視野に入る。 (unofficial translation: “Taking into account that, if things proceed smoothly, the full-scale operation of manufacturing capacity from the policy support will advance substantially from 2025 onward (Rapidus, TSMC, Micron and others), achievement of ¥15 trillion in domestic production sales in 2030 comes into view.”) (METI, 18 March 2026 meeting material (opens in a new tab), p. 27).

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
