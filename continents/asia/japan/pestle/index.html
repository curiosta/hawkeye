Japan PESTLE

The large outside forces acting on a place, in six groups: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal and Environmental.

Political 4 points

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • China, in the defence ministry’s words. 中国は、過去30年以上にわたり、透明性を欠いたまま、継続的に高い水準で国防費を増加させ、核・ミサイル戦力や海上・航空戦力を中心に、軍事力の質・量を広範かつ急速に強化している。 (unofficial translation: “For more than 30 years China has kept raising its defence spending at a high level without transparency, and is broadly and rapidly strengthening the quality and quantity of its military, centred on nuclear and missile forces and sea and air power.”) (Ministry of Defense, white paper 2025 (opens in a new tab))
  • Nuclear-armed neighbours. In January 2026 Russia held 5,420 nuclear warheads, China 620 and North Korea 60. Japan holds none (SIPRI Yearbook 2026 summary (opens in a new tab)).
  • Defence spending at 2% of GDP in FY2027. 2027年度において、防衛力の抜本的強化とそれを補完する取組をあわせ、そのための予算水準が現在の国内総生産（ＧＤＰ）の２％に達するよう、所要の措置を講ずる。 (unofficial translation: “In FY2027, the necessary measures will be taken so that the budget level for the fundamental reinforcement of defence capabilities, together with the efforts that complement it, reaches 2% of current gross domestic product (GDP).”) (National Security Strategy, 16 Dec 2022 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Military build-up in the region

Governance and cooperation

  • An economic security law. The 2022 act 安全保障の確保に関する経済施策を総合的かつ効果的に推進することを目的とする。 (unofficial translation: “aims to promote economic measures for ensuring security comprehensively and effectively.”) (Economic Security Promotion Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1)

Economic 4 points

Economy and finance

  • The highest of 172. 債務残高の対GDP比は、Ｇ７諸国のみならず、その他の諸外国と比べても突出した水準となっています。 (unofficial translation: “The ratio of debt to GDP stands out not only against the G7 but also against other countries.”) In the ministry’s chart Japan ranks highest of 172 countries and regions for 2024 general government debt (Ministry of Finance (opens in a new tab), from IMF data). The percentage has not been read from the chart (TBC).
  • 562 trillion yen. ３．対外純資産残高：561兆7,504億円（対前年末比＋23兆6,495億円、＋4.4％） 対外資産の増加額が対外負債の増加額を上回ったことから、対外純資産残高は8年連続で増加した。 (unofficial translation: “3. Net external assets: 561.7504 trillion yen (up 23.6495 trillion yen, or 4.4%, on a year before). Because external assets grew more than external liabilities, net external assets rose for the 8th year in a row.”) (Ministry of Finance, end of 2025 (opens in a new tab))

Natural resources

  • The lowest self-sufficiency in the G7. 日本のエネルギー自給率は15.3%（G7各国で一番低い水準）。発電は７割程度を化石エネルギーに依存。 (unofficial translation: “Japan’s energy self-sufficiency is 15.3% (the lowest level of the G7 countries). About 70% of power generation depends on fossil energy.”) (Energy White Paper 2025 (opens in a new tab)). The year of the 15.3% is not printed beside it (TBC).
  • 38% on a calorie basis. 令和6年度食料自給率は、カロリーベースで38％（前年度並み）、生産額ベースで64％（前年度＋3ポイント） (unofficial translation: “Food self-sufficiency in FY2024 was 38% on a calorie basis (the same as the year before) and 64% on a production-value basis (3 points up on the year before)”) (MAFF, 10 October 2025 (opens in a new tab))

Social 4 points

Population and demographics

  • 29.4% aged 65 or over. 65歳以上人口は3619万人と前年に比べ５万人の減少 総人口に占める割合は29.4％と過去最高 (unofficial translation: “The population aged 65 or over is 36.19 million, 50,000 fewer than a year before. Its share of the total population is 29.4%, the highest ever.”) (Statistics Bureau, September 2025 (opens in a new tab), as of 15 September 2025). The same source ranks it highest of 38 countries.
  • A median age of 49.8. Against 30.9 for the world in 2025 (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · The world’s oldest population

Human development

  • 81.35 and 87.33 years. 令和７(2025)年簡易生命表によると、男の平均寿命（０歳の平均余命のこと。以下同じ。）は81.35年、女の平均寿命は87.33年となり、前年と比較して男は0.25年、女は0.20年上回っている。 (unofficial translation: “According to the 2025 abridged life table, life expectancy at birth was 81.35 years for men and 87.33 years for women, 0.25 years and 0.20 years higher than the year before.”) (MHLW, life tables 2025 (opens in a new tab))
  • 117th of 145. 日本の順位：117位／145か国　（2026.9.16発表） (unofficial translation: “Japan’s rank: 117th of 145 countries (published 16 September 2026)”) in the World Economic Forum’s Global Gender Gap Index, with a score of 0.670 (Gender Equality Bureau (opens in a new tab)).

Technological 3 points

Technology and AI

  • A 2030 goal of 15 trillion yen. 2030年に、国内で半導体を生産する企業の合計売上高（半導体関連）として、15兆円超（※2020年現在５兆円）を実現し、我が国の半導体の安定的な供給を確保する。 (unofficial translation: “By 2030, achieve combined sales (semiconductor-related) of over 15 trillion yen by companies producing semiconductors in Japan (5 trillion yen as of 2020), and secure a stable supply of semiconductors for Japan.”) (METI, semiconductor and digital industry strategy (opens in a new tab))
  • 3.70% of GDP. 2024年度の科学技術研究費は23兆7925億円で、前年度（22兆497億円）に比べ7.9％増となっています。 (unofficial translation: “Spending on science and technology research in FY2024 was 23.7925 trillion yen, up 7.9% on the year before (22.0497 trillion yen).”) 2024年度の国内総生産（ＧＤＰ）に対する研究費の比率は3.70％で、前年度に比べ0.14ポイント上昇しています。 (unofficial translation: “The ratio of research spending to GDP in FY2024 was 3.70%, 0.14 points higher than the year before.”) (Statistics Bureau, R&D survey (opens in a new tab))
  • 28th of 38 in the OECD. 日本の時間当たり労働生産性は、60.1ドル。OECD加盟38カ国中28位。 (unofficial translation: “Japan’s labour productivity per hour is 60.1 dollars. 28th of the 38 OECD members.”) (Japan Productivity Center, 2025 (opens in a new tab), data for 2024, purchasing power parity)

Legal 3 points

Energy transition

  • The GX Promotion Act (2023). 我が国における脱炭素成長型経済構造への円滑な移行を推進するため、 (unofficial translation: “in order to promote a smooth transition to a decarbonised, growth-oriented economic structure in Japan,”) the act provides for, among other things, 脱炭素成長型経済構造移行債の発行 (unofficial translation: “the issue of decarbonised growth-oriented economic structure transition bonds”) (GX Promotion Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1)
  • 20 trillion yen of public support. 新たに「GX 経済移行債」を創設し (unofficial translation: “a new ‘GX Economic Transition Bond’ will be created”) and used so that 国として 20 兆円規模の大胆な先行投資支援を実行する。 (unofficial translation: “the state gives bold upfront investment support on the scale of 20 trillion yen.”) (GX Basic Policy, 2023 (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · Green transformation bonds

Water and food security

  • Food security in law (amended 2024). Article 2 says 食料安全保障（良質な食料が合理的な価格で安定的に供給され、かつ、国民一人一人がこれを入手できる状態をいう。以下同じ。）の確保が図られなければならない。 (unofficial translation: “food security (a state in which good-quality food is supplied stably at reasonable prices and every person can obtain it) has to be ensured.”) (Food, Agriculture and Rural Areas Basic Act (opens in a new tab), Article 2)

Environmental 3 points

Climate change / global warming

  • 60% less by FY2035, 73% less by FY2040. Japan aims to reduce its greenhouse gas emissions by 60 percent in fiscal year (FY) 2035 and by 73 percent in FY 2040, respectively, from its FY 2013 levels, (Japan NDC (opens in a new tab)). The FY2030 target is on the GAP page.

Natural and cosmic hazards

  • About 80% within 30 years. 南海トラフ沿いでマグニチュード８～９程度の地震が今後 30 年以内に発生する確率は 80％程度とされている (unofficial translation: “the probability of an earthquake of about magnitude 8 to 9 along the Nankai Trough within the next 30 years is put at about 80%”) (Cabinet Office, Nankai Trough report 2025 (opens in a new tab))
  • The disaster law. この法律は、国土並びに国民の生命、身体及び財産を災害から保護するため、防災に関し、基本理念を定め、 (unofficial translation: “This Act, in order to protect the national land and the lives, bodies and property of the people from disasters, sets out basic principles on disaster management,”) (Disaster Countermeasures Basic Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1)

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Official sources, and labelled analysis. Hawkeye reports official and authoritative sources, quoted or attributed, with a link. Documented goals and programmes appear as those bodies stated them. TOWS strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice to any person or firm. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
