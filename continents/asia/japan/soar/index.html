A Aspirations

Hawkeye found no single long-term vision document for Japan. The Aspirations below come from Cabinet decisions and Japan’s climate pledge, each labelled with its status. Japanese quotations are given as published. English translations are unofficial.

Investment in strategic fields

Basic Policy 2026 (Honebuto), Cabinet decision 21 July 2026, not a law

「責任ある積極財政」の考え方の下、政府が一歩前に出て、官民手を携え、戦略分野への投資を進める。 (unofficial translation: “under the idea of ‘responsible and proactive public finance’, the government will take a step forward and, hand in hand with the private sector, advance investment in strategic fields.”) (Honebuto 2026 (opens in a new tab), p. 5)

Deep emission cuts by FY2035 and FY2040

Commitment under the Paris Agreement (NDC), February 2025, not a law

Japan aims to reduce its greenhouse gas emissions by 60 percent in fiscal year (FY) 2035 and by 73 percent in FY 2040, respectively, from its FY 2013 levels, (Japan NDC (opens in a new tab))

Defence spending at 2% of GDP

National Security Strategy, Cabinet decision 16 December 2022

2027年度において、防衛力の抜本的強化とそれを補完する取組をあわせ、そのための予算水準が現在の国内総生産（ＧＤＰ）の２％に達するよう、所要の措置を講ずる。 (unofficial translation: “In FY2027, the necessary measures will be taken so that the budget level for the fundamental reinforcement of defence capabilities, together with the efforts that complement it, reaches 2% of current gross domestic product (GDP).”) (National Security Strategy, 16 Dec 2022 (opens in a new tab)).

2030, the last chance on births

Children’s Future Strategy, Cabinet decision 22 December 2023

繰り返しになるが、我が国にとって2030年までがラストチャンスである。 (unofficial translation: “To repeat, the years up to 2030 are the last chance for our country.”) (Children’s Future Strategy, 22 December 2023 (opens in a new tab))

Chip sales above 15 trillion yen by 2030

Semiconductor and Digital Industry Strategy, METI, June 2023

2030年に、国内で半導体を生産する企業の合計売上高（半導体関連）として、15兆円超（※2020年現在５兆円）を実現し、我が国の半導体の安定的な供給を確保する。 (unofficial translation: “In 2030, achieve combined (semiconductor-related) sales of more than ¥15 trillion by companies producing semiconductors in Japan (¥5 trillion as of 2020), securing a stable supply of semiconductors for Japan.”) (METI, Semiconductor and Digital Industry Strategy (opens in a new tab), p. 3).

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
