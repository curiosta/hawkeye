Hawkeye analysis

Strategy cells on this page are Hawkeye’s own analysis. Each recommendation pairs a verified strength or weakness with a verified opportunity or threat, and compares it with a documented case from another place. The precedent facts are sourced. The pairing, the transfer and the recommendation are Hawkeye’s work, not official policy and not advice to any person or firm.

The documented responses listed lower on this page remain official programmes or instruments, each confirmed at their official source. Several of those responses already work in the same area as a recommendation. A recommendation is a pairing and a lesson from elsewhere. It is not a claim that Japan has no policy in that area. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.

  • ST Responses that use strengths to reduce threats

    • Cut imported energy use by electrifying road transport on the back of research and public incentives

      Pairs SWOT · Strengths · Research spending · SWOT · Threats · Imported energy

      Use Japan’s research base, and existing green-transformation policy, to electrify more road transport and so cut imported energy use. Official Japanese programmes already include the GX Promotion Act. This recommendation pairs research strength with the energy-import threat, and uses a documented national electric-vehicle result.

      Analogous case. The Norwegian Ministry of Transport, in “Norway is electric” (last updated 15 January 2025), states: At the end of 2024, electric vehicles (EVs) held an 88% market share in the market for new passenger cars in Norway, resulting in EVs making up 28% of the total passenger car stock. For new urban busses, the EV market share reached 72% by the end of 2024. The same page records a large fiscal cost for the incentives (Norwegian Ministry of Transport, 15 January 2025 (opens in a new tab)). Statistics Norway, 16 June 2025 (amended 11 November 2025), states: Energibruken i veitransport gikk ned 3,7 prosent i 2024 selv om de totale kjørelengdene for norsk-registrerte kjøretøy var omtrent uendret fra året før. Road-transport energy use fell 3.7% in 2024 even though total distances driven were about unchanged. Electric vehicles and assumed electric driving by plug-in hybrids stod for til sammen 29 prosent av de totale kjørelengdene i 2024, against 26% in 2023 and 2% in 2015 (Statistics Norway, 16 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. Japan imports a large share of its energy, and research spending is a live strength (live SWOT). Norway shows that high electric-vehicle sales can go with a fall in road-transport energy use, even when distances driven stay about the same. The analogy is limited because Norway has a very high share of low-carbon electricity and a long-running tax incentive with a large fiscal cost (the official page estimates a large loss of car-related tax revenue). Japan’s power mix still includes fossil generation, so charging can remain import-intensive until the grid mix changes. Norway is also much smaller. The 2025 passenger-car sales target on the Norwegian page is a target, not a result already delivered for 2025.

  • WO Responses that use opportunities to reduce weaknesses

    • Open export and digital-service jobs to more women

      Pairs SWOT · Weaknesses · A wide gender gap · SWOT · Opportunities · Trade through the CPTPP

      Use CPTPP trade, and related service and digital work, to raise women’s participation in paid work, with attention to job quality and pay. Official Japanese programmes already include the Children Basic Act and related family policy. This recommendation pairs the live gender-gap weakness with that trade opportunity.

      Analogous case. The Philippines built a large information-technology and business-process sector with high female employment. The ILO reports: As of 2019, the IT-BPM sector employed over 1.1 million employees, and women represented 53 per cent of total jobs, but faced a 13 per cent pay gap, largely representing the lower paying and skilled positions they occupy. (ILO Research Brief, June 2020 (opens in a new tab), Box 1, p. 5).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. Japan ranks 117th of 145 on the World Economic Forum gender-gap index in the live SWOT, and CPTPP is a live trade opportunity. Export and remote service work can open paid jobs where workplace norms still limit women’s careers. The analogy is limited by the 13 per cent pay and skill gap in the Philippine case, and by the ILO’s warning about automation risk. A rise in headcount alone will not close Japan’s gender gap if new jobs stay non-regular or low-paid. Japan’s official rank is a composite index, not only labour-force participation.

Documented national responses 9

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Economic Security Promotion Act

    Adopted by National Diet, Act No. 43, promulgated 18 May 2022

    The act 安全保障の確保に関する経済施策を総合的かつ効果的に推進することを目的とする。 (unofficial translation: “aims to promote economic measures for ensuring security comprehensively and effectively.”) (Economic Security Promotion Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1)

  • GX Promotion Act

    Adopted by National Diet, Act No. 32, promulgated 19 May 2023

    我が国における脱炭素成長型経済構造への円滑な移行を推進するため、 (unofficial translation: “in order to promote a smooth transition to a decarbonised, growth-oriented economic structure in Japan,”) the act provides for, among other things, 脱炭素成長型経済構造移行債の発行 (unofficial translation: “the issue of decarbonised growth-oriented economic structure transition bonds”) (GX Promotion Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1)

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Green transformation bonds

  • National Security Strategy

    Adopted by Cabinet decision, 16 December 2022

    2027年度において、防衛力の抜本的強化とそれを補完する取組をあわせ、そのための予算水準が現在の国内総生産（ＧＤＰ）の２％に達するよう、所要の措置を講ずる。 (unofficial translation: “In FY2027, the necessary measures will be taken so that the budget level for the fundamental reinforcement of defence capabilities, together with the efforts that complement it, reaches 2% of current gross domestic product (GDP).”) (National Security Strategy, 16 Dec 2022 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Military build-up in the region · GAP · Security-related spending at 2% of GDP in FY2027

  • Basic Policy on Economic and Fiscal Management and Reform 2026

    Adopted by Cabinet decision, 21 July 2026

    「責任ある積極財政」の考え方の下、政府が一歩前に出て、官民手を携え、戦略分野への投資を進める。 (unofficial translation: “under the idea of ‘responsible and proactive public finance’, the government will take a step forward and, hand in hand with the private sector, advance investment in strategic fields.”) (Honebuto 2026 (opens in a new tab), p. 5)

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · High public debt

  • Security Clearance Act

    Adopted by National Diet, enacted 10 May 2024, promulgated 17 May 2024

    The Cabinet Office summary says the act aims その漏えいの防止を図り、もって我が国及び国民の安全の確保に資することを目的とするものです。 (unofficial translation: “to prevent leaks of such information and so contribute to ensuring the security of Japan and its people.”) (Cabinet Office, Security Clearance Act (opens in a new tab))

  • Children Basic Act and Children’s Future Strategy

    Adopted by National Diet (Act No. 77 of 2022, in force 1 April 2023), Cabinet decision on the strategy, 22 December 2023

    The act aims for a society in which every child 将来にわたって幸福な生活を送ることができる (unofficial translation: “can lead a happy life into the future”) (Children Basic Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1). The strategy says: 繰り返しになるが、我が国にとって2030年までがラストチャンスである。 (unofficial translation: “To repeat, the years up to 2030 are the last chance for our country.”) (Children’s Future Strategy, 22 December 2023 (opens in a new tab))

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · The world’s oldest population · GAP · Men’s childcare-leave take-up of 85% in 2030 (private sector)

  • Semiconductor and Digital Industry Strategy

    Adopted by Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry, June 2023

    Its 2030 sales goal is quoted on the GAP page (METI (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Chip factories · GAP · Domestic semiconductor sales above ¥15 trillion in 2030

  • Food, Agriculture and Rural Areas Basic Act, amended 2024

    Adopted by National Diet, amending Act No. 44 of 5 June 2024

    食料安全保障（良質な食料が合理的な価格で安定的に供給され、かつ、国民一人一人がこれを入手できる状態をいう。以下同じ。）の確保が図られなければならない。 (unofficial translation: “food security (a state in which good-quality food is supplied stably at reasonable prices and every person can obtain it) has to be ensured.”) (Food, Agriculture and Rural Areas Basic Act (opens in a new tab), Article 2)

    Related SWOT · Threats · Imported food · GAP · Calorie-based food self-sufficiency of 45% in FY2030

  • Disaster Countermeasures Basic Act

    Adopted by National Diet, Act No. 223 of 15 November 1961, as amended

    この法律は、国土並びに国民の生命、身体及び財産を災害から保護するため、防災に関し、基本理念を定め、 (unofficial translation: “This Act, in order to protect the national land and the lives, bodies and property of the people from disasters, sets out basic principles on disaster management,”) (Disaster Countermeasures Basic Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1)

    Related SWOT · Threats · A Nankai Trough megaquake

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every documented national response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Those responses are not ranked. Strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice.
