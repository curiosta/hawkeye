Documented national responses 9

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Economic Security Promotion Act

    Adopted by National Diet, Act No. 43, promulgated 18 May 2022

    The act 安全保障の確保に関する経済施策を総合的かつ効果的に推進することを目的とする。 (unofficial translation: “aims to promote economic measures for ensuring security comprehensively and effectively.”) (Economic Security Promotion Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1)

  • GX Promotion Act

    Adopted by National Diet, Act No. 32, promulgated 19 May 2023

    我が国における脱炭素成長型経済構造への円滑な移行を推進するため、 (unofficial translation: “in order to promote a smooth transition to a decarbonised, growth-oriented economic structure in Japan,”) the act provides for, among other things, 脱炭素成長型経済構造移行債の発行 (unofficial translation: “the issue of decarbonised growth-oriented economic structure transition bonds”) (GX Promotion Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1)

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Green transformation bonds

  • National Security Strategy

    Adopted by Cabinet decision, 16 December 2022

    2027年度において、防衛力の抜本的強化とそれを補完する取組をあわせ、そのための予算水準が現在の国内総生産（ＧＤＰ）の２％に達するよう、所要の措置を講ずる。 (unofficial translation: “In FY2027, the necessary measures will be taken so that the budget level for the fundamental reinforcement of defence capabilities, together with the efforts that complement it, reaches 2% of current gross domestic product (GDP).”) (National Security Strategy, 16 Dec 2022 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Military build-up in the region · GAP · Security-related spending at 2% of GDP in FY2027

  • Basic Policy on Economic and Fiscal Management and Reform 2026

    Adopted by Cabinet decision, 21 July 2026

    「責任ある積極財政」の考え方の下、政府が一歩前に出て、官民手を携え、戦略分野への投資を進める。 (unofficial translation: “under the idea of ‘responsible and proactive public finance’, the government will take a step forward and, hand in hand with the private sector, advance investment in strategic fields.”) (Honebuto 2026 (opens in a new tab), p. 5)

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · High public debt

  • Security Clearance Act

    Adopted by National Diet, enacted 10 May 2024, promulgated 17 May 2024

    The Cabinet Office summary says the act aims その漏えいの防止を図り、もって我が国及び国民の安全の確保に資することを目的とするものです。 (unofficial translation: “to prevent leaks of such information and so contribute to ensuring the security of Japan and its people.”) (Cabinet Office, Security Clearance Act (opens in a new tab))

  • Children Basic Act and Children’s Future Strategy

    Adopted by National Diet (Act No. 77 of 2022, in force 1 April 2023), Cabinet decision on the strategy, 22 December 2023

    The act aims for a society in which every child 将来にわたって幸福な生活を送ることができる (unofficial translation: “can lead a happy life into the future”) (Children Basic Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1). The strategy says: 繰り返しになるが、我が国にとって2030年までがラストチャンスである。 (unofficial translation: “To repeat, the years up to 2030 are the last chance for our country.”) (Children’s Future Strategy, 22 December 2023 (opens in a new tab))

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · The world’s oldest population · GAP · Men’s childcare-leave take-up of 85% in 2030 (private sector)

  • Semiconductor and Digital Industry Strategy

    Adopted by Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry, June 2023

    Its 2030 sales goal is quoted on the GAP page (METI (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Chip factories · GAP · Domestic semiconductor sales above ¥15 trillion in 2030

  • Food, Agriculture and Rural Areas Basic Act, amended 2024

    Adopted by National Diet, amending Act No. 44 of 5 June 2024

    食料安全保障（良質な食料が合理的な価格で安定的に供給され、かつ、国民一人一人がこれを入手できる状態をいう。以下同じ。）の確保が図られなければならない。 (unofficial translation: “food security (a state in which good-quality food is supplied stably at reasonable prices and every person can obtain it) has to be ensured.”) (Food, Agriculture and Rural Areas Basic Act (opens in a new tab), Article 2)

    Related SWOT · Threats · Imported food · GAP · Calorie-based food self-sufficiency of 45% in FY2030

  • Disaster Countermeasures Basic Act

    Adopted by National Diet, Act No. 223 of 15 November 1961, as amended

    この法律は、国土並びに国民の生命、身体及び財産を災害から保護するため、防災に関し、基本理念を定め、 (unofficial translation: “This Act, in order to protect the national land and the lives, bodies and property of the people from disasters, sets out basic principles on disaster management,”) (Disaster Countermeasures Basic Act (opens in a new tab), Article 1)

    Related SWOT · Threats · A Nankai Trough megaquake

Sources checked 26 and 31 October and 12 November 2026. Japanese quotations are given as published; English translations are unofficial.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.
