Asia PESTLE

The large outside forces acting on a place, in six groups: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal and Environmental.

Political 6 points

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • Military spending. US$838.8 billion in 2025, 29.1% of the world total (SIPRI (opens in a new tab); Hawkeye’s sum)
  • Nuclear warheads. 1,130 in January 2026, held by China, India, Pakistan, Israel and North Korea (SIPRI Yearbook 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • Strategic competition. Asia and Oceania continued to be marked by intensifying strategic competition between China and the USA (SIPRI Yearbook 2026, Summary (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Rising military spending and five nuclear-armed states · SWOT · Strengths · A South-East Asian nuclear-weapon-free zone

Economy and finance

  • A new member for ASEAN. We welcomed the Democratic Republic of Timor-Leste as the 11th Member State of ASEAN (Chairman’s Statement, 47th ASEAN Summit (opens in a new tab), para 3; 26 October 2025)
  • A regional trade agreement. RCEP, in force since 1 January 2022, links the ten original ASEAN members with China, Japan, the Republic of Korea, Australia and New Zealand (RCEP Agreement (opens in a new tab))
  • Scale. The 13 Asian RCEP parties hold 47.3% of Asia’s population (UN WPP 2024 (opens in a new tab); Hawkeye’s arithmetic)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · The RCEP free-trade partnership

Economic 3 points

Economy and finance

  • A third of world output. GDP US$41,327 billion in 2025, 34.9% of the world (IMF WEO, Oct 2026 (opens in a new tab); sum of 45 states)
  • Concentrated in China. China’s US$19,626 billion is 47.5% of Asia’s total; Japan US$4,453 billion and India US$3,909 billion follow (IMF WEO, Oct 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • Growth targets off track. The goal of decent work and economic growth (Goal 8) has the highest number of targets with negative trends, significantly hindering overall progress. (ESCAP, 2025 (opens in a new tab); Asia-Pacific region)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Public finances and growth targets off track

Social 6 points

Population and demographics

  • Median age. 32.5 years in 2025, against 30.9 for the world (UN WPP 2024 (opens in a new tab))
  • Older in the east. Eastern Asia 41.0 years; South-Eastern Asia 30.9; Southern and Western Asia 27.5; Central Asia 26.7 (UN WPP 2024 (opens in a new tab))
  • Country range. Japan 49.8 years, India 28.8 (UN WPP 2024 (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Most of the world’s people

Water and food security

  • Undernourishment falling. 6.7% of people in Asia, or 323 million, in 2024, down from 7.9% in 2022 (FAO, 2025 (opens in a new tab))
  • Western Asia. An estimated 12.7%, more than 39 million people, may have faced hunger in 2024 (FAO, 2025 (opens in a new tab))
  • Water and glaciers. WMO links glacier loss to long-term water security in the world’s most heavily populated region (WMO, 2026 (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Hunger

Technological 3 points

Technology and AI

  • Industry and infrastructure. Significant progress has been made on industry, innovation and infrastructure (Goal 9), which has no regressing targets in 2024. (ESCAP, 2025 (opens in a new tab); Asia-Pacific region)
  • Manufacturing emissions and investment. ESCAP also reports improvements in reducing CO2 emissions per unit of manufacturing value added and in infrastructure investment in the least developed countries (ESCAP, 2025 (opens in a new tab))
  • Mobile networks. A growing share of the population is covered by a mobile network (ESCAP, 2025 (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Progress on industry and infrastructure · SWOT · Opportunities · Mobile networks and road links

Legal 3 points

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • A treaty ban. Parties to the Bangkok Treaty undertake not to develop, acquire, possess, station, test or use nuclear weapons anywhere (Bangkok Treaty (opens in a new tab), Art. 3(1))
  • Dates. Signed on 15 December 1995; in force since 27 March 1997 (Bangkok Treaty (opens in a new tab))
  • Parties. The ten ASEAN members that signed it; Timor-Leste’s status is TBC (Bangkok Treaty (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · A South-East Asian nuclear-weapon-free zone

Environmental 4 points

Climate change / global warming

  • Warming. The 1991–2025 warming trend was about twice that of 1961–1990 (WMO, 17 June 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • Glaciers. All 23 monitored glaciers in High-mountain Asia lost mass in 2025 (WMO, 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • Emissions. 23,403 million tonnes of fossil CO2 in 2024, 60.6% of the world (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab))
  • Historical emissions. 625,477 million tonnes from 1850 to 2024, 33.9% of the world total (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab))

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a continent, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups. Where a continent has no permanent population, government or national economy, as with Antarctica, the groups follow what its governing treaties and official bodies report on.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Official sources, and labelled analysis. Hawkeye reports official and authoritative sources, quoted or attributed, with a link. Documented goals and programmes appear as those bodies stated them. TOWS strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice to any person or firm. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
