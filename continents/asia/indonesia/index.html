T

Threats

External · harmful

Volcanoes, earthquakes and floods

  • 127 active volcanoes. Indonesia memiliki 127 gunungapi aktif (unofficial translation: “Indonesia has 127 active volcanoes”) (BNPB, Data Bencana Indonesia 2023 (opens in a new tab))
  • 5,400 disasters in 2023. BNPB recorded 5,400 disaster events in 2023. Jumlah tersebut naik dibandingkan dengan tahun sebelumnya yaitu 3.544 kejadian (unofficial translation: “That number is up compared with the year before, namely 3,544 events”). Bencana hidrometeorologi mendominasi kejadian bencana (unofficial translation: “Hydro-meteorological disasters dominated the events”) (BNPB (opens in a new tab))

Also in PESTLE · Environmental

Rising seas and sinking land

  • Coasts at risk. Kombinasi antara penurunan muka tanah dan kenaikan muka air laut semakin memperparah risiko di wilayah pesisir. (unofficial translation: “The combination of land subsidence and sea-level rise makes the risk in coastal areas worse.”) (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab))
  • 6,367 coastal villages. The plan says terdapat 311 kabupaten/kota, 1.643 kecamatan, dan 6.367 desa/kelurahan di wilayah pesisir Indonesia yang rentan terhadap dampak perubahan iklim. (unofficial translation: “there are 311 regencies and cities, 1,643 districts and 6,367 villages in Indonesia’s coastal areas that are vulnerable to the impacts of climate change.”) (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab))

Also in PESTLE · Environmental

China’s nine-dash line

  • Indonesia’s position at the UN. Indonesia reiterates that the Nine-Dash Line map implying historic rights claim clearly lacks international legal basis and is tantamount to upset UNCLOS 1982. (Indonesia, note to the UN, May 2020 (opens in a new tab)). The note does not name the Natuna waters. That the line overlaps Indonesia’s exclusive economic zone there is Hawkeye’s context, not a quote.

Also in PESTLE · Political

Trade disputes and carbon border taxes

  • The nickel ore export ban at the WTO. The export prohibition is inconsistent with Article XI:1 of the GATT 1994. (WTO panel report DS592 (opens in a new tab), para. 8.2). Indonesia appealed on 8 December 2022.
  • The EU’s carbon border tax. As of 1 January 2026, CBAM is applicable under its definitive regime. CBAM applies to imports of selected goods in the following carbon-intensive sectors: cement, iron and steel, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity and hydrogen. (European Commission, CBAM (opens in a new tab)). Annex I of the regulation, as consolidated on 20 October 2025, lists chapter 72 (iron and steel) with named exceptions. Ferro-nickel is not among those exceptions. The list does not include nickel of chapter 75, or nickel ores. (Regulation (EU) 2023/956, Annex I, consolidated 20 October 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

O

Opportunities

External · helpful

Finance for the energy transition

  • US$21.6 billion. The initially agreed funding commitment in the joint statement was $20 billion USD, but with various additions, it has now reached $21.6 billion USD. (JETP Indonesia Secretariat, 21 November 2023 (opens in a new tab))
  • A conditional target. The CIPP outlines a decarbonization scenario with a shared conditional target of 250 million tons of CO2 emissions for the on-grid electricity sector by 2030, with a renewable energy share of 44%. (JETP Indonesia Secretariat (opens in a new tab))

The RCEP trade agreement

  • Ratified in 2022. UU ini mengatur mengenai pengesahan Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (Persetujuan Kemitraan Ekonomi Komprehensif Regional) yang telah ditandatangani oleh Pemerintah Indonesia pada tanggal 15 November 2020 di Bogor, Indonesia. (unofficial translation: “This law covers the ratification of the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement, which the Government of Indonesia signed on 15 November 2020 in Bogor, Indonesia.”) (UU 24/2022 (opens in a new tab))

Talks to join the OECD

  • The first from South-East Asia. The Council of the OECD has today decided to open accession discussions with Indonesia. Indonesia's application is the first from Southeast Asia, one of the most dynamic growth regions of the world, (OECD, 20 February 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
  • The technical phase began in 2025. On 3 June 2025 Indonesia submitted its Initial Memorandum to the OECD and a formal request to join the OECD Anti-Bribery Convention (OECD, 3 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)). The OECD’s 2026 report to ministers says: Argentina, Indonesia and Thailand entered the accession process in 2024 and began the technical phase of their process in 2025. (OECD Secretary-General, report to ministers 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
W

Weaknesses

Internal · harmful

Child stunting

  • 21.5% in 2023. The national plan gives a baseline of 21.50% of children under five stunted in 2023, with targets of 18.8% for 2025 and 14.2% for 2029 (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab)).
  • Below 20% in 2024. Menteri Kesehatan Budi Gunadi Sadikin mengumumkan bahwa prevalensi stunting di Indonesia pada tahun 2024 berhasil turun menjadi 19,8 persen, untuk pertama kalinya berada di bawah angka 20 persen. (unofficial translation: “Health Minister Budi Gunadi Sadikin announced that the prevalence of stunting in Indonesia fell to 19.8 percent in 2024, below 20 percent for the first time.”) (Kemenkes, 12 November 2025 (opens in a new tab))

Also in PESTLE · Social

Slow growth of renewables

  • 13.1% at the end of 2023. The energy ministry mencatat realisasi bauran energi primer yang berasal dari energi baru dan terbarukan, pada akhir tahun 2023, mencapai 13,1 persen atau 238,1 MBOE. (unofficial translation: “recorded that the share of new and renewable energy in the primary energy mix reached 13.1 percent, or 238.1 million barrels of oil equivalent, at the end of 2023.”) (ESDM (opens in a new tab)). The ministry’s target was 23% by 2025.

Low research spending

  • 0.28% of GDP. The national plan gives science, technology and innovation spending of 0.28% of GDP in 2020, with targets of 0.30% for 2025 and 0.45% for 2029 (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab), row 23).

S

Strengths

Internal · helpful

The world’s largest nickel producer

  • About two-thirds of world mine output. Indonesia mined 2.31 million tonnes of nickel in 2024 and an estimated 2.6 million tonnes in 2025, 62% and 67% of the world total (USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (opens in a new tab), the shares are Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
  • The largest reserves. 62 million tonnes, the largest in the USGS table, against a world total of more than 140 million tonnes (USGS (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Economic

A young, large population

  • 285.7 million people. Indonesia had 285.7 million people in 2025, with a median age of 30.4 years, near the world’s 30.9 (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Social

Steady growth

  • 5.11% in 2025. ekonomi Indonesia secara keseluruhan tahun 2025 tumbuh sebesar 5,11% (yoy), meningkat dari pertumbuhan ekonomi tahun sebelumnya sebesar 5,03% (yoy). (unofficial translation: “Indonesia’s economy as a whole grew by 5.11% (year on year) in 2025, up from 5.03% the year before.”) (Bank Indonesia, 5 February 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • US$1.4 trillion. GDP was US$1,445.6 billion in 2025 (IMF World Economic Outlook, October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Also in PESTLE · Economic

Forests and peatland

  • 96 million hectares of forest. In 2022, Indonesia memiliki tutupan hutan seluas 96,16 juta hektare (unofficial translation: “Indonesia has forest cover of 96.16 million hectares”). Indonesia juga memiliki lahan gambut seluas 13,42 juta hektare (unofficial translation: “Indonesia also has 13.42 million hectares of peatland”) (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab))
  • A net sink by 2030. The forestry and land-use sector is targeted to reach minus 140 megatonnes (Mt) CO2e by 2030 (KLHK, FOLU Net Sink 2030 (opens in a new tab)).
