Indonesia GAP

Where things stand now compared with an officially agreed target.

Each row sets a target from Indonesia’s current medium-term development plan, the RPJMN 2025–2029 (Presidential Regulation 12/2025), against the latest official figure. Plan targets are often ranges; the distance is measured to the nearer end. Distances are Hawkeye’s arithmetic; where a figure has not been confirmed at source, the cell says TBC. Indonesian quotations are given as published, with unofficial translations.

Targets and latest values

Poverty rate of 4.5–5.0% by 2029

Target
The plan’s table of main targets for National Priority 6 (Tabel 3.9) sets the poverty rate at 7.0–8.0% for 2025 and 4.5–5.0% for 2029, and extreme poverty at 0% by 2029 (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029 (opens in a new tab)). Its regional chapter restates the goal: penurunan kemiskinan mencapai 4,5 persen dan kemiskinan ekstrem mencapai 0 persen (unofficial translation: “a fall in poverty to 4.5 percent and in extreme poverty to 0 percent”) (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
2024: 9.03% (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029 (opens in a new tab), Tabel 3.9).
Latest
March 2026: tingkat kemiskinan turun dari 8,47% pada Maret 2025 menjadi 8,07% pada Maret 2026. (unofficial translation: “the poverty rate fell from 8.47% in March 2025 to 8.07% in March 2026.”) (BPS, Sep 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
3.07 percentage points above the top of the 2029 range (8.07 − 5.0) (Hawkeye’s arithmetic). The March 2026 figure is 0.07 points above the top of the 2025 range (8.07 − 8.0).

Stunting among children under five down to 14.2% by 2029

Target
The plan’s human-development targets (row 11) set the prevalence of stunting in children under five at 18.8% for 2025 and 14.2% for 2029 (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
2023: 21.50% (SKI 2023, Kemenkes; Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
2024: Menteri Kesehatan Budi Gunadi Sadikin mengumumkan bahwa prevalensi stunting di Indonesia pada tahun 2024 berhasil turun menjadi 19,8 persen, untuk pertama kalinya berada di bawah angka 20 persen. (unofficial translation: “Health Minister Budi Gunadi Sadikin announced that the prevalence of stunting in Indonesia fell to 19.8 percent in 2024, below 20 percent for the first time.”) (Kemenkes, 12 Nov 2025 (opens in a new tab); SSGI 2024 survey).
Distance
5.6 percentage points above the 2029 target (19.8 − 14.2), and 1.0 above the 2025 target (19.8 − 18.8) (Hawkeye’s arithmetic; the 2024 figure is from SSGI, the baseline from SKI).

Economic growth of 8% by 2029

Target
Tabel 2.3 sets GDP growth at 5.30% for 2025 and 8.00% for 2029 (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029 (opens in a new tab)). The plan describes its purpose as reaching …dan pertumbuhan ekonomi 8 persen. (unofficial translation: “…and economic growth of 8 percent.”) (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
2024 outlook in the plan: 5.05% (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029 (opens in a new tab), Tabel 2.3).
Latest
2025: ekonomi Indonesia secara keseluruhan tahun 2025 tumbuh sebesar 5,11% (yoy), meningkat dari pertumbuhan ekonomi tahun sebelumnya sebesar 5,03% (yoy). (unofficial translation: “Indonesia’s economy grew by 5.11% (yoy) overall in 2025, up from growth of 5.03% (yoy) the previous year.”) (Bank Indonesia (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
2.89 percentage points short of the 2029 target (8.00 − 5.11), and 0.19 short of the 2025 target (5.30 − 5.11) (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
