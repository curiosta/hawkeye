Documented national responses 9

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • National Long-Term Development Plan 2025–2045 (UU 59/2024)

    Adopted by House of Representatives (DPR) and the President, law of 13 September 2024

    Undang-Undang No. 59 Tahun 2024 Tentang Rencana Pembangunan Jangka Panjang Nasional Tahun 2025-2045 dalam mendukung perwujudan Visi Indonesia Emas 2045, Negara Kesatuan Republik Indonesia yang Bersatu, Berdaulat, Maju, dan Berkelanjutan. (unofficial translation: “Law No. 59 of 2024 on the National Long-Term Development Plan 2025-2045, in support of the Golden Indonesia 2045 Vision: a Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia that is United, Sovereign, Advanced and Sustainable.”) (Bappenas, Indonesia Emas 2045 (opens in a new tab)). Its five main targets are on the SOAR page.

  • The new capital, Nusantara (UU 3/2022, amended by UU 21/2023)

    Adopted by DPR and the President, law of 15 February 2022, amended 31 October 2023

    Ibu Kota Nusantara memiliki visi sebagai kota dunia untuk semua yang dibangun dan dikelola dengan tujuan untuk: 1) menjadi kota berkelanjutan di dunia; 2) sebagai penggerak ekonomi Indonesia di masa depan; dan 3) menjadi simbol identitas nasional yang merepresentasikan keberagaman bangsa Indonesia, berdasarkan Pancasila dan Undang-Undang Dasar Negara Republik Indonesia Tahun 1945. (unofficial translation: “The capital Nusantara has a vision as a world city for all, built and managed with the aims of: 1) becoming a sustainable city of the world; 2) acting as a driver of Indonesia’s future economy; and 3) becoming a symbol of national identity that represents the diversity of the Indonesian nation, based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution.”) (UU 3/2022, BPK abstract (opens in a new tab)). The budget figures in the research were not confirmed (TBC).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Rising seas and sinking land

  • Mineral and Coal Mining Law (UU 3/2020)

    Adopted by DPR and the President, law of 10 June 2020, later amended

    Dalam Undang-Undang ini juga dilakukan pengaturan kembali terkait kebijakan peningkatan nilai tambah Mineral dan Batubara, divestasi saham, (unofficial translation: “This law also re-regulates the policy on adding value to minerals and coal, share divestment,”) (UU 3/2020, BPK abstract (opens in a new tab))

    Related SWOT · Strengths · The world’s largest nickel producer · SWOT · Threats · Trade disputes and carbon border taxes

  • Accelerating renewable power (Perpres 112/2022)

    Adopted by President, regulation of 13 September 2022

    Dalam rangka transisi energi sektor ketenagalistrikan, Menteri ESDM menyusun peta jalan percepatan pengakhiran masa operasional PLTU yang dituangkan dalam dokumen perencanaan sektoral. (unofficial translation: “For the energy transition in the power sector, the Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources draws up a roadmap to speed up ending the operation of coal-fired power plants, set out in a sector planning document.”) (Perpres 112/2022, BPK abstract (opens in a new tab)). The wording on new coal plants and a 2050 date was not confirmed (TBC).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Slow growth of renewables

  • Accelerating the reduction of stunting (Perpres 72/2021)

    Adopted by President, regulation of 5 August 2021

    percepatan penurunan stunting yang dilaksanakan secara holistik, integratif, dan berkualitas. (unofficial translation: “the acceleration of stunting reduction, carried out in a holistic, integrated and quality way.”) (Perpres 72/2021, BPK abstract (opens in a new tab))

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Child stunting · GAP · Stunting among children under five down to 14.2% by 2029

  • Carbon pricing (Perpres 98/2021, replaced by Perpres 110/2025)

    Adopted by President, Perpres 98/2021 of 29 October 2021, revoked by Perpres 110/2025

    The BPK record lists Perpres 98/2021 as no longer in force and replaced by Perpres No. 110 Tahun 2025 tentang Penyelenggaraan Instrumen Nilai Ekonomi Karbon dan Pengendalian Emisi Gas Rumah Kaca Nasional (unofficial translation: “Presidential Regulation No. 110 of 2025 on carbon economic value instruments and national greenhouse gas emission control”) (Perpres 98/2021, BPK record (opens in a new tab)). The date Perpres 110/2025 entered into force was not confirmed (TBC).

  • Enhanced Nationally Determined Contribution

    Adopted by Government of Indonesia, submitted to the UNFCCC in September 2022

    The target was further increased from 29 percent to 31.89 percent unconditionally and from 41 percent to 43.20 percent conditionally, as documented in the Enhanced NDC that was submitted to the Secretariat of the UNFCCC in September 2022. (KLHK, FOLU Net Sink 2030 (opens in a new tab)). A Second NDC was reported in October 2025. Its targets have not been checked (TBC).

  • Just Energy Transition Partnership investment plan

    Adopted by Government of Indonesia with its JETP partners, plan launched 21 November 2023

    Quoted in the SWOT Opportunities (JETP Indonesia Secretariat (opens in a new tab)): US$21.6 billion committed, and a conditional target of 250 Mt of CO2 and 44% renewables for on-grid power by 2030.

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Finance for the energy transition

  • Financial Sector Development and Strengthening Law (UU 4/2023)

    Adopted by DPR and the President, law of 12 January 2023, amended by UU 4/2026

    Its BPK abstract is quoted on the PESTLE page (UU 4/2023, BPK abstract (opens in a new tab)).

Sources checked 26 and 31 October and 12 November 2026. Indonesian quotations are given as published; English translations are unofficial.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.
