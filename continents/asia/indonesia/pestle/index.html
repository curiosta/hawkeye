Political 2 points

Governance and cooperation

  • The 2024 presidential election. Prabowo Subianto and Gibran Rakabuming Raka won 96,214,691 of 164,227,475 valid votes, 58.59% (KPU, decision 360/2024 (opens in a new tab), Lampiran I. Share: Hawkeye’s arithmetic). The decision is dated 20 March 2024.

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • Indonesia’s position at the UN. Indonesia reiterates that the Nine-Dash Line map implying historic rights claim clearly lacks international legal basis and is tantamount to upset UNCLOS 1982. (Indonesia, note to the UN, May 2020 (opens in a new tab)). The note does not name the Natuna waters. That the line overlaps Indonesia’s exclusive economic zone there is Hawkeye’s context, not a quote.

Economic 4 points

Economy and finance

  • 5.11% in 2025. ekonomi Indonesia secara keseluruhan tahun 2025 tumbuh sebesar 5,11% (yoy), meningkat dari pertumbuhan ekonomi tahun sebelumnya sebesar 5,03% (yoy). (unofficial translation: “Indonesia’s economy as a whole grew by 5.11% (year on year) in 2025, up from 5.03% the year before.”) (Bank Indonesia, 5 February 2026 (opens in a new tab))
  • US$1.4 trillion. GDP was US$1,445.6 billion in 2025 (IMF World Economic Outlook, October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Steady growth

Natural resources

  • About two-thirds of world mine output. Indonesia mined 2.31 million tonnes of nickel in 2024 and an estimated 2.6 million tonnes in 2025, 62% and 67% of the world total (USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (opens in a new tab), the shares are Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
  • The largest reserves. 62 million tonnes, the largest in the USGS table, against a world total of more than 140 million tonnes (USGS (opens in a new tab)).

Social 3 points

Population and demographics

  • 285.7 million people. Indonesia had 285.7 million people in 2025, with a median age of 30.4 years, near the world’s 30.9 (UN World Population Prospects 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · A young, large population

Pandemics and health

  • 21.5% in 2023. The national plan gives a baseline of 21.50% of children under five stunted in 2023, with targets of 18.8% for 2025 and 14.2% for 2029 (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab)).
  • Below 20% in 2024. Menteri Kesehatan Budi Gunadi Sadikin mengumumkan bahwa prevalensi stunting di Indonesia pada tahun 2024 berhasil turun menjadi 19,8 persen, untuk pertama kalinya berada di bawah angka 20 persen. (unofficial translation: “Health Minister Budi Gunadi Sadikin announced that the prevalence of stunting in Indonesia fell to 19.8 percent in 2024, below 20 percent for the first time.”) (Kemenkes, 12 November 2025 (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Child stunting

Technological 2 points

Technology and AI

  • QRIS digital payments. Sampai Semester I 2025, QRIS telah menjangkau 57 juta pengguna dan 39,3 juta merchant yang 93,16% di antaranya adalah UMKM. (unofficial translation: “By the first half of 2025, QRIS had reached 57 million users and 39.3 million merchants, 93.16% of them micro, small and medium enterprises.”) (Bank Indonesia, QRIS (opens in a new tab))
  • 0.28% of GDP. The national plan gives science, technology and innovation spending of 0.28% of GDP in 2020, with targets of 0.30% for 2025 and 0.45% for 2029 (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab), row 23).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Low research spending

Legal 2 points

Economy and finance

  • Financial sector reform. Undang-Undang ini mereformasi sektor keuangan dengan mengatur kelembagaan dan Stabilitas Sistem Keuangan dan pengembangan dan penguatan industri. (unofficial translation: “This law reforms the financial sector by regulating institutions and financial system stability, and the development and strengthening of industry.”) (UU 4/2023, BPK abstract (opens in a new tab)). It was amended by UU 4/2026.

Natural resources

  • The nickel ore export ban at the WTO. A panel found the ban inconsistent with Article XI:1 of the GATT 1994. On 8 December 2022, Indonesia notified the DSB of its decision to appeal to the Appellate Body certain issues of law and legal interpretations in the panel report. (WTO, DS592 page (opens in a new tab))

Environmental 5 points

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • 96 million hectares of forest. In 2022, Indonesia memiliki tutupan hutan seluas 96,16 juta hektare (unofficial translation: “Indonesia has forest cover of 96.16 million hectares”). Indonesia juga memiliki lahan gambut seluas 13,42 juta hektare (unofficial translation: “Indonesia also has 13.42 million hectares of peatland”) (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab))
  • A net sink by 2030. The forestry and land-use sector is targeted to reach minus 140 megatonnes (Mt) CO2e by 2030 (KLHK, FOLU Net Sink 2030 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Forests and peatland

Natural and cosmic hazards

  • 127 active volcanoes. Indonesia memiliki 127 gunungapi aktif (unofficial translation: “Indonesia has 127 active volcanoes”) (BNPB, Data Bencana Indonesia 2023 (opens in a new tab))
  • 5,400 disasters in 2023. BNPB recorded 5,400 disaster events in 2023. Jumlah tersebut naik dibandingkan dengan tahun sebelumnya yaitu 3.544 kejadian (unofficial translation: “That number is up compared with the year before, namely 3,544 events”). Bencana hidrometeorologi mendominasi kejadian bencana (unofficial translation: “Hydro-meteorological disasters dominated the events”) (BNPB (opens in a new tab))
  • Coasts at risk. Kombinasi antara penurunan muka tanah dan kenaikan muka air laut semakin memperparah risiko di wilayah pesisir. (unofficial translation: “The combination of land subsidence and sea-level rise makes the risk in coastal areas worse.”) (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab))

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
