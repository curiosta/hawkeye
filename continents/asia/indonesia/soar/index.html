A Aspirations

Indonesia’s long-term plan, the RPJPN 2025–2045, sets the vision of a Golden Indonesia in 2045. The medium-term plan, the RPJMN 2025–2029, sets the steps to 2029. Indonesian quotations are given as published. English translations are unofficial.

Golden Indonesia 2045

National Long-Term Development Plan (UU 59/2024), as presented by Bappenas

Undang-Undang No. 59 Tahun 2024 Tentang Rencana Pembangunan Jangka Panjang Nasional Tahun 2025-2045 dalam mendukung perwujudan Visi Indonesia Emas 2045, Negara Kesatuan Republik Indonesia yang Bersatu, Berdaulat, Maju, dan Berkelanjutan. (unofficial translation: “Law No. 59 of 2024 on the National Long-Term Development Plan 2025-2045, in support of the Golden Indonesia 2045 Vision: a Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia that is United, Sovereign, Advanced and Sustainable.”) (Bappenas, Indonesia Emas 2045 (opens in a new tab))

Main targets for 2045

RPJPN 2025–2045, as presented by Bappenas

The plan sets five main targets (Bappenas, Indonesia Emas 2045 (opens in a new tab)). Three of them, with figures:

  1. Pendapatan per kapita Indonesia menjadi ke-5 terbesar dunia sekitar USD23.000-30.300. (unofficial translation: “Indonesia’s per-capita income to become the 5th largest in the world, about USD 23,000-30,300.”)
  2. Tingkat kemiskinan pada kisaran 0,5-0,8 persen, (unofficial translation: “A poverty rate in the range of 0.5-0.8 percent,”)
  3. Tingkat penurunan intensitas emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK) mencapai 93,5% serta Indeks Kualitas Lingkungan Hidup meningkat menjadi 83,0. (unofficial translation: “The reduction in greenhouse gas emission intensity reaches 93.5% and the Environmental Quality Index rises to 83.0.”)

The site links “5th largest in the world” to per-capita income. The law’s own article text was not read (TBC).

Steps to 2029

Medium-Term Development Plan 2025–2029 (Perpres 12/2025), set 10 February 2025

The plan is penjabaran dari Visi, Misi, Program Prioritas (Asta Cita) Presiden-Wakil Presiden terpilih untuk mencapai penurunan kemiskinan, peningkatan kualitas sumber daya manusia menuju unggul, dan pertumbuhan ekonomi 8 persen. (unofficial translation: “the elaboration of the vision, mission and priority programmes (Asta Cita) of the elected President and Vice-President to reduce poverty, raise the quality of human resources and reach economic growth of 8 percent.”) (Perpres 12/2025, RPJMN 2025–2029, Lampiran I (opens in a new tab), p. 78)

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.
