Each row sets a target from an official Indian text against the latest official figure. India’s fiscal year runs from April to March. Distances are Hawkeye’s arithmetic. The net-zero goal for 2070 is under SOAR, because it has no measurable latest value yet.

Targets and latest values

Emissions intensity of GDP 45% below 2005 by 2030

Target
As per the updated NDC, India now stands committed to reduce Emissions Intensity of its GDP by 45 percent by 2030, from 2005 level (Cabinet decision, PIB 3 August 2022 (opens in a new tab)). The NDC is India’s Nationally Determined Contribution under the Paris Agreement.
Baseline
2005 level (the NDC’s reference year).
Latest
the emission intensity of its GDP has been reduced by 33 percent between 2005 and 2019 (Ministry of Environment, PIB 18 December 2023 (opens in a new tab), citing India’s Third National Communication).
Distance
12 percentage points to go, measured on 2019 data.
Assessment by the tracking agency
No official assessment against the 45% target has been confirmed at source (TBC).

About 50% of power capacity from non-fossil sources by 2030

Target
achieve about 50 percent cumulative electric power installed capacity from non-fossil fuel-based energy resources by 2030 (PIB, 3 August 2022 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
32.54% on 31 March 2014 (81.16 GW of 249.42 GW) (Ministry of Power, PIB 16 March 2026 (opens in a new tab), Annexure).
Latest
51.93% on 31 December 2025 (266.79 GW of 513.73 GW). 50.08% on 30 June 2025 (Ministry of Power (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
Target passed in June 2025.
Assessment by the tracking agency
The ministry: more than five years ahead of the target set under its Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) to the Paris Agreement (PIB 16 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

500 GW of non-fossil power capacity by 2030

Target
The Government of India has set a target of achieving 500 GW of installed electricity generation capacity from non-fossil fuel-based sources by 2030 (Ministry of Power, PIB 30 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
81.16 GW on 31 March 2014 (Ministry of Power (opens in a new tab), Annexure).
Latest
266.79 GW on 31 December 2025 (Ministry of Power, PIB 16 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
233.21 GW still to add.
Assessment by the tracking agency
No official assessment confirmed at source (TBC).

Central fiscal deficit below 4.5% of GDP in 2025-26

Target
Set in Budget 2021-22: a fiscal deficit below 4.5 per cent of GDP in FY 2025-26 (PIB, 1 February 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
Not published here (TBC).
Latest
4.4% of GDP in 2025-26. This is a Revised Estimate, not a final figure. The target for 2026-27 is 4.3% (PIB, 1 February 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
0.1 percentage points below the target, on the Revised Estimate.
Assessment by the tracking agency
The Ministry of Finance: As announced in Budget 2021-22, the Government made true its intention of reaching a fiscal deficit below 4.5 per cent of GDP in FY 2025-26. The next aim is a central government debt of 50 per cent of GDP, plus or minus 1, by 2030-31 (PIB, 1 February 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Tap water for every rural household by December 2028

Target
JJM 2.0 aims to declare all Gram Panchayats as “Har Ghar Jal” by ensuring tap water connections to all 19.36 crore rural households across the country by December 2028. (Ministry of Jal Shakti, PIB 31 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)). JJM is the Jal Jeevan Mission.
Baseline
TBC. The release says that over 12.59 crore households were connected after the mission started.
Latest
out of around 19.36 crore rural households in the country, about 15.83 crore (around 81.8%) households now have tap water supply in their homes. (PIB, 31 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
About 3.53 crore households still to connect.
Assessment by the tracking agency
No official assessment against the 2028 date confirmed at source (TBC).

Sources checked 3 and 12 November 2026.

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
