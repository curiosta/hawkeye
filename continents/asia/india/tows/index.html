Documented national responses 10

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • PM-Surya Ghar: rooftop solar

    Adopted by Union Cabinet, 29 February 2024

    The Cabinet has approved PM-Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana with a total outlay of Rs.75,021 crore for installing rooftop solar and providing free electricity up to 300 units every month for One Crore households. (PIB, 29 February 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Strengths · Half of power capacity from non-fossil sources

  • National Green Hydrogen Mission

    Adopted by Union Cabinet, 4 January 2023

    The initial outlay for the Mission will be Rs.19,744 crore (PIB, 4 January 2023 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Green hydrogen and solar factories

  • IndiaAI Mission

    Adopted by Union Cabinet, 7 March 2024

    Outlay Rs.10,371.92 crore, with Public AI Compute Infrastructure of 10,000 or more GPUs (PIB, 7 March 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

  • Semicon India programme

    Adopted by Union Cabinet, 2021

    the Union Cabinet in 2021 approved the Semicon India programme with a financial outlay of INR 76,000 crore (PIB press note (opens in a new tab)).

  • Incentives for solar panel factories

    Adopted by Union Cabinet, 21 September 2022

    Tranche II of the Production Linked Incentive scheme for high-efficiency solar panels, with an outlay of Rs.19,500 crore (PIB, 21 September 2022 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Green hydrogen and solar factories

  • PM E-DRIVE: electric vehicles

    Adopted by Union Cabinet, 11 September 2024

    A scheme for electric vehicles and charging with an outlay of Rs.10,900 crore over a period of two years (PIB, 11 September 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Imported oil and critical minerals

  • PM GatiShakti National Master Plan

    Adopted by Launched October 2021

    The PM GatiShakti National Master Plan (PMGS NMP), launched by the Hon'ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi in October 2021, is completing five years. It is a shared digital map for planning roads, railways, ports and other infrastructure across ministries (PIB, 13 October 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

  • PM Vishwakarma: support for craftspeople

    Adopted by Union Cabinet, 16 August 2023

    A scheme for traditional artisans and craftspeople. Scheme to have a financial outlay of Rs.13,000 crore (PIB, 16 August 2023 (opens in a new tab)).

  • Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0: rural tap water

    Adopted by Union Cabinet, 10 March 2026

    The Union Cabinet on 10 March 2026 approved Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) 2.0, with an enhancement of the total outlay to ₹8.69 lakh crore (PIB, 31 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related GAP · Tap water for every rural household by December 2028

  • National Clean Air Programme

    Adopted by Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, January 2019

    The ministry launched National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) in January, 2019 with an aim to improve air quality in 131 cities (PIB, 23 March 2023 (opens in a new tab)).

Sources checked 3 and 12 November 2026.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
