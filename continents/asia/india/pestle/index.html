Political 2 points

Governance and cooperation

  • 977,965,560 electors in 2024. The 2024 general election to the Lok Sabha, the lower house of Parliament, covered 542 constituencies. Turnout was 66.1% including postal ballots (Election Commission of India, statistical report 2024 (opens in a new tab), items 1, 3 and 6).

Governance and cooperation

  • 41% of the tax pool for the States. The Sixteenth Finance Commission sent its report for 2026-27 to 2030-31 to the President on 17 November 2025 (PIB, 17 November 2025 (opens in a new tab)). The Budget release says: SFC has recommended for retaining the share of devolution to the States at 41 per cent of divisible pool. (PIB, 1 February 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Economic 2 points

Economy and finance

  • Real growth of 7.7% in 2025-26. Real GDP at constant prices grew 7.7%. Nominal GDP was ₹346.36 lakh crore (MoSPI, PIB 5 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Fast growth

Economy and finance

  • Policy repo rate 5.50%. On 12 November 2026, the Reserve Bank of India listed its policy repo rate at 5.50% (Reserve Bank of India, current rates (opens in a new tab)). The repo rate is the rate at which the central bank lends to banks.

Social 2 points

Society and mindset

  • 40.0% of women in the labour force. In 2025, 40.0% of women aged 15 and over were in the labour force, against 79.1% of men (PLFS Annual Report 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Few women in paid work

Human development

  • Multidimensional poverty down to 11.28%. The share fell from 29.17% in 2013-14 to 11.28% in 2022-23, by NITI Aayog’s estimate (PIB, 15 January 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Poverty

Technological 2 points

Technology and AI

  • Over 24,162 crore UPI payments in 2025-26. The number was 2 crore in 2016-17 (PIB factsheet (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Digital public infrastructure

Technology and AI

  • ₹10,371.92 crore for artificial intelligence. In March 2024, the Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi has approved the comprehensive national-level IndiaAI mission with a budget outlay of Rs.10,371.92 crore. The plan includes Public AI Compute Infrastructure of 10,000 or more GPUs (PIB, 7 March 2024 (opens in a new tab)). GPUs are the chips used to train AI models.

Legal 2 points

Governance and cooperation

  • Data protection rules from November 2025. The Government of India notified the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Rules, 2025 on 14 November 2025. This marks the full operationalisation of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act ). The Rules introduce an eighteen-month period for phased compliance. (PIB, 17 November 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

Governance and cooperation

  • New criminal code from 1 July 2024. The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023 replaced the Indian Penal Code, 1860. It came into force on 1 July 2024. (PIB, 19 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Environmental 2 points

Climate change / global warming

  • 3,193 Mt of fossil CO2 in 2024. That is 8.3% of the world total and second in Asia after China. Per person, it is 2.2 tonnes, against a world average of 4.7 tonnes (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab). Share: Hawkeye’s arithmetic).

Energy transition

  • 51.93% of power capacity non-fossil. On 31 December 2025, non-fossil sources made up 266.79 GW of 513.73 GW of installed capacity. India first passed 50% in June 2025 (Ministry of Power, PIB 16 March 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Half of power capacity from non-fossil sources · GAP · About 50% of power capacity from non-fossil sources by 2030

Sources checked 3 and 12 November 2026.

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Official sources, and labelled analysis. Hawkeye reports official and authoritative sources, quoted or attributed, with a link. Documented goals and programmes appear as those bodies stated them. TOWS strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice to any person or firm. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
