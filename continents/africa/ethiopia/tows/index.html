Documented national responses 5

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Tight monetary policy

    Adopted by National Bank of Ethiopia, Monetary Policy Committee (3 November 2026)

    The bank says it will absorb excess liquidity through open market operation (OMO) (National Bank of Ethiopia, 3 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · High inflation

  • Debt treatment with official creditors

    Adopted by Government of Ethiopia and its Official Creditor Committee, MoU of 2 July 2025

    France, Italy and Israel signed bilateral agreements in February, March and April 2026 (Paris Club (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Low income and a large debt · SWOT · Opportunities · Debt relief and an IMF programme

  • The Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam

    Adopted by Ethiopian Electric Power (inaugurated 9 September 2025)

    5,150 MW from 13 turbines (Ethiopian Electric Power, 9 September 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Millions without electricity · SWOT · Opportunities · The Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam

  • The Green Legacy Initiative

    Adopted by Government of Ethiopia (since 2019)

    Over 40 billion seedlings planted by 2023/24, with a target of 50 billion by 2030 (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Strengths · Tree planting · SWOT · Threats · Drought in East Africa

  • The national nutrition programme

    Adopted by Government of Ethiopia (expansion phase from July 2021)

    The programme initially targeted 240 high-burden woredas and, now aims to scale up to approximately 700 woredas by the end of 2025. A woreda is a district (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Child stunting

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Placing a response in a cell is Hawkeye’s only editorial step. Responses are not ranked.
