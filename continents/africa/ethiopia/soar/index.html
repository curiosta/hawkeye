A Aspirations

Ethiopia sets its long-term goals in a ten-year plan for 2021 to 2030. The Aspirations below quote that plan and the central bank, each labelled with its status.

An African Beacon of Prosperity

Vision of the Ten Years Development Plan 2021–2030

The plan lays a long-term vision of making Ethiopia an “African Beacon of Prosperity” by creating the necessary and sufficient conditions. (Ten Years Development Plan 2021–2030 (opens in a new tab)). It defines prosperity in terms of outcomes such as:

  1. Improvement in income levels and wealth accumulations so that every citizen would be able to satisfy their basic needs and aspirations.
  2. Basic economic and social services such as food, clean water, shelter, health, education, and other basic services should be accessible to every citizen regardless of their economic status.

Reliable and affordable power

Energy targets of the Ten Years Development Plan, 2020/21 to 2029/30

The plan aims for quality electric power service that is accessible, equitable and affordable and sets targets including 19,900 MW of capacity and 96% grid coverage (Ten Years Development Plan 2021–2030 (opens in a new tab)).

Single-digit inflation

Medium-term goal of the National Bank of Ethiopia

The committee noted that maintaining tight monetary policy stance is indispensable to achieve NBE’s medium term goal of single-digit inflation. (National Bank of Ethiopia, 3 November 2026 (opens in a new tab))

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
