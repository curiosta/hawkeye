Targets and latest values

19,900 MW of generating capacity by 2029/30

Target
The ten-year plan aims To raise power generation capacity from 4,478 megawatts to 19,900 megawatts (Ten Years Development Plan 2021–2030 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
4,478 MW in 2020/21 (Ten Years Development Plan 2021–2030 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
9,730 MW in the fiscal year 2025/26 (Ethiopian Electric Power, 9 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
10,170 MW to add, about 66% of the planned increase still to come (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in these sources.

Single-digit inflation

Target
The central bank’s medium term goal of single-digit inflation (National Bank of Ethiopia, 3 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
Not given in this row (TBC).
Latest
14.9% in September 2026 (National Bank of Ethiopia, 3 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
At least 5.0 percentage points to go (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
The committee expects inflation to remain in double digits over the six-month forecast horizon (National Bank of Ethiopia, 3 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

50 billion seedlings by 2030

Target
The government aims to plant 50 billion seedlings by 2030, with 7.5 billion targeted for 2024/25 alone. (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab))
Baseline
Planting began in 2019 (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
Over 40 billion seedlings by 2023/24 (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
Fewer than 10 billion still to plant, as of 2023/24 (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
The review says the initiative exceeded its goal of 20 billion (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

End stunting in children under two by 2030

Target
The national nutrition programme is Anchored in the goal of ending stunting among children under two by 2030 (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
38% of children in 2015/16 (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
39% in 2021/22 (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)). The review does not give the age group for this figure here, so it may not match the goal’s children under two (TBC).
Distance
Not calculable until the age groups are confirmed (TBC).
Assessment by the tracking agency
The review calls stunting the persistent challenge of chronic malnutrition (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
