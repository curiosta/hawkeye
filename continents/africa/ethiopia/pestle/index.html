Political 1 point

Governance and cooperation

  • The ruling party won the 2026 election. The election board announced final results on 21 June 2026 for the election of 1 June. As the Prosperity Party won a majority of seats in the House of Peoples’ Representatives, it has secured the mandate for the incumbent party to form the next federal government and appoint a new cabinet. (Ethiopian News Agency, 21 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The board counts 54,057,861 registered voters and 51,734,193 who voted (National Election Board of Ethiopia (opens in a new tab)).

Economic 2 points

Economy and finance

  • Policy rate kept at 16%. On 3 November 2026 the committee proposed to maintain the NBR at 16 percent and keep the ±300 basis point corridor band unchanged. The NBR is the National Bank Rate (National Bank of Ethiopia, 3 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · High inflation

Economy and finance

  • Money supply growing fast. Annual reserve money growth accelerated to 87.2 percent in the first quarter of 2026/27, mainly because of the central bank’s gold operations (National Bank of Ethiopia, 3 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Social 1 point

Water and food security

  • Stunting not falling. Child stunting rose from 38% in 2015/16 to 39% in 2021/22 (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Child stunting · GAP · End stunting in children under two by 2030

Technological 1 point

Energy transition

  • A new giant hydro plant. The Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam, with 5,150 MW, was inaugurated on 9 September 2025 (Ethiopian Electric Power, 9 September 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · The Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam · GAP · 19,900 MW of generating capacity by 2029/30

Legal 1 point

Economy and finance

  • A new central bank law. The Monetary Policy Committee works under the NBE’s Establishment Proclamation 1359/2025, Article 23 (National Bank of Ethiopia, 3 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Environmental 1 point

Biodiversity and ecosystems

  • Tree planting. Over 40 billion trees have been planted since 2019 under the Green Legacy Initiative (Ethiopia Voluntary National Review 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Tree planting · GAP · 50 billion seedlings by 2030

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
