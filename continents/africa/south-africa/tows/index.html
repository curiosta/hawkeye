South Africa TOWS

Hawkeye analysis

Strategy cells on this page are Hawkeye’s own analysis. Each recommendation pairs a verified strength or weakness with a verified opportunity or threat, and compares it with a documented case from another place. The precedent facts are sourced. The pairing, the transfer and the recommendation are Hawkeye’s work, not official policy and not advice to any person or firm.

The documented responses listed lower on this page remain official programmes or instruments, each confirmed at their official source. Several of those responses already work in the same area as a recommendation. A recommendation is a pairing and a lesson from elsewhere. It is not a claim that South Africa has no policy in that area. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.

The four cells

  • SO Responses that use strengths to pursue opportunities

    • Use the open electricity market and transition finance to raise the renewable share of power

      Pairs SWOT · Strengths · An open electricity market in law · SWOT · Opportunities · Climate finance for a just energy transition

      Use the open electricity market now in law, and just-transition finance, to raise the renewable share of power. Official South African programmes already include the Electricity Regulation Amendment Act 38 of 2024 and the Just Energy Transition Investment Plan. This recommendation pairs that legal market strength with the finance opportunity. It is not a claim that the market law is missing.

      Analogous case. Portugal, a smaller economy, raised the renewable share of electricity production while cutting energy import dependence. The Directorate-General for Energy and Geology (DGEG) states: A dependência energética em 2024 foi de 64,3%, tendo descido 2,4 p.p. face a 2023. Energy dependence in 2024 was 64.3%, 2.4 percentage points lower than in 2023. O contributo das fontes de energia renovável (%FER) no consumo final bruto de energia, em 2024, foi de 36,5%, sendo o contributo das fontes de energia renovável na produção de eletricidade (%FER-E), de 65,8%. The renewable share of electricity production was 65.8% in 2024. (DGEG, Balanço Energético Nacional 2024 (opens in a new tab), published 31 October 2025).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. The Electricity Regulation Amendment Act 38 of 2024, assented on 16 August 2024, provides for an open market platform for competitive electricity trading, and the 2022 joint statement on the Just Energy Transition Investment Plan identifies US$98 billion in financial requirements over five years (live SWOT). Portugal raised the renewable share of electricity to 65.8% in 2024 and cut energy dependence. The analogy is limited because the US$98 billion figure is a requirement, not money already disbursed, and because a market law does not by itself add megawatts. Portugal is a smaller European economy with a different starting mix. Coal still supplied 81.5% of the electricity delivered by Eskom and independent power producers in the year to March 2026, which remains a live weakness.

  • ST Responses that use strengths to reduce threats

    • Cut the carbon in platinum-group metal production to meet border-carbon rules

      Pairs SWOT · Strengths · The world’s leading platinum producer · SWOT · Threats · Carbon border taxes on exports

      Cut the carbon in platinum-group metal mining and refining, so that border-carbon charges do less harm to export access. Official South African programmes already include the Climate Change Act 22 of 2024 and the just-energy-transition plan. This recommendation pairs platinum strength with that trade threat. It does not claim an opened official use of platinum in electrolysers, and it does not pair platinum with electric-vehicle demand.

      Analogous case. Sweden’s state-owned miner LKAB and the steelmaker SSAB ran the HYBRIT project. The Government Offices of Sweden record: SSAB, LKAB produced the world's first hydrogen-reduced DRI (Direct Reduced Iron) for fossil free steel production as part of HYBRIT (Government Offices of Sweden, Annual Report for State-owned Enterprises 2021 (opens in a new tab), LKAB section). The Swedish Energy Agency later stated that the demonstration plant has potential for cutting Sweden´s carbon emissions by 1.8 million tonnes annually and can produce 1.35 million tonnes of fossil-free sponge iron annually at full operation, after the EU Innovation Fund granted the project € 108 million for the demonstration phase (Swedish Energy Agency, 14 December 2023 (opens in a new tab)).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. South Africa produced an estimated 120,000 kg of platinum in 2024, about 71% of the world total, and the Reserve Bank flags high carbon intensity against a low domestic carbon price compared with major trading partners (live SWOT). HYBRIT shows a documented first industrial step toward fossil-free metal reduction, with EU innovation funding. The 1.8 million tonne figure is a potential at full operation, not a result already delivered. The analogy is limited because HYBRIT is iron, not platinum-group metals, and because Sweden is a smaller high-income economy with different ore, power prices and state-owned firms. Weaker platinum-group metal output is a separate live threat, driven by prices, deep-level costs and labour disputes, and decarbonisation does not fix that. A shift to electric vehicles can also cut demand for catalysts in conventional engines, so platinum is not paired with the electric-vehicle opportunity here.

  • WO Responses that use opportunities to reduce weaknesses

    • Use just-transition finance to cut the coal share of electricity

      Pairs SWOT · Weaknesses · Coal-dependent electricity · SWOT · Opportunities · Climate finance for a just energy transition

      Use just-transition finance to cut the coal share of electricity. Official South African programmes already include the Just Energy Transition Investment Plan, the Climate Change Act, and Eskom debt relief. This recommendation pairs the coal-power weakness with that finance opportunity. The US$98 billion figure in the plan is a requirement, not money already disbursed.

      Analogous case. The UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero records that The last UK coal-fired power station closed in England in September 2024. In the same official document: In September 2024, Ratcliffe-on-Soar, the last coal-fired power station in the UK, closed. UK fossil fuel generation decreased by 16 per cent compared to 2023. Coal-fired generation’s share of UK electricity was only 0.7 per cent in 2024, a record low. The low carbon share of total UK generation stood at its highest value on the time series at 64.7 per cent. Renewables’ share rose 3.9 percentage points to 50.4 per cent. (DESNZ, Electricity generation and supply, 2020 to 2024 (opens in a new tab)). Energy Trends December 2024 confirms: Ratcliffe-on-Soar closed at the end of the third quarter on 30 September 2024. (DESNZ, Energy Trends December 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. Coal supplied 81.5% of the 202,890 GWh delivered by Eskom and independent power producers in the year to March 2026, and made up 71.0% of their 55,943 MW nominal capacity (live SWOT). The United Kingdom closed its last coal-fired station and cut coal’s share of electricity to 0.7% in 2024. The analogy is limited because the UK started from a different plant fleet, grid and income path, and because coal is still dominant in the latest South African year in the live SWOT. The US$98 billion figure is a requirement, not a disbursement. The green-hydrogen strategy is approved for implementation, which is not a realised change in the generation mix.

  • WT Responses that reduce weaknesses and limit threats

    • Cut municipal water losses and reuse treated wastewater as drought risk stays high

      Pairs SWOT · Weaknesses · Municipal water losses · SWOT · Threats · Drought and extreme weather

      Cut municipal water losses, and reuse treated wastewater where treatment already exists, as drought and extreme weather already hit the wider region. Official South African programmes already include the National Water and Sanitation Master Plan. This recommendation pairs the loss of municipal water with the drought threat.

      Analogous case. Israel combined regulation, reuse and irrigation reform in a water-stressed climate. A 2023 report by the United States Environmental Protection Agency, after a visit hosted with the Israel Water Authority and other Israeli ministries, states: After drastic infrastructure, regulatory, and institutional reform, Israel transformed from one of the world’s most water-stressed countries to a water secure country, becoming a global leader in agricultural water reuse. Today, nearly 90 percent of Israel’s treated wastewater is reused for irrigation purposes. (US EPA EPA-822-S-23-001, March 2023 (opens in a new tab), Background/Purpose).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. Non-revenue water in South African municipalities is estimated at an average of 41%, with about R9.9 billion of potential revenue lost each year (live SWOT). The most recent El Niño cycle brought Southern Africa’s worst drought in more than a century, leaving 61 million people in the region in need of assistance (live SWOT, a regional figure, not a South Africa-only count). Israel documents a national reuse result of nearly 90% of treated wastewater for irrigation. The analogy is limited because leakage control and wastewater reuse are related tools, but they are not the same. The opened US EPA source does not report a turnaround in a 41% municipal non-revenue-water rate. Israel is a smaller country with a different water law and a large desalination role. Reuse and leak repair do not end a regional drought.

    • Cut national fossil emissions as drought and extreme weather intensify

      Pairs SWOT · Weaknesses · Africa’s largest emitter · SWOT · Threats · Drought and extreme weather

      Cut South Africa’s fossil carbon dioxide, the largest such total in Africa, as drought and extreme weather already affect the region. Official South African programmes already include the Climate Change Act 22 of 2024 and the just-energy-transition plan. This recommendation pairs that emissions weakness with the drought threat.

      Analogous case. The European Environment Agency, in its 9 November 2026 press release on the Trends and Projections report, states: In 2025, net EU greenhouse gas emissions under the scope of the European Climate Law dropped by 39% below 1990 levels, and This drop also came while gross domestic product and populations grew by 74% and 8% respectively over the same period. The figures for 2025 are preliminary. The same release records that the pace of cuts slowed in 2025, and that year-on-year emissions rose in domestic transport and buildings (EEA, 9 November 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. South Africa emitted 440 Mt of fossil CO2 in 2024, 1.1% of the world total and 29.3% of Africa’s, or 6.9 tonnes per person against a world average of 4.7 t (live SWOT). The EU cut net emissions 39% below 1990 while GDP grew. The 2025 figures are preliminary. The analogy is limited because coal still supplied 81.5% of delivered electricity in the year to March 2026, a much higher share than in the EU mix, and because transport and buildings in the EU still saw emissions rise in 2025. Emissions cuts do not at once end a regional drought. The 61 million people in need is a Southern Africa figure, not a South Africa-only count.

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every documented national response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Those responses are not ranked. Strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice.
