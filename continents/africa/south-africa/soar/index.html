A Aspirations

South Africa’s long-term goals are set out in the National Development Plan 2030. The Medium-Term Development Plan 2024–2029 sets three strategic priorities for the current government. The Aspirations below are quoted from both.

The NDP vision for 2030

National Development Plan 2030 (2012), a policy plan, not a law

The vision statement opens: Now in 2030 we live in a country which we have remade. We have created a home where everybody feels free yet bounded to others; where everyone embraces their full potential. (National Development Plan 2030 (opens in a new tab), p. 14)

Three strategic priorities, 2024–2029

Medium-Term Development Plan 2024–2029

The plan’s three priorities:

  1. Inclusive growth and job creation is the Apex Priority for the 7th Administration. (MTDP 2024–2029 (opens in a new tab), p. 25)
  2. Strategic Priority 2 seeks to reduce poverty and tackle the high cost of living. (MTDP 2024–2029 (opens in a new tab), p. 37)
  3. Strategic Priority 3 aims to build a capable, ethical and developmental state, which is critical for the success of the other two Strategic Priorities. (MTDP 2024–2029 (opens in a new tab), p. 24)

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.
