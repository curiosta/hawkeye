South Africa PESTLE

Political 3 points

Governance and cooperation

  • A coalition plan. the MTDP 2024-2029 integrates the Government of National Unity’s (GNU) Statement of Intent with the National Development Plan 2030 (NDP 2030) to drive inclusive growth, improved service delivery, and better living conditions for all citizens. (DPME statement on the MTDP (opens in a new tab))

Conflict and nuclear weapons

  • A 2,900-strong deployment. In February 2024 the government reported that South Africa is to deploy at least 2 900 members of the South African National Defence Force to assist in the fight against illegal armed groups in the eastern Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC). The SADC Mission in the Democratic Republic of Congo (SAMIDRC) was deployed on 15 December 2023 (SAnews, 13 February 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Withdrawal under way, not complete. SADC states: As previously announced on 01 May 2025, the official withdrawal of SAMIDRC commenced on 29 April 2025. (SADC, 12 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)). On 26 June 2025 the government said: About 1 718 SANDF troops have now arrived in the country, and more are expected to arrive over the next few weeks. (SAnews, 26 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)). Hawkeye has not confirmed at source when the return was completed.

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Losses in the eastern DRC mission

Economic 4 points

Economy and finance

  • Growth under 1%. The annual increase in real GDP of 0,6% in 2024 was primarily led by higher economic activities in finance, real estate and business services; growth was 0.7% in 2023 (Stats SA, GDP Q4 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
  • 33.7% unemployed. Statistics South Africa reports: The official unemployment rate increased by 0,1 of a percentage point to 33,7% in Q3: 2026 compared with Q2: 2026. During the same period, the number of employed individuals increased by 101 000 to 16,8 million, and the number of unemployed persons grew by 97 000 to reach 8,6 million. (QLFS Q3 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Mass unemployment and slow growth

Natural resources

  • About 71% of world platinum. The USGS describes South Africa as the world’s leading producer of PGM-containing mined material, and estimates 2024 platinum mine production at 120,000 kg of a world 170,000 kg, and palladium at 72,000 kg of 190,000 kg (USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Falling production. The U.S. Geological Survey reports that South African production of platinum-group metals fell in 2024 owing to declining prices, higher costs associated with deep-level mining, labor disputes, and ongoing disruptions to the supply of electricity. (USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 (opens in a new tab), PGM chapter).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · The world’s leading platinum producer · SWOT · Threats · Weaker platinum-group metal output

Social 3 points

Economy and finance

  • Wider measures. the combined rate of unemployment and potential labour force (LU3) stood at 44,0% in the third quarter of 2026. The composite measure of labour underutilisation remained unchanged at 46,3% compared with Q2: 2026 (QLFS Q3 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Mass unemployment and slow growth

Human development

  • A Gini index of 0.54. The World Bank’s Poverty and Inequality Platform gives a Gini index of 0.5405 for the 2022 survey (consumption basis); the poorest 40% received about 10.5% of consumption (Hawkeye’s sum of deciles 1–4) (World Bank PIP (opens in a new tab)).
  • Over half below the upper-bound line. Approximately 30.3 million South Africans (55.5% of the population) live in poverty, as defined by the national upper-bound poverty line. (MTDP 2024–2029 (opens in a new tab), p. 37)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Poverty and inequality

Technological 3 points

Energy transition

  • Competitive trading. The Electricity Regulation Amendment Act 38 of 2024 provides for an open market platform that allows for competitive electricity trading (Electricity Regulation Amendment Act 38 of 2024 (opens in a new tab), assented 16 August 2024).
  • 81.5% from coal. Coal supplied 81.5% of the 202,890 GWh delivered by Eskom and independent power producers in the year to March 2026, and made up 71.0% of their 55,943 MW nominal capacity (Eskom Integrated Report 2026 (opens in a new tab), p. 8). Eskom’s coal-fired stations generated 165,431 GWh in that year.

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · An open electricity market in law · SWOT · Weaknesses · Coal-dependent electricity

Technology and AI

  • MeerKAT. The MeerKAT telescope is an array of 64 interlinked receptors. It is a precursor to the Square Kilometre Array (SKA) telescope and will be integrated into the mid-frequency component of SKA Phase 1. (SARAO, About MeerKAT (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Radio astronomy: MeerKAT

Legal 2 points

Climate change / global warming

  • The Climate Change Act 22 of 2024. Its long title: To enable the development of an effective climate change response and a long-term, just transition to a low-carbon and climate-resilient economy and society for South Africa in the context of sustainable development; and to provide for matters connected therewith. (Climate Change Act 22 of 2024 (opens in a new tab)). It was assented to on 18 July 2024. Hawkeye has not checked its commencement date.

Governance and cooperation

  • The Public Procurement Act 28 of 2024. Its long title: To regulate public procurement; to prescribe a framework within which preferential procurement must be implemented; and to provide for matters connected therewith. (Public Procurement Act 28 of 2024 (opens in a new tab)). It was assented to on 18 July 2024.

Environmental 2 points

Climate change / global warming

  • 440 Mt of fossil CO2. South Africa emitted 440 Mt of fossil CO2 in 2024: 1.1% of the world total, 29.3% of Africa’s, and 6.9 tonnes per person against a world average of 4.7 t (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab); shares and per person: Hawkeye’s arithmetic with UN WPP 2024 population). Only Libya is higher per person in Africa. The national targets are on the GAP page.

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Africa’s largest emitter

Water and food security

  • 41% non-revenue water. Non-revenue water levels in municipalities are estimated at an average of 41%, which is unacceptably high. As a result, municipalities are losing some R 9.9 billion of potential revenue per year. (National Water and Sanitation Master Plan, 2019 (opens in a new tab), Vol. 1). This is the 2019 estimate; a newer figure has not been confirmed at source yet.

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Municipal water losses

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
