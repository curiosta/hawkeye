T

Threats

External · harmful

Carbon border taxes on exports

  • High carbon intensity, low carbon price. The Reserve Bank writes: South Africa’s carbon intensity remains high, but its domestic carbon price is low compared to its major trading partners in advanced economies. This exposes the country’s exports to climate-related trade restrictions such as the European Union’s carbon border adjustment mechanism (EU CBAM). (SARB Occasional Bulletin, April 2024 (opens in a new tab), p. 3).

Losses in the eastern DRC mission

  • A 2,900-strong deployment. In February 2024 the government reported that South Africa is to deploy at least 2 900 members of the South African National Defence Force to assist in the fight against illegal armed groups in the eastern Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC). The SADC Mission in the Democratic Republic of Congo (SAMIDRC) was deployed on 15 December 2023 (SAnews, 13 February 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Fourteen killed. 14 troop members lost their lives in fighting from 23 to 27 January 2025 (SAnews, 4 May 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Withdrawal under way, not complete. SADC states: As previously announced on 01 May 2025, the official withdrawal of SAMIDRC commenced on 29 April 2025. (SADC, 12 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)). On 26 June 2025 the government said: About 1 718 SANDF troops have now arrived in the country, and more are expected to arrive over the next few weeks. (SAnews, 26 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)). Hawkeye has not confirmed at source when the return was completed.

Weaker platinum-group metal output

  • Falling production. The U.S. Geological Survey reports that South African production of platinum-group metals fell in 2024 owing to declining prices, higher costs associated with deep-level mining, labor disputes, and ongoing disruptions to the supply of electricity. (USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 (opens in a new tab), PGM chapter).
  • Substitution. The same chapter notes: Palladium has been used as a substitute for platinum in most gasoline-engine catalytic converters (USGS, 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

Drought and extreme weather

  • The worst regional drought in a century. In Southern Africa , the signal is more pronounced. The most recent El Niño cycle brought the region’s worst drought in more than a century, leaving 61 million people in need of assistance, straining livestock, water systems and pasturelands, and pushing more than 8 million people into food insecurity. (FAO, 22 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)). The figures are for the region, not South Africa alone.
  • Recognised in law. The preamble of the Climate Change Act 22 of 2024 states: AND WHEREAS climate variability in the Republic, including the increased frequency and intensity of extreme weather events, will affect, amongst other things, human health, access to food and water, biodiversity, habitats and ecosystems, the coast and coastal infrastructure and human settlements; (Climate Change Act 22 of 2024 (opens in a new tab), assented 18 July 2024).
O

Opportunities

External · helpful

Climate finance for a just energy transition

  • US$98 billion over five years. The 2022 joint statement on the Just Energy Transition Investment Plan: It identifies $98 billion in financial requirements over 5 years to begin South Africa's 20 year energy transition. Investment will be required from both public and private sectors. The IPG is mobilising an initial $8.5 billion to catalyse the first phase of the programme. (Joint statement, 7 November 2022 (opens in a new tab)). The IPG is the International Partners Group.
  • R1.5 trillion. South Africa's JET IP sets out the scale of investment needed (ZAR 1.5 trillion) over five years for the country's Just Transition to a low-carbon and climate-resilient economy in line with its updated Nationally Determined Contribution (JET Implementation Plan 2023–2027 (opens in a new tab)).

Green hydrogen and electric vehicles

  • A hydrogen strategy for implementation. The statement on the Cabinet meeting of 18 October 2023: Cabinet approved GHCS for South Africa for implementation. The GHCS will ensure South Africa becomes a major producer and exporter of green hydrogen. (The Presidency (opens in a new tab)). GHCS is the Green Hydrogen Commercialisation Strategy; an earlier draft was approved for public comment on 30 November 2022 (the dtic (opens in a new tab)).
  • A 150% allowance for vehicle makers. Section 12V of the Income Tax Act, inserted by the Taxation Laws Amendment Act 42 of 2024 (assented 20 December 2024), allows a person that is a motor vehicle manufacturer to deduct an amount equal to 150 per cent of the cost of buildings, new machinery or improvements used mainly in the production of battery electric or hydrogen-powered vehicles in the Republic, brought into use from 1 March 2026 and before 1 March 2036 (Taxation Laws Amendment Act 42 of 2024 (opens in a new tab), s 12). The allowance is enacted law and applies to vehicle manufacturers only.

African free-trade area

  • Preferential trade from January 2024. At the launch of South Africa’s preferential trade under the African Continental Free Trade Area in Durban, the President said: The implementation of the African Continental Free Trade Area will accelerate the development of regional and local value chains, offering investors access to a population of 1.7 billion people with a fast-growing continental GDP. (Remarks of 31 January 2024 (opens in a new tab)).
W

Weaknesses

Internal · harmful

Mass unemployment and slow growth

  • 33.6% unemployed. Statistics South Africa reports an increase of 0,9 of a percentage point in the unemployment rate to 33,6% in the second quarter of 2026. During the same period, the number of employed individuals decreased by 16 000 to 16,7 million, while the number of unemployed persons grew by 345 000 to reach 8,5 million. (QLFS Q2 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Wider measures. the combined rate of unemployment and potential labour force (LU3) stood at 43,8% in the second quarter of 2026. The composite measure of labour underutilisation remained unchanged at 46,3% compared with Q1: 2026 (QLFS Q2 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Growth under 1%. The annual increase in real GDP of 0,6% in 2024 was primarily led by higher economic activities in finance, real estate and business services; growth was 0.7% in 2023 (Stats SA, GDP Q4 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

Also in GAP · Unemployment · GAP · Growth

Poverty and inequality

  • A Gini index of 0.54. The World Bank’s Poverty and Inequality Platform gives a Gini index of 0.5405 for the 2022 survey (consumption basis); the poorest 40% received about 10.5% of consumption (Hawkeye’s sum of deciles 1–4) (World Bank PIP (opens in a new tab)).
  • Over a third below the lower-bound line. Statistics South Africa: the lower-bound poverty line (LBPL) – set at R1 300 per person per month (in 2023 prices) – fell to 37,9% in 2023, and the food poverty headcount fell to 17,6% in 2023. This indicates that approximately 10,8 million people in South Africa remain food poor (Stats SA, 11 December 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

Also in GAP · Income poverty · GAP · Inequality

Municipal water losses

  • 41% non-revenue water. Non-revenue water levels in municipalities are estimated at an average of 41%, which is unacceptably high. As a result, municipalities are losing some R 9.9 billion of potential revenue per year. (National Water and Sanitation Master Plan, 2019 (opens in a new tab), Vol. 1). This is the 2019 estimate; a newer figure has not been confirmed at source yet.

Also in GAP · Non-revenue water

Coal-dependent electricity

  • 81.5% from coal. Coal supplied 81.5% of the 202,890 GWh delivered by Eskom and independent power producers in the year to March 2026, and made up 71.0% of their 55,943 MW nominal capacity (Eskom Integrated Report 2026 (opens in a new tab), p. 8). Eskom’s coal-fired stations generated 165,431 GWh in that year.

Also in GAP · Power plant availability

Africa’s largest emitter

  • 440 Mt of fossil CO2. South Africa emitted 440 Mt of fossil CO2 in 2024: 1.1% of the world total, 29.3% of Africa’s, and 6.9 tonnes per person against a world average of 4.7 t (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab); shares and per person: Hawkeye’s arithmetic with UN WPP 2024 population). Only Libya is higher per person in Africa. The national targets are on the GAP page.

Also in GAP · NDC 2030

S

Strengths

Internal · helpful

The world’s leading platinum producer

  • About 71% of world platinum. The USGS describes South Africa as the world’s leading producer of PGM-containing mined material, and estimates 2024 platinum mine production at 120,000 kg of a world 170,000 kg, and palladium at 72,000 kg of 190,000 kg (USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Most of the world’s reserves. South Africa’s platinum-group metal reserves are 63,000,000 kg, of a world total above 81,000,000 kg, about 78% (Hawkeye’s arithmetic) (USGS, 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

A resilient financial system

  • Services uninterrupted. The Reserve Bank’s assessment: Overall, the South African financial system remains resilient as evidenced by financial institutions’ ability to provide financial services uninterruptedly (SARB Financial Stability Review, First Edition 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

An open electricity market in law

  • Competitive trading. The Electricity Regulation Amendment Act 38 of 2024 provides for an open market platform that allows for competitive electricity trading (Electricity Regulation Amendment Act 38 of 2024 (opens in a new tab), assented 16 August 2024).

Radio astronomy: MeerKAT

  • MeerKAT. The MeerKAT telescope is an array of 64 interlinked receptors. It is a precursor to the Square Kilometre Array (SKA) telescope and will be integrated into the mid-frequency component of SKA Phase 1. (SARAO, About MeerKAT (opens in a new tab)).
