Hawkeye analysis

Strategy cells on this page are Hawkeye’s own analysis. Each recommendation pairs a verified strength or weakness with a verified opportunity or threat, and compares it with a documented case from another place. The precedent facts are sourced. The pairing, the transfer and the recommendation are Hawkeye’s work, not official policy and not advice to any person or firm.

The documented responses listed lower on this page remain official programmes or instruments, each confirmed at their official source. Several of those responses already work in the same area as a recommendation. A recommendation is a pairing and a lesson from elsewhere. It is not a claim that Egypt has no policy in that area. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.

The four cells

  • SO Responses that use strengths to pursue opportunities

    • Use large-scale solar, interconnectors and European finance to raise the renewable share of power

      Pairs SWOT · Strengths · Large-scale solar · SWOT · Opportunities · Renewables and interconnectors

      Use the Benban solar park, new interconnectors, and European and Gulf finance, to raise the renewable share of electricity. Official Egyptian programmes already include the NWFE energy pillar and the National Climate Change Strategy 2050. This recommendation pairs existing solar scale with the live renewables and interconnector opportunity.

      Analogous case. Portugal, a smaller economy, raised the renewable share of electricity production while cutting energy import dependence. The Directorate-General for Energy and Geology (DGEG) states: A dependência energética em 2024 foi de 64,3%, tendo descido 2,4 p.p. face a 2023. Energy dependence in 2024 was 64.3%, 2.4 percentage points lower than in 2023. O contributo das fontes de energia renovável (%FER) no consumo final bruto de energia, em 2024, foi de 36,5%, sendo o contributo das fontes de energia renovável na produção de eletricidade (%FER-E), de 65,8%. The renewable share of electricity production was 65.8% in 2024. (DGEG, Balanço Energético Nacional 2024 (opens in a new tab), published 31 October 2025).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. Egypt’s 2023 NDC lists Benban Solar Park at 1,465 MW, and the NWFE energy pillar aims to retire 5 GW of inefficient fossil-fuel capacity and add at least 10 GW of solar and wind (live SWOT). Portugal cut energy dependence and raised the renewable share of electricity to 65.8% in 2024. The analogy is limited because Portugal is a smaller European economy with a different resource mix, and because Egypt’s live weakness is still a fossil-heavy power system (12.2% renewable electricity in 2021). Interconnectors can support trade in power, but they do not by themselves add generation.

  • WO Responses that use opportunities to reduce weaknesses

    • Use transition finance to cut the fossil share of electricity

      Pairs SWOT · Weaknesses · Fossil-heavy power · SWOT · Opportunities · European finance and Gulf investment

      Use European finance and Gulf investment to cut the fossil share of electricity. Official Egyptian programmes already include the NWFE energy pillar, which aims to retire 5 GW of inefficient fossil-fuel capacity. This recommendation pairs the fossil-heavy power weakness with that financing opportunity.

      Analogous case. The UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero records that The last UK coal-fired power station closed in England in September 2024. In the same official document: In September 2024, Ratcliffe-on-Soar, the last coal-fired power station in the UK, closed. UK fossil fuel generation decreased by 16 per cent compared to 2023. Coal-fired generation’s share of UK electricity was only 0.7 per cent in 2024, a record low. The low carbon share of total UK generation stood at its highest value on the time series at 64.7 per cent. Renewables’ share rose 3.9 percentage points to 50.4 per cent. (DESNZ, Electricity generation and supply, 2020 to 2024 (opens in a new tab)). Energy Trends December 2024 confirms: Ratcliffe-on-Soar closed at the end of the third quarter on 30 September 2024. (DESNZ, Energy Trends December 2024 (opens in a new tab)).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. Renewable sources produced 12.2% of Egypt’s electricity in 2021, and a EUR 7.4 billion EU package for 2024-2027 is a live opportunity (live SWOT). The United Kingdom closed its last coal-fired station and cut coal’s share of electricity to 0.7% in 2024. The analogy is limited because the UK started from a different plant fleet, fuel mix and income level, and because Egypt’s latest World Bank series year in the live SWOT is 2021. The EUR 7.4 billion package also includes macro-financial assistance, not only power-plant finance. A finance package does not by itself retire fossil capacity on time.

  • WT Responses that reduce weaknesses and limit threats

    • Reuse treated wastewater to ease scarce Nile-dependent water and wheat import risk

      Pairs SWOT · Weaknesses · Scarce water from outside · SWOT · Threats · Nile water and imported wheat

      Reuse treated wastewater for irrigation, so that scarce Nile-dependent water goes further, and so that drought or upstream water disputes do less harm to food supply. Official Egyptian programmes already include the Water Resources and Irrigation Law and the NWFE water and food pillars. This recommendation pairs the water-scarcity weakness with the Nile-water and imported-wheat threat.

      Analogous case. Israel combined regulation, reuse and irrigation reform in a water-stressed climate. A 2023 report by the United States Environmental Protection Agency, after a visit hosted with the Israel Water Authority and other Israeli ministries, states: After drastic infrastructure, regulatory, and institutional reform, Israel transformed from one of the world’s most water-stressed countries to a water secure country, becoming a global leader in agricultural water reuse. Today, nearly 90 percent of Israel’s treated wastewater is reused for irrigation purposes. (US EPA EPA-822-S-23-001, March 2023 (opens in a new tab), Background/Purpose).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. Annual freshwater resources available per person fell from 1,972 cubic metres in 1970 to 570 cubic metres in 2018, and Egypt depends on the Nile for 97% of its water (live SWOT). Israel documents a national reuse result of nearly 90% of treated wastewater for irrigation. The analogy is limited because Israel is a smaller coastal country, and because large desalination plants are central to its drinking-water supply. Reuse does not create a new Nile allocation, and it does not stop sea-level rise in the low-lying, food-producing delta, which is a separate live threat.

Documented national responses 7

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Egypt Vision 2030 (updated edition)

    Adopted by Ministry of Planning, Economic Development and International Cooperation

    The update sets out Egypt’s Vision 2030’s Six Strategic Goals, which embody the state’s aspirations for realizing sustainable development. (Egypt Vision 2030, updated edition (opens in a new tab), p. 7). They are listed on the SOAR page.

  • National Climate Change Strategy 2050

    Adopted by Government of Egypt

    The 2050 Strategy sets forth five objectives to guide climate action, namely: achieving low-emission sustainable economic growth, strengthening the country's adaptive capacity and resilience to climate change, enhancing climate change action governance, improving climate financing infrastructure and supporting scientific research and technology. (Ministry of International Cooperation, NWFE (opens in a new tab))

    Related SWOT · Threats · A low-lying, food-producing delta

  • NWFE country platform (water, food and energy)

    Adopted by Government of Egypt, with international partners, launched July 2022

    Egypt's Country Platform, launched in July 2022, for the Nexus of Water, Food and Energy (NWFE) Program aims, in the ministry’s words, to accelerate the national climate agenda. (Ministry of International Cooperation, NWFE (opens in a new tab)) Its energy pillar is quoted in the SWOT Opportunities.

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Renewables and interconnectors

  • Nationally Determined Contribution, second update

    Adopted by Government of Egypt, submitted to the UNFCCC in June 2023

    Its power-sector and renewables targets are quoted on the GAP page (NDC (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · Poverty and women’s work · GAP · Haya Karima (Decent Life): the first phase

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every documented national response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Those responses are not ranked. Strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice.
