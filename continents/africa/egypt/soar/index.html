A Aspirations

Egypt’s long-term goals are set out in Egypt Vision 2030, updated by the Ministry of Planning. The Aspirations below are quoted from it.

The vision for 2030

Egypt Vision 2030, updated edition, a national strategy, not a law

By 2030, Egypt aims at having a competitive, balanced, and diversified economy, based on knowledge and innovation; to be built on a just, inclusive, and participatory society; with a sustainable and diverse ecosystem. This will pave the way forward towards achieving sustainable development and improving the quality of life for Egyptians, without compromising the rights of future generations. (Egypt Vision 2030, updated edition (opens in a new tab), p. 5)

Six strategic goals

Egypt Vision 2030, updated edition

The six strategic goals, by their titles (Egypt Vision 2030, updated edition (opens in a new tab), pp. 7–8):

  1. Improve Egyptians’ Quality of Life and Raise their Living Standards
  2. Social Justice and Equality
  3. Integrated and Sustainable Environmental System
  4. Diversified, Knowledge-based, and Competitive Economy
  5. Well-Developed Infrastructure
  6. Governance and Partnerships

The document says the six goals unfold into 32 general goals.

Human-centred development

Egypt Vision 2030, updated edition, guiding principles

Anchored in the principle of “Human-Centered Development”, the GoE commits to improving lives of the Egyptian citizens through the application of the principles of “Equity and Accessibility” to achieve sustainable development characterized by “Resilience and Adaptation” to international and local exigencies. (Egypt Vision 2030, updated edition (opens in a new tab), p. 52)

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
