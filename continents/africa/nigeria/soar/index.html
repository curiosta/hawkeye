A Aspirations

Nigeria sets its long-term goals in Nigeria Agenda 2050 and its medium-term goals in five-year plans. The plan for 2021 to 2025 has ended. Its successor, the Renewed Hope Plan for 2026 to 2030, was being prepared when this page was checked. The Aspirations below quote these texts, each labelled with its status.

A dynamic, industrialized and knowledge-based economy

Vision of Nigeria Agenda 2050, approved in 2020

The vision: Nigeria will be a dynamic, industrialized and knowledge-based economy that generates inclusive and sustainable development. (Nigeria Agenda 2050 (opens in a new tab)). Among its strategic objectives:

  1. Achieve meaningful economic diversification
  2. Achieve and sustain an average of 7% economic growth rate during the plan period
  3. Effectively deal with social problems such as poverty, unemployment, inequality etc

The Renewed Hope Plan, 2026 to 2030

Being prepared. Framework unveiled at the National Economic Council. Final approval not confirmed at an official source (TBC)

The National Economic Council was told that the plan will ensure policy continuity, consolidate ongoing reforms, and respond to emerging socio-economic challenges (State House, National Economic Council (opens in a new tab)). The President said it is evidence-based, realistic, and anchored on inclusive, balanced, and environmentally sustainable growth (State House, 9 February 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Net-zero emissions

Climate Change Act, 2021

The Act sets out setting a target for year 2050 - 2070 for the attainment of a netzero GHG emission (Climate Change Act, 2021 (opens in a new tab)).

How this SOAR analysis works

For a country, the Aspirations are its official goal statements, quoted word for word. Where a country has no single national vision document, the page names the official documents it quotes instead.

SOAR is built from other pages: Strengths and Opportunities link to the SWOT items, and Results are the matching rows of the GAP analysis. Only the Aspirations are new, and they are always quotations.

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
