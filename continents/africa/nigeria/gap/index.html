Targets and latest values

Average growth of 7% a year

Target
One strategic objective of Agenda 2050 is to Achieve and sustain an average of 7% economic growth rate during the plan period (Nigeria Agenda 2050 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
3.89% in the first quarter of 2026, year on year (Central Bank of Nigeria, 22 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
4.43% in the second quarter of 2026, year on year (Central Bank of Nigeria, 22 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
2.57 percentage points below 7% (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in these sources. The central bank expects growth to remain resilient for the rest of 2026 (Central Bank of Nigeria, 22 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Income of US$33,328 per person by 2050

Target
Per capita annual income is projected to rise to about US$ 33,328. This is Agenda 2050’s figure for the end of the plan (Nigeria Agenda 2050 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
Not given in this row (TBC).
Latest
GDP per person of US$1,223 in 2025 (IMF World Economic Outlook, October 2026 (opens in a new tab)). GDP and income per person are close but not the same measure.
Distance
About 27 times the 2025 level (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in these sources.

100 million people out of poverty by 2030

Target
Agenda 2050 refers to government efforts to lift 100 million Nigerians out of poverty by 2030 (Nigeria Agenda 2050 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
40.1% of people below the national monetary poverty line in 2018 and 2019 (NBS, 17 November 2022 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
133 million people, 63%, multidimensionally poor in the 2022 survey (NBS, 17 November 2022 (opens in a new tab)). No newer national poverty count has been checked (TBC).
Distance
Not calculable from these figures (TBC).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in these sources.

Electricity for everyone by 2030

Target
By 2030, ensure universal access to affordable, reliable and modern energy services (UN global indicator framework (opens in a new tab), target 7.1).
Baseline
Not given in this row (TBC).
Latest
87 million people without electricity in 2024, the largest number of any country (Tracking SDG 7, 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
87 million people still to connect by 2030.
Assessment by the tracking agency
For Sub-Saharan Africa as a whole, the pace of electrification must triple to achieve universal access by 2030 (Tracking SDG 7 press release, 24 June 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Net-zero emissions between 2050 and 2070

Target
The Climate Change Act, 2021 sets out setting a target for year 2050 - 2070 for the attainment of a netzero GHG emission (Climate Change Act, 2021 (opens in a new tab)). Agenda 2050 refers to net zero by 2060 (Nigeria Agenda 2050 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
Not given in this row (TBC).
Latest
136 million tonnes of fossil CO2 in 2024. This leaves out other gases and land use (Global Carbon Budget 2025 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
Not calculable from fossil CO2 alone (TBC).
Assessment by the tracking agency
None published in these sources.

Public debt below 60% of GDP

Target
The central bank refers to the national debt ceiling of 60 per cent of GDP (CBN Economic Report, Q2 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Baseline
30.36% of GDP at the end of March 2026 (CBN Economic Report, Q2 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Latest
31.78% of GDP at the end of June 2026 (CBN Economic Report, Q2 2026 (opens in a new tab)).
Distance
Within the ceiling, with 28.22 percentage points to spare (Hawkeye’s arithmetic).
Assessment by the tracking agency
The central bank says the debt-to-GDP ratio is below the ceiling, suggesting continued debt sustainability (CBN Economic Report, Q2 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Sources checked 12 November 2026.

How this GAP analysis works

For a country, the targets are those set in its own laws and official commitments, plus the global Sustainable Development Goal targets that apply to it.

Each row quotes the target word for word and gives the latest official value with its year. A distance is worked out only where the target and the latest value measure the same thing. An “on track” or “off track” label appears only where the agency that tracks the target publishes one. Hawkeye does not assign its own.
