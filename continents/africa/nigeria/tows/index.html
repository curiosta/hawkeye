Hawkeye analysis

Strategy cells on this page are Hawkeye’s own analysis. Each recommendation pairs a verified strength or weakness with a verified opportunity or threat, and compares it with a documented case from another place. The precedent facts are sourced. The pairing, the transfer and the recommendation are Hawkeye’s work, not official policy and not advice to any person or firm.

The documented responses listed lower on this page remain official programmes or instruments, each confirmed at their official source. Several of those responses already work in the same area as a recommendation. A recommendation is a pairing and a lesson from elsewhere. It is not a claim that Nigeria has no policy in that area. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.

  • WO Responses that use opportunities to reduce weaknesses

    • Scale off-grid solar to cut the world’s largest electricity-access gap

      Pairs SWOT · Weaknesses · The largest number of people without electricity · SWOT · Opportunities · Off-grid solar and gas for transport

      Scale official off-grid solar, already funded through the DARES project, so that fewer of the 87 million people without electricity remain unconnected. Official Nigerian programmes already include DARES and the Climate Change Act. This recommendation pairs the access weakness with the solar part of that opportunity. It does not recommend gas for transport as a substitute for electricity access.

      Analogous case. Kenya’s official energy compact records a national result: Kenya has made significant strides in expanding electricity access, with national access levels rising from approximately 30% in 2014 to over 75% (65% on grid, 0.84% mini grid and 9.2% Standalone PV) as at 2024. This progress has largely been complemented by a strong focus on renewable energy development, which now accounts for 82% of the country’s total installed electricity generation capacity and 93% of actual energy consumption. (Kenya National Energy Compact 2025–2030 (opens in a new tab), figures as at 2024).

      Why it transfers, and the limit. Nigeria had 87 million people without electricity in the live comparison, the largest such total in the world, and the DARES project allocated USD 300 million for off-grid solar (live SWOT). Kenya raised national access from about 30% in 2014 to over 75% in 2024, including mini-grids and standalone solar. The analogy is limited because a large leftover in Kenya still lacked access in 2024, and because Nigeria’s gap is larger. USD 300 million does not close an 87 million person gap on its own. Access is not the same as generation from rising oil output, which is a separate live strength and is not used in this pairing.

Documented national responses 5

Listed in no particular order. None is ranked.

  • Resetting the policy rate

    Adopted by Central Bank of Nigeria, Monetary Policy Committee (22 September 2026)

    The reset aims at strengthening monetary policy transmission and reinforcing the primacy of the monetary policy rate (Central Bank of Nigeria, 22 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Easing inflation and lower interest rates

  • Fiscal and monetary coordination

    Adopted by Federal Ministry of Finance and Central Bank of Nigeria

    The two signed a Memorandum of Understanding on fiscal-monetary coordination. The central bank says it will give a structured framework to strengthen policy harmonisation towards the achievement of low and stable inflation (Central Bank of Nigeria, 22 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Easing inflation and lower interest rates · SWOT · Weaknesses · A low income per person

  • Off-grid solar (DARES)

    Adopted by Distributed Access through Renewable Energy Scale-up project

    The project has allocated US$300 million for off-grid solar (Tracking SDG 7, 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Weaknesses · The largest number of people without electricity · SWOT · Opportunities · Off-grid solar and gas for transport

  • Gas-powered public transport

    Adopted by Presidential Initiative on National Affordable CNG Transit Programme

    The central bank expects it to lower transport costs and help bring inflation down (Central Bank of Nigeria, 22 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Opportunities · Off-grid solar and gas for transport

  • The Climate Change Act

    Adopted by National Assembly, 2021

    Its aims include identifying risks and vulnerabilities, building resilience and strengthening existing adaptive capacities to the impacts of climate change (Climate Change Act, 2021 (opens in a new tab)).

    Related SWOT · Threats · Flash floods

How this TOWS matrix works

For a country, the responses are documented national laws, decrees, plans and programmes, each named with the body that adopted it.

Every documented national response is attributed to the body behind it and links to its official text. Those responses are not ranked. Strategy cells, where filled, are Hawkeye’s own analysis, based on analogous cases. They are not official policy and not advice.
