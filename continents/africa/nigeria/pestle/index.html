Nigeria PESTLE

Political 1 point

Governance and cooperation

  • General elections in January 2027. INEC’s calendar sets the presidential and National Assembly election for 16 January 2027, and governorship and state assembly elections for 6 February 2027 (INEC election calendar (opens in a new tab)). The central bank sees election-related spendings as a possible risk to prices (Central Bank of Nigeria, 22 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Economic 2 points

Economy and finance

  • Rate reset and falling inflation. The policy rate was reset to 23% on 22 September 2026. Headline inflation was 15.39% in August 2026, food inflation 19.57% and core inflation 13.29% (Central Bank of Nigeria, 22 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Opportunities · Easing inflation and lower interest rates

Economy and finance

  • A steadier naira. The naira exchanged at an average of ₦1,365.95/US$ in Q22026, compared with ₦1,384.48/US$ in Q12026 (CBN Economic Report, Q2 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Growth, reserves and a surplus

Social 1 point

Human development

  • Poverty is higher in rural areas. Multidimensional poverty is higher in rural areas, where 72% of people are poor, compared to 42% of people in urban areas. (NBS, 17 November 2022 (opens in a new tab))

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Poverty and short lives

Technological 1 point

Technology and AI

  • Technology leads growth. Information and communications technology led the growth of the non-oil economy in the second quarter of 2026 (Central Bank of Nigeria, 22 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Information technology drives growth

Legal 1 point

Climate change / global warming

  • A climate law. The Climate Change Act, 2021 includes the aim of setting a target for year 2050 - 2070 for the attainment of a netzero GHG emission, in line with Nigeria's international climate change obligations (Climate Change Act, 2021 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page GAP · Net-zero emissions between 2050 and 2070

Environmental 1 point

Natural and cosmic hazards

  • Flash floods. The Mokwa flood of 28 May 2025 killed 159 people, NEMA reported, with more than 98 missing (NEMA, 2 June 2025 (opens in a new tab)).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Threats · Flash floods

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a country, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups: politics and government, the economy, society and population, technology, laws, and the environment. Official national sources come first.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact. Spanish quotations are given as published, with unofficial English translations.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
