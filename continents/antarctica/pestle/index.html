Antarctica PESTLE

The large outside forces acting on a place, in six groups: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal and Environmental.

  • SWOT
  • PESTLE
  • GAP

Antarctica has no permanent population, government or national economy. Here the Economic group covers fishing (CCAMLR data) and the Social group covers tourism (IAATO industry data).

Political 3 points

Governance and cooperation

  • Claims frozen. No new claim, or enlargement of an existing claim, to territorial sovereignty in Antarctica shall be asserted while the present treaty is in force. (Antarctic Treaty (opens in a new tab), Article IV(2))
  • Decisions need every entitled party. The measures referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall become effective when approved by all the Contracting Parties whose representatives were entitled to participate in the meetings held to consider those measures. (Antarctic Treaty (opens in a new tab), Article IX(4))
  • Who takes decisions. Of 58 Treaty Parties, 29 are Consultative Parties: The other 29 Non-Consultative Parties are invited to attend the Consultative Meetings but do not participate in the decision-making. (Antarctic Treaty Secretariat, Parties (opens in a new tab))

Economic 3 points

Oceans

  • Record krill catch, 2025. 624,918 tonnes of Antarctic krill were caught in Area 48 in the 2025 season, above the 620,000-tonne trigger level (CCAMLR Fishery Report 2025 (opens in a new tab), section 2.1; SC-CAMLR-44 (opens in a new tab), paragraph 2.13).
  • Fishery closed on 1 August 2025. Catches were taken faster than in any previous season with the result that the trigger level was reached and the fishery was closed on 1 August 2025. (SC-CAMLR-44 report (opens in a new tab), paragraph 2.18)
  • Who fishes. In the period 2014-2024, six Members have fished for krill, 63.8% of the total catch has been taken by Norway, 18% by China and 9.8% by the Republic of Korea. and The increase in catches since 2010 is largely driven by Norway. (CCAMLR Fishery Report 2024 (opens in a new tab), section 1.1)

Social 3 points

Society and mindset

  • 118,491 visitors in 2024–25 (industry data). The total number of visitors (Vessel and Deep Field) in the 2024-25 season was 118,491. (IAATO report to ATCM 47 (opens in a new tab))
  • About 5% fewer than 2023–24. the 2024-25 passenger levels are approximately 5% lower than the 2023-24 season. This can primarily be attributed to one cruise-only operator not operating during the 2024-25 season. The number of landed passengers remained virtually the same between the 2023-24 and 2024-25 seasons. (IAATO, 2025 (opens in a new tab))
  • 2025–26 at a glance. IAATO lists 85,195 landed visits, 27,217 cruise-only visitors and 1,106 deep-field visitors for IAATO operators (IAATO, Data & Statistics (opens in a new tab)). IAATO gives the three separately; they are not added up here.

Technological 3 points

Governance and cooperation

  • Results exchanged freely. scientific observations and results from Antarctica shall be exchanged and made freely available. (Antarctic Treaty (opens in a new tab), Article III(1)(c), which applies to the greatest extent feasible and practicable)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Claims frozen and science shared

Climate change / global warming

  • Ice mass measured from space. These images, created from GRACE and GRACE-FO data, show changes in Antarctic ice mass since 2002. (NASA, 2023 (opens in a new tab))
  • Sea ice tracked by satellite. NSIDC places the 2026 minimum in the 48-year satellite record (NSIDC, 2026 (opens in a new tab)).

Legal 3 points

Natural resources

  • Mining banned. Any activity relating to mineral resources, other than scientific research, shall be prohibited. (Environmental Protocol (opens in a new tab), Article 7)

On the SWOT page SWOT · Strengths · Mining banned except for science

Governance and cooperation

  • Liability annex not in force. Entry into force: not yet in force (depositary status list, 6 October 2025 (opens in a new tab)). 19 of the 28 Consultative Parties whose approval is needed have approved (Hawkeye’s count; see Weaknesses).
  • Protocol membership. 42 of the 58 Treaty Parties are Parties to the Environmental Protocol (Hawkeye’s count from the Antarctic Treaty Secretariat list (opens in a new tab); see GAP).

On the SWOT page SWOT · Weaknesses · Liability annex not in force; the all-party approval rule

Environmental 3 points

Climate change / global warming

  • Ice loss. About 150 billion tonnes of ice a year, 2002–2023, with the loss concentrated on the West Antarctic Ice Sheet (NASA, 2023 (opens in a new tab)).
  • Record-low sea ice, February 2023. 1.79 million km² on 21 February 2023, the lowest in the 45-year satellite record (NSIDC, 2023 (opens in a new tab), preliminary).
  • Near-average minimum in 2026. 2.58 million km² on 26 February 2026, sixteenth lowest in the satellite record (NSIDC, 2026 (opens in a new tab), preliminary).

How this PESTLE analysis works

For a continent, PESTLE covers the large forces acting on it in six groups. Where a continent has no permanent population, government or national economy, as with Antarctica, the groups follow what its governing treaties and official bodies report on.

Each factor has up to five sourced points; where fewer have been confirmed at source, fewer are shown. Points link to the SWOT item that covers the same fact.

  • P

    Political

    Geopolitics, conflict and cooperation

  • E

    Economic

    Growth, debt, trade and inequality

  • S

    Social

    Population, health, education and society

  • T

    Technological

    Technology, AI, energy and digital access

  • L

    Legal

    International law and treaties

  • E

    Environmental

    Climate, biodiversity, oceans, land and water

Reporting, not recommending. Hawkeye reports; it never recommends. Goals, aspirations and strategies appear only as stated by official or authoritative bodies, quoted or attributed, with a link. Nothing here is Hawkeye’s own view. Every figure links to its source and gives the year it refers to.
